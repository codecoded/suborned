# The Suborned — How to Play

**macOS demo · Version 0.2.336 · Single player**

Play: https://codecoded.itch.io/the-suborned

## Install and start

1. Download **the-suborned-mac.zip — Version 0.2.336** from the itch page.
2. Unzip it and move **The Suborned.app** to Applications.
3. Open the app, read the mission briefing, and select **Begin**.

This download is for Apple silicon Macs. The app is signed and notarised. You do not need Unity or any development tools. A mouse and keyboard are recommended.

## Your mission

Councillor Popovic has a file on your cell. At the end of his shift, he will leave the Civic Records Office carrying it. **Take him alive and get him away in a car.**

You command four agents in a city that keeps moving: people follow routines, cameras watch the streets, and another crew wants the same target. Learn what people need, persuade them to help, and coordinate your agents before the opportunity closes.

## Your four agents

| Agent | Main role |
| --- | --- |
| **Scout** | Watch people and security, listen for useful information, and mislead the rival lookout. |
| **Fixer** | Turn people into allies and ask them to help your plan. |
| **Tech** | Loop cameras, redirect the patrol, and drive the getaway car. |
| **Enforcer** | Take Popovic and carry him to the car. Only the Enforcer can carry him. |

Select an agent by clicking their portrait or pressing **1–4**. Press **5** to select all four.

## Essential controls

These are the default controls in the overhead commander view. Settings can change the click scheme and key bindings.

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move selected agents | Left-click the ground |
| Run to a point | Double-click the ground |
| See actions for a person or object | Left-click it, then choose from the action ring |
| Use the suggested nonviolent action | Hold the left button until the indicator fills, then release |
| Pan the camera | Left-drag, middle-drag, or use WASD / arrow keys |
| Zoom | Scroll wheel or trackpad scroll |
| Rotate the overhead view | Q / E |
| Pause or resume the mission clock | Space, or the on-screen pause control |
| Open the Book | J, or the Book button |
| Open the pause menu | Esc |

Read an action's cost and requirements before choosing it. Hover over a price to see more detail. If an action is unavailable, its explanation tells you what is missing.

**Combat:** hold the right mouse button over a valid target until the fire indicator fills, then release to give a continuing fire order. Press **X** or click **Stop** to stop firing. During a fight, a right-click on a hostile focuses fire. Keep Popovic alive.

**Optional ground view:** press **T** to switch to the selected agent's third-person view and back. Use WASD to move and **E** for the prompted interaction. The controls above describe the overhead view, which is a good place to learn the mission.

## A first attempt

1. **Read the briefing.** It introduces the location, your contacts, and the dangers. Check the mission clock and deadlines.
2. **Pause and look around.** Find your car, Inez's café, the Office entrance, and Popovic's pickup. You can give orders while paused; with the default settings, they start when you resume.
3. **Gather information.** Use the Scout to Watch the square's cameras and learn about people before approaching them. Listen for needs that the Fixer can address.
4. **Choose a way in.** Inez can help with the front-door route; Popovic's driver offers another opportunity at the pickup. Use the Book's **Job** page to check the conditions for each route.
5. **Coordinate the take.** Get security out of the way or stop it seeing the critical action, and position the Enforcer in time. Watch for rival interference.
6. **Finish the escape.** Taking Popovic is only part of the job. Get him into a car and use its **Go** action. Read any message explaining why departure is blocked, and account for agents left behind.

## Read the situation

- **Book → Job:** your objective, route requirements, deadlines, and ways the mission can fail.
- **Book → People:** what you have learned about your contacts.
- **Book → Journal:** a record of discoveries and events.
- **Trail:** evidence left by your actions. A quieter approach can leave less behind.
- **Alert:** the current security response. Noise and witnessed acts can bring more trouble.

An offer can fail if you have not learned enough about the person. Check what changed after each action instead of repeating the same approach. Guns can solve an immediate problem while creating witnesses, police attention, and danger to your target.

If the attempt goes wrong, press **Esc** and choose **Restart mission**. Try a different contact, order of actions, or timing. Use **Quit** in the pause menu to exit.
