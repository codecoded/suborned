# Objective · stops and stations (Vantage, brief 001 output 2)
Place tram stops and sky rail stations on the lines Datum proposed (the candidate plan is in the task). Positions on the lines, platforms, entrances fronting public space, interchange pairs; every area in reach of one.

You return STOPS: every stop or station of every line you list (the list replaces that line's stops). `path` names one of the line's paths; `at` must lie on it; `fronts` names the road or foot path whose public space the entrance opens onto. Give sky rail stations a `length`. Mark an interchange as `"<line>/<stop id>"` on both members of the pair.

Plan checker rules your stops are judged by:
- T-S1: tram stop spacing and S-S2: sky rail station spacing, measured along the line's ordered paths (`standards.tram.stopSpacing`, `standards.skyrail.stationSpacing`).
- T-S2/S-S1: platforms on straight track or beam.
- N-P1: a stop sits on a path of its own line (within `standards.transit.onLine`). N-P2: no stop on a plot.
- N-R1: every plot centroid within `standards.transit.reach` of a stop or station (the worst plot is reported).
- S-I1/N-C1: interchange partners within `standards.skyrail.interchange`; the network of lines is one connected whole.
Do not move lines; if a line cannot satisfy a rule, say so in `notes` for Datum.

Harbour District plan › notes › why
- Tram: one track (x −2.5) in a 16 m street from z −40, ending 6 m short of the circus. No loop, no crossover, no second track, one stop island; the line starts and ends nowhere.
- Sky rail: a closed loop 24 m up the west promenade, straight over the tram street, round the Sphere station at r 88, then diagonally back across the west city with pylons dropped onto whatever is underneath.
- Harbour line: a second closed loop on the quay with a balloon round the skyport (R 48). It shares no station with the sky rail or the tram.
- Roads: the street kit is one straight street and a side street. The map graph (harbour-map.json) links the civic block to Comet Row and Sunset Way by two exit roads; nothing defines the district's road hierarchy or where those roads go.

Harbour District plan › notes › concept
- Buchanan's hierarchy: a primary ring (Sunset Way, Crown Road, Harbour Road, Comet Row) carries through traffic round the district; district distributors (the Boulevard, Market Street, Circus Road, Crown Avenue) feed it; access roads serve seven environmental areas and never link two distributors.
- Every way a car can enter an environmental area is a gate. For a mission, the gates are the seal points; the Records Precinct has two.
- Crown Circus is the hub (EPCOT 1966: a radial centre with transit through it). Four arms: the Boulevard south, Crown Avenue north, Circus Road west, Crown Parade (a pedestrian mall) east to the harbour.
- Tram T1 runs in the Boulevard's central reservation with stops about 300 m apart. It turns on a balloon loop round the circus island (R 28), so single-ended trams need no crossover. South of Comet Row it runs on to Southside.
- Sky rail S1 is the airport line (Tokyo 1964). One straight trunk: Skyport, Harbour, Crown Circus, Sphere Gardens, then the West End. It runs over public space only: the Parade, the circus island and Circus Road's median. Trains turn on a loop round the skyport.
- The two systems cross at right angles at Crown Circus, the one interchange (sky station to tram stop ≈ 120 m).
- The Records Axis: the Sphere Gardens sky station, Garden Walk, the café, the fountain, the raised table on Orbit Walk and the office door all sit on x = −160.
- Players cross any road anywhere. Kerbs, medians, the tram reservation and the circus are walkable ground (no guard rails); crossings and raised tables only calm traffic. Walking distances on this sheet are measured that way: round buildings and water, straight over streets.
