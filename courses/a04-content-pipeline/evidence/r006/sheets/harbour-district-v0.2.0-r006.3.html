Concept

The plan in seven moves

  1. Buchanan's hierarchy: a primary ring (Sunset Way, Crown Road, Harbour Road, Comet Row) carries through traffic round the district; district distributors (the Boulevard, Market Street, Circus Road, Crown Avenue) feed it; access roads serve seven environmental areas and never link two distributors.
  2. Every way a car can enter an environmental area is a gate. For a mission, the gates are the seal points; the Records Precinct has two.
  3. Crown Circus is the hub (EPCOT 1966: a radial centre with transit through it). Four arms: the Boulevard south, Crown Avenue north, Circus Road west, Crown Parade (a pedestrian mall) east to the harbour.
  4. Tram T1 runs in the Boulevard's central reservation with stops about 300 m apart. It turns on a balloon loop round the circus island (R 28), so single-ended trams need no crossover. South of Comet Row it runs on to Southside.
  5. Sky rail S1 is the airport line (Tokyo 1964). One straight trunk: Skyport, Harbour, Crown Circus, Sphere Gardens, then the West End. It runs over public space only: the Parade, the circus island, Circus Road's median and the Skyport Pier. Trains turn on a loop over the water round the skyport pad; the Skyport station stands on the pier's apron, the landside end.
  6. The two systems cross at right angles at Crown Circus, the one interchange (sky station to tram stop ≈ 120 m).
  7. The Records Axis: the Sphere Gardens sky station, Garden Walk, the café, the fountain, the raised table on Orbit Walk and the office door all sit on x = −160.
  8. Players cross any road anywhere. Kerbs, medians, the tram reservation and the circus are walkable ground (no guard rails); crossings and raised tables only calm traffic. Walking distances on this sheet are measured that way: round buildings and water, straight over streets.

Why re-plan: the current world

  • Tram: one track (x −2.5) in a 16 m street from z −40, ending 6 m short of the circus. No loop, no crossover, no second track, one stop island; the line starts and ends nowhere.
  • Sky rail: a closed loop 24 m up the west promenade, straight over the tram street, round the Sphere station at r 88, then diagonally back across the west city with pylons dropped onto whatever is underneath.
  • Harbour line: a second closed loop on the quay with a balloon round the skyport (R 48). It shares no station with the sky rail or the tram.
  • Roads: the street kit is one straight street and a side street. The map graph (harbour-map.json) links the civic block to Comet Row and Sunset Way by two exit roads; nothing defines the district's road hierarchy or where those roads go.

Open questions

  1. Scale: 1.1 × 1.1 km with seven environmental areas. Keep that, or plan only the Records Precinct and Lamplight for the slice and leave the rest as massing?
  2. Tram: terminate on the circus loop (as drawn), or run on up Crown Avenue to Crown Road?
  3. Sky rail: one straight trunk replaces the Sphere loop and the separate harbour line. OK to retire both?
  4. Names: Comet Row is now the south ring road (it was the Boulevard's continuation). Rocket Lane is the getaway street; Satellite Street, Starline Street and Orbit Gardens are reused. Keep them?
  5. Records Precinct: two vehicle gates (Rocket, Satellite) to seal against cars, but on foot the crew can leave across any street. Is the escape on foot meant to be open like that, with the police sealing stations rather than streets?
