What the pipeline does well

  • Game-specific. The outputs are edits to the district plan that the Unity graybox is derived from. They are judged by the same plan checker that gates every plan. The GDD rule "no AI at runtime, exact replay" holds: bun run levels:replay r003 rebuilds every round from committed data, without Docker or a model.
  • Deterministic and model critics split the work. In r003 round 1 the checker caught N-R1: rocket-west was 273 m from any stop. Keel caught what no rule covers: a Skyport station over water that nobody could walk to. In round 2 Datum added a T2 stop at Sunset Way (worst reach 247 m), Vantage removed the station and handed the missing apron to Datum without inventing geometry, and Keel passed.
  • Runs pin their world inputs. Each run records its civic block (as a copy plus its sha256) and its GDD version. When a design source changes after a run, replay labels the result as drift with its cause, instead of crashing or reporting a false failure:
    • r001: superseded: recorded against the Oct 5 block, replays at commit 3dbb9ca;
    • r002: recorded against GDD v0.14.0.
    Integrity checks still fail hard: schemas, plan = input + proposals, the checker rerun, outcome and cost.
