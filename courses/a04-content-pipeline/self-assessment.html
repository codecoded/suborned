Self-assessment · A04 content pipeline (run r001)

What the pipeline does well

  • Game-specific. It is built for this game. The outputs are edits to the district plan that the Unity graybox is derived from. They are judged by the same plan checker that gates every plan. The GDD rule "no AI at runtime, exact replay" holds: bun run levels:replay r001 rebuilds every round from committed data without Docker or a model.
  • Deterministic and model critics split the work. The checker (45 rules, including 7 new transit rules, each with a source and a failing test) catches geometry. Keel catches what the rules can't. In r001 the checker was clean in round 1 and Keel still found 3 real faults:
    • A station over water. The plot rule N-P2 doesn't cover water.
    • A journey time that added a ride which never happens.
    • An open question claiming the GDD makes boarding a fast-travel power, which it does not.
    Each fault cited chunk ids. Those chunks were pinned into the owner's next retrieval, and both owners fixed their faults in round 2.
  • Nothing invented. Pulse left boarding unpriced and raised it as an open question for Sky and Motive. Vantage refused to keep an entrance nobody could walk to and handed the gap back.
  • Everything is versioned. Every round has a version (0.2.0-r001.N) and a sheet named with that version. Card, corpus and checker-code hashes are recorded.

Retrieval tweak (applied)

standards › tram holds stopSpacing. The single query "tram stop spacing sky rail stations every area in reach of a stop interchange" ranked it 9th, outside the top 8 the agents see, because the plan's notes outranked it. Every objective now leads with a literal standards query, standards tram stopSpacing radiusRunning radiusLoop platform trackCentres. With that change it ranks 3rd for Datum, 2nd for Vantage and 5th for Pulse and Keel (evidence/ranks.json, bun run levels:ranks).

Next concrete tweaks

  1. Prompt or retrieval: in tools/levels/objectives/index.ts, replace Datum's query "Datum holds boarding as fast travel; …" with "the cell rides trams and sky rail between stops and stations". That stops the unsupported claim at its source.
  2. Routing: add an optional handoffs: [{owner, blocking, ask}] field to each builder schema. A blocking handoff becomes a fault for its owner, just like a Keel fault, so Vantage's Skyport request would have reached Datum in round 3.
  3. Checker: add a rule that every stop and station entrance has a direct walk from public space (reusing walk.ts), so a station over water fails deterministically.
