**State:** parked design intent, to design and build later. Owner: Aster (then Motive for the rules, Pulse for the response AI, Vela and Forma for the drones). Sky, 2026-10-21.

## Sky's intent
"There's a severe penalty for being on a rampage - if you're spotted killing civilians, the city defence force sends in flying drones to take out the squad (we will design and add later) but if the player gets that powerful they can take on mini-gunned armed flying drones, then so be it - but the city would be against them - they've failed to suborn the city. We can work out what that means later (bit like GTA where 5 stars means escaping police is near impossible most of the time)."

## What it implies (for when it's designed)
- An escalation ladder above today's response dial (Alert 0–3): seen killing civilians climbs it fast; at the top the **city defence force** deploys **armed flying drones** (mini-guns) against the cell.
- Escaping at the top is near impossible most of the time, but never scripted impossible: a strong enough cell can fight drones.
- The cost is campaign-level and thematic: the city turns against the cell (Standing deep in Menace, contacts refuse, the "orchestrate the city" pillar fails); the game frames it as having failed to suborn the city.
- Fits: shoot anyone (Motive's ruling in `briefs/027/motive/tuning.md` §8/§9); Standing and witnesses; Heat bands (brief 031); the response dial.
- World test: drones are clockwork-smart on fixed routines (no thinking machines), 1950s style with centuries-ahead tech.
