## Real-time tactics
- Shadow Tactics, OPENED: https://www.gamepressure.com/shadowtactics/interface-and-key-shortcuts/z1941c . LMB moves (double-click runs) and picks the spot for an action; RMB cancels an action before it starts, cancels moves, selects; RMB on an enemy shows its field of view; A S D F G pick an ability (shared by all); Shift = shadow mode, Enter = execute; "actions already in progress can't be canceled".
- Desperados III, OPENED: https://www.gamepressure.com/desperados-iii/controls/zad05a . Skills picked with A S D F G Y then a target by cursor; double-click runs; Ctrl first = interact mode for objects and characters; Shift = Showdown (pauses time except on the top difficulty), Enter executes.
- Desperados III, OPENED: https://bit-tech.net/reviews/gaming/pc/desperados-iii-review/1/ . Default is actions on the left button, selection on the right ("the opposite approach of most strategy games"); the group moves together by default and "easy to trigger by accident"; the review leans on quickload for failures.
- Satellite Reign, OPENED: https://herringsfishbait.com/2015/09/18/review-satellite-reign/ . Right click does double duty by weapon state; a drawn weapon turns "select civilian" into "incinerate civilian"; "easy to get into the state where one agent has a weapon drawn by accident".
- Satellite Reign, SNIPPET: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=368532514 (keyboard guide; player reports of Ctrl+click = forced shot, Alt+right-click = forced action with lower success).
- Syndicate (1993), SNIPPET: https://www.hardcoregaming101.net/syndicate/ and https://forceforgood.co.uk/strategy/syndicate/ . Left click moves, right button aims and fires; reviewer notes no confirmation feedback on move orders. Not opened.

## Pie menus and wheels
- Pie menu, OPENED: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pie_menu . The Sims (2000) used pie menus extensively; direction beats distance (Fitts); menus that wait for the pointer to stop are about as unobtrusive as gestures for experts; GTA V weapon and radio wheels.
- Marking menus, SNIPPET: https://billbuxton.com/MMUserLearn.html and https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~fox/summaries/ui/marking_menus.html (about 3.5x faster than the menu in a 1994 field study of 2 users; novice to expert path; error rises with depth and breadth).
- Mass Effect 3, SNIPPET: https://masseffect.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_(Mass_Effect_3) (hold to open the power wheel, which pauses the game; powers fire when the wheel closes).
- GTA V, SNIPPET: https://gtaforums.com/topic/532181-gta-v-weapons-information/ (slow motion while the weapon wheel is open).
- Red Dead Redemption 2, SNIPPET: https://www.quartertothree.com/fp/?p=47296 (tap vs hold on one button for different acts); https://steamcommunity.com/app/1174180/discussions/0/1735507058425216928 (players: inconsistent hold or tap); https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/iHuafuI6D4LVge59s82oh/enabling-the-tap-assist-accessibility-settings-in-red-dead-redemption-2 (Tap Assist: Off, 2x, 3x, 4x, Hold); https://polycount.com/discussion/226812/press-and-hold-input-in-aaa-games-anything-to-do-with-performance (a developer: hold used in place of a confirm pop-up, a trend they dislike).

## Mis-click and double-click
- Raskin, OPENED: https://alistapart.com/article/neveruseawarning . Confirmation dialogs are clicked through by habit; louder warnings do not help; let the act happen and give undo.
- Raymond Chen, OPENED: https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20090429-00/?p=18413 . A single-click handler that must tell a double-click apart waits the double-click time (about half a second on Windows); single-clickers pay it every time.
- Microsoft, OPENED: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winuser/nf-winuser-getdoubleclicktime . The double-click time is user-set, up to 5000 ms; the page gives no default.
