## Objective
A proof of concept in the game of the job and cell pages in the light page's look, on the slot template, so a new player can answer "what do my agents do?" and "what am I trying to do?", and Sky can judge the look in a real capture.

## Done-check
- Opening the book stops the clock; closing it resumes exactly; a saved run replays to the same hash whether or not the book was opened.
- The job and cell pages are drawn from data in named slots: empty slots take no room, and a test section is added by data alone.
- Words come from existing text where it exists; new words are listed for Fable.
- A real capture holds up beside the mock on Sky's phone.
- The book gives no orders: a way on a page closes it and opens that person's ring, price first.
- No frame cost on the street while the book is shut.

## Not in this round
The people pages (they wait for the faces panel), the journal, filters and lists, the campaign's use, and a GDD change, which follows once Sky has seen a real capture.
