# 028 · Bring the testbed over

**Status (2026-10-20):** open. Asked by Sky: "as much as possible from testbed should come over to new." Owner: Aster. Follows brief 026 (render quality), where Sky compared the old 2.5D web testbed and the old city with today's Unity world and preferred the old look nearly everywhere.

## Objective

The old 2.5D web testbed (`~/Projects/the-suborned/testbed/`, art in `~/Projects/the-suborned-store/testbed-art/`) is the reference for look and assets. Port as much of it as possible into the Unity game: faithfully, as generated kit pieces, materials and world-generator rules (deterministic, hash-checked, provenance kept), never as hand-placed scene edits.

Already under way (brief 026): road (strip-light ribbons, powder-blue enamel, lozenges, seams, pearl kerb), paving (limestone, teal tile, tree pits), café lighting (night version), café sign plate, parasols and interior, street palms, the old props (hydrant, pneumatic post, twin booth, curved kiosk, automat, control cabinet), the enforcer's run (`run_lean`).

New here: the car park stand / pickup bay (testbed `boarding-pavilion*`), and a full inventory so nothing else is missed.

## Palm LODs, 2026-10-22 (Vela)
Sky: "why not do a LoD for the trees?" Report `briefs/028/palm/lod/index.html`; boards `lod/board-1-levels.png` (L0 | L1 | L2 | impostor trial at the distance each takes over), `board-2-market.png` and `board-2-market-w.png` (the real boulevard from the third-person camera, all L0 | with levels | difference), `board-3-play.png` (play zoom and close), `board-4-pop.png` (the switch), `board-5-l2-before-after.png` (the 20 Oct L2 against the new one); numbers `lod/metrics-*.json`, `lod/district.json`, `lod/street-stats.json`. **Nothing is adopted or shipped:** `FULL_DETAIL` (tools/kit/lod.ts) still holds both ids, so the catalogue and the game are unchanged. Sky approves first; then Splice (below).
- **What was wrong.** The 20 Oct levels were the generator's collapse (25 % and 6 %). L1 was fine (1,576 / 2,056 tris, silhouette within 1 to 2 % of L0). **L2 was not**: it tears the cards apart, keeping 72 % of the foliage and 30 % silhouette error (board 5). So the fix is an authored L2, and a lighter L1.
- **The levels** (`tools/decokit/palm_lod.py`, one builder: its level 0 reproduces the shipped pieces' vertices exactly, checked by `palm_lod_check.py`; every level uses the same random stream, so the same 22 fronds in the same places; the same four materials, no new texture or material):

  | level | CoconutPalm | CoconutPalm_Street | what it is |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | L0 | 6,310 | 8,230 | as built (untouched) |
  | L1 | 1,330 | 1,330 | every frond exactly as shipped (a coarser frond was the only thing that ever showed in the sweep); trunk a plain 8-sided taper (no leaf-scar rings), coconuts as octahedra, 8-sided boot |
  | L2 | 514 | 514 | the same 22 fronds as cards: 4 rows of one card each side of the rachis (the texture's leaf, cut 10 % wide so no leaflet tip is trimmed), 6-sided taper, 3 coconuts |

  Per palm L1 is 21 % / 16 % of L0 and L2 is 8 % / 6 %. The frond is the whole look (the texture carries the leaflets), so the cards keep the full, bright, feathered crown: foliage coverage 99.8 % of L0, mean colour within 0.7/255, silhouette 3.5 to 3.7 % (the floor from leaflet edges is 3 to 4 %).
- **An impostor was tried and fails** (`palm_lod_impostor.py`, board 1 last panel, `metrics-impostor-trial.json`): three crossed cards baked from L0, 6 triangles, one cutout material. It is 13 % fat (the three crowns add), 25 % silhouette error at any distance, and reads wrong until about 300 m, where L2 already holds within 4 %; its light is baked from the day sun (night and phase changes would show it); it needs a new texture, a material and a third level the pipeline does not have; and it saves about 500 triangles a palm, 0.2 M a frame at most. **Recommendation: no impostor.** If Sky wants one anyway, it is a separate piece of work.
- **Switch distances** (matched to the game; derived, not guessed). Third person is HostFollow: 55 degrees vertical, 3.1 m up, far clip 350 m, haze linear 90 to 350 m (haze.json); the picture scale is the Air's 1864 px panel. Sweep of 17 distances x 3 azimuths x both pieces (`palm_lod_eval.py`, `palm_lod_metrics.py`): L1 is within 0.4 % silhouette of L0 from 25 m out (only the trunk rings differ, faintly visible to about 50 m: 4 px between rings at 60 m); L2 reaches 1.4 % blurred-pixel difference at 140 m and 0 % at 175 m. So **L0 to L1 at 60 m, L1 to L2 at 140 m** (haze 19 % there), the same distances for both pieces because they share one crown. Unity's LODGroup wants screen-relative heights, and the group's size is the largest side of the bounds (CoconutPalm 7.94 m, CoconutPalm_Street 10.65 m): `lods[i].screen` = size / (2 x tan 27.5 x distance):

  | piece | L1 below | L2 below |
  |---|---|---|
  | CoconutPalm (size 7.94) | 0.1271 | 0.0545 |
  | CoconutPalm_Street (size 10.65) | 0.1705 | 0.0731 |

  A palm placed at scale 1.33 (some `CoconutPalm`s go to 1.33) switches 1.33 times later, as it should. The kit's category value (`trees` 0.08 / 0.025) does not fit (it puts L1 at 108 m and L2 at 346 m), so these two ids need their own values (Splice: an id override in `lod.ts`). The last (cull) level KitLods adds stays: 0.010 for CoconutPalm (under 8 m) and 0.004 for Street, far past the 350 m clip.
- **Commander view: every palm stays full detail.** An orthographic camera reads a palm as size / (2 x half-height): the zoom runs 12.5 to 18.5, so a palm is never below 0.215 (CoconutPalm at 18.5; Street 0.288), against the L1 switch at 0.127 and 0.171. The half-height would have to reach 31 m. Board 3 shows the Market Street avenue at zoom 14: 14 palms, all L0, 94,100 triangles, unchanged. L1 and L2 at play zoom are in the same board for the record: L1 loses the trunk rings (9 px apart at 66 px a metre), L2's cards show flat edges, so they must not be used there.
- **Pop check** (`board-4-pop.png`; Unity's LODGroup fade is off, so a switch is one frame). The picture before and after at the switch, difference x6: L0 to L1 at 60 m changes 0.3 to 0.4 % of the silhouette, 0.8 % of the pixels after a 2 px blur (worst azimuth 1.6 %), mean colour 0.2/255, and the difference is the trunk's rings alone. L1 to L2 at 140 m: silhouette 3.5 to 3.7 %, 1.4 to 1.5 % of pixels after blur (worst azimuth 3.0 %), colour 0.7/255, coverage 99.8 %, haze 19 %: leaflet-edge shimmer, no change of size, lean, spread or tone. Over a whole Market Street frame at 1920 x 1080 (205 palms, 12 L0, 25 L1, 168 L2) **41 pixels of 2,073,600 differ by more than 12/255 (max 23)**; from the other end 71 (max 26) (`street-stats.json`; boards 2). So by measurement the switch does not show; **it still has to be looked at in the game** (Latch: walk Market Street east and west in third person, switch the LODGroups on and off; it needs a build, which I did not make).
- **Triangles.** District: 947 palms (648 CoconutPalm, 299 CoconutPalm_Street) = **6.55 M triangles all at L0** (today); all at L1 1.26 M; all at L2 0.49 M. What a third-person frame draws of them (`palm_lod_district.py`, `district.json`; palms in a 16:10 frustum to 350 m): Market Street 1.34 M to 0.20 M (15 %) and 1.18 M to 0.16 M (13 %); Redline's two views (brief 048) 0.94 M to 0.11 M and 0.99 M to 0.13 M; standing at any palm foot in the district, median 0.67 M to 0.09 M (14 %), 90th percentile 1.02 M to 0.14 M (26 %), worst 1.32 M to 0.21 M. The play view draws what it drew.
- **The path to the game** (the generator side is done and checked; nothing here was run against Unity, the editor is Latch's). `palm_lod` is in `kit.FAMILIES` and registers the levels as the pieces' hand-built `near=` / `far=`; `export_kit.py --only CoconutPalm,CoconutPalm_Street` into a scratch folder wrote `DK_<piece>_L1.fbx` and `_L2.fbx` with one mesh object and the four materials in L0's order (KitLods needs one renderer and as many submeshes as materials), bounds within KitLods' tolerance of L0 (checked in Blender, not in Unity). **Use `--lods-only` when adopting**: a full re-export of the two L0 files differs in bytes from the catalogued ones (a missing-texture warning on this machine, not a geometry change: the vertices are identical), and L0 must not move.
