Digest of the two concept sheets, written once by Splice from the sheets themselves (Blocks/contact_sheet.jpg, AtomicAge/contact_sheet.jpg) before any comparison ran. One line per tile: silhouette · height class · crown · relation to the rail. This is the form a digest takes in the 007 F design (one entry per tile); it is the best case for text: a reader looked at every tile and wrote down what a planner needs.

Blocks sheet (aerial views of one block, 3/4 view from above)
blocks/hanging_terraces · a stepped garden ziggurat (planted terraces, pool, glass canopy) as podium, two slender white needle towers and a coral-ribboned rounded tower rise from it; a glazed bridge arch links the two needles · dominant: the two needles over a low terraced mass · crowns: finials and spires on the needles, a rounded cap on the coral tower · rail: none visible.
blocks/lifted_megablock · a park deck with a pond lifted on an arched concrete frame with red stairs, a capsule-topped rounded tower and a stepped spired tower rise from the deck · rival pair over a wide deck · crowns: smooth capsule dome; gold spire · rail: none visible.
blocks/machine_crowns · a broad podium with a cluster of five to six stepped towers, every crown a machine: radar dishes, red-and-white lattice masts, ringed insulators · mid rivals in a tight cluster, no single dominant · crowns: dish, lattice mast, antenna · rail: an elevated road or rail at the left edge only.
blocks/mixed_skyline · a podium with a pool; a saucer observation tower (white shaft, ring and cabin) at the left, a tall stepped spired tower behind, a glass cylinder tower with teal bands and a rooftop sphere at the right, a red lattice tower · mixed heights, one tallest stepped spire · crowns: saucer, gold spire, sphere, lattice · rail: a glazed tube bridge leaves the cylinder tower to the right (a sky bridge, not a rail).
blocks/needle_trio · three spires on one long podium (pool, glass dome, gardens): left spire with a silver atom (orbit rings), centre the tallest, fluted, with a gold starburst crown, right a slimmer fluted tower with a teal dome and finial, a red lattice mast far right · dominant: the centre spire, rivals at about two thirds · crowns: atom, starburst, dome and finial, lattice · rail: none visible.
blocks/rail_through · one dense block; a monorail on slim pylons runs along the street, enters the central tower through an arched opening about a third of the way up and leaves on the far side, then enters a second tower through another arch; a glazed barrel-vault station gallery is attached to the central tower at the same level · dominant: the central stepped, fluted tower with coral fins · crowns: gold stepped crown with needle and silver sphere; red lattice mast on the right tower; chrome spheres on roofs · rail: through the buildings, at arched portals, at the level of the station gallery (mid-storeys).
blocks/saucer_spires · a cluster of needle spires with saucer observation decks (Space-Needle type) over a wide curved podium with a car park and glass dome pool · rival cluster of three to four, one coral-and-white spire tallest · crowns: saucer deck on a needle; finials; gold ball · rail: none visible.
blocks/skyport_tower · one central pier tower with three stacked landing discs (air cars parked on them) and a rocket-like gold spire above; gold-domed corner pavilion on the podium · dominant: the disc tower · crowns: stacked discs, rocket spire · rail: none visible (air cars, not rail).
