# Research for brief 011 C, round 4: how real-time tactics and stealth games keep the HUD small

Glyph, 2026-10-15. Web search and fetch only, no screenshots viewed; every fact below is what the source text says. The GDD and briefs stay
the source. This adds to `syndicate-hud.md` (round 3).

| Game | Fact used | What round 4 took from it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shadow Tactics | The interface is a list of portraits with health bars, an action panel for the active character with a hotkey on each action (1-5 select characters, A S D F G the abilities), a planning "shadow mode" entered with Shift. Right-click on an enemy shows its field of view; right-click and hold on the ground places an "eye" marker and the enemies that can see it get a dotted line. | The keyboard hotbar (v10, v12, v13, v25: 1-4 agent, Q W E R verb) and the ground marker idea (rings, symbols on the pavement). | https://www.gamepressure.com/shadowtactics/interface-and-key-shortcuts/z1941c |
| Desperados III | Cones are the status readout (solid and striped zones); a view-cone marker can be placed anywhere or attached to a character; a blue radius shows how loud an action is; ear icons mark off-screen enemies who can hear you; "Showdown" pauses and lets you assign each character an action and target. | Information drawn on the ground (rings, arcs: v01, v08, v15, v22) and drag-agent-to-target orders (v03, v15, v26). | https://www.player2.net.au/2020/06/14-darn-tootin-tips-for-desperados-iii/ |
| Hitman | Players ask for the NPC threat icons to have levels: full, minimal (icons only in instinct mode) or off, because the icons clutter the HUD and hurt immersion; cone display was later limited to instinct mode. | Showing a mark only when it matters (price only when risky: v02, v09, v27) and the "numerals only" icon mode. | https://www.hitmanforum.com/t/npc-icons-full-minimal-or-off/6045 |
| Door Kickers 2 | Missions start paused; orders are a drawn line with facing; the squad then moves out together and the player can pause at any moment to change orders. | The cursor-drawn order line (v03, v15, v26). | https://entertainium.co/2025/02/24/door-kickers-2-review/ and https://www.thumbculture.co.uk/door-kickers-2-task-force-north-pc-review |
| Invisible, Inc. | Turn-based, so not a model for real time; reviews note it gives the player all the information to plan every step, and that agents can be switched at any point. | Nothing taken; noted as the counter-example (information-first, turn-based). | https://pcgamer.com/invisible-inc-review |
| Commandos | Enemy vision is a cone in two zones (close, far); the player views one enemy's cone at a time. (Origins: view cones criticised as clunky and inconsistent.) | The cones stay world signals, never restated in the HUD. | https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Concepts/Line_of_Sight_Stealth (and round 3 notes) |
| Satellite Reign | Not found: no source described its HUD layout. | Nothing taken. | n/a |
