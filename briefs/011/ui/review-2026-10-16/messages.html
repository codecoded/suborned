Play HUD messages: review

Fable, 2026-10-16, for Aster and Glyph. Sky's notes: "way too overlay heavy", "meaningless stats", "no constant info at the cursor". Scope: every string the play HUD shows (text.json and hard-coded C#). Nothing edited: no code, no text.json, no GDD. Read against GDD v0.22.0 §04, §05, §10 and the three screenshots.

The test applied to every line: what decision does the player make with this, now? No decision, no words. A line that repeats another line, tells the player a control they already used, or is a sentence the engine wrote for itself is cut. What stays is a price, a deadline, a result, a lock with its way out, or a person speaking.

Top 10 cuts and rewrites

  1. The move chip (screenshots 2 and 1). Scout: walk 8 s · run 4 s · unseen by security · 7 people near becomes 8 s · run 4 s. "Unseen" is the default, so it is silence; "7 people" is a witness count with no price (GDD §05: a lead, no T, no standing). The run is worth a word only when it costs: run 4 s · the guard sees: Alert 1. "can't run: carrying a downed operative, the operative walks" is cut from hover; the reason shows once, on the refused double-click, from the existing lock line.
  2. Control instructions inside state text. "Scout DOWN · select another agent, right click: carry" (world plate), "Down: select another agent and right click to carry", "Right click to give an order" under every idle strip, and the "right click: actions" suffix on every chip are cut. Controls are taught once, by gesture, in the Next Step plate, then retired (§3 ledger). The downed Scout shows a down icon; hover gives Carry Scout · 4 s.
  3. The seven "Cell" history rows are refused Panic orders (§2). Pressing both mouse buttons outside a fight is refused ("Panic works only once a fight is on."), and the host files the refusal as a card titled with the raw speaker id "cell", with no result. Panic should not exist outside a fight (no refusal, no pop, no row); refusals never become cards; the "Cell" filter goes.
  4. Cancelled walks. "Cancelled · Scout / the walk / Stops where they stand. Nothing is lost." is cut: stopping a walk is not an event. Cancels that cost something keep a one-line result ("The money comes back, but they remember the approach and your face.").
  5. Engine-speak and ids. "operative", "DOWN", "(Arms)", "the engine has no verbs for them yet", "a found fault", the raw pop ✓ scout watch waitress, card titles built from event ids ("Guard at cafe"), kebab-case route ids and the replay hash on the end card. All become player words or go to the F12 debug layer.
  6. Words where an icon belongs. The ring over a carrying agent reads "lift"; other marks read "eye", "ear", "in", "route", "tip", "take", "car". The Enforcer's slot caption "Enter / Take / Car" lists three verbs for one key. Icons (Glyph's atlas) and, for the slot, the verb that applies at the hovered target ("Enter the Office", "Take Vale", "Put Vale in the car"), else "Act".
  7. Duplicated and zero text on the action card. "Locked: {reason}" then "To unlock: {line}" says the same thing twice in two voices (the first is engine prose): keep one. Outcome lines that repeat the card's own sentence ("Learns their routine", "Loops the camera; the Tech stays at the cabinet") are cut. Zero prices ("$0", "A 0") are not shown.
  8. The briefing repeats itself. "YOU WIN" repeats OBJECTIVE; the five-sentence card repeats both; YOU LOSE is ten items; "Alert 0 of 3" is always 0 and "Cell armed" is a hard-coded constant (HudToolkit.cs:456). Cut to objective, three situation lines, four fails, two ways in, and the clock and purse.
  9. Fallback event cards print engine prose and skip the sight rule. When a city scene is "in-sight only" and the cell cannot see it, HostFeed.FeedEvent still makes a card from FeedEvents using the engine's log text (rivals.arrive, guard.at-cafe). Text should come from text.json event cards (title + result, table in §1 D) and the fallback should honour sight.
  10. Cell chatter. Silence acks for move, run, cancel, stance, cease, holster; drop the arrive beat from the ticker (it repeats ack); cut "The result lands when the act finishes." under every live card (the bar and "5s left" say it). Keep the cell's voice for acts that start something and for trouble.

2. The history "Cell" rows

What makes them. Both mouse buttons pressed together outside a fight. HandoverHost.Controls reads left-down-while-right-held (or the reverse) as panic and calls Panic(), which issues an order with Op = "cell". Outside a fight the engine refuses it (panic-no-fight: "Panic works only once a fight is on."). Issue() then calls Say(o.Op, Humanise(...)) with speaker "cell" (HandoverHost.cs:642). FeedSay returns early for the four agents and for an empty speaker, but not for "cell", so it makes a card: kind city, title Text.Speaker("cell"), which has no entry in names.speakers and falls back to the capitalised id: "Cell" (PlayText.cs:182; HostFeed.cs:181). The refusal line goes into the card's lines; the history row prints title + footer, and the footer is empty. Seven rows in 2 seconds (0:24 ×2, 0:26 ×5) is a player clicking quickly with the right button still down: ordinary real-time play.

Each should say: nothing. There is no decision and no event. Fixes (owners in brackets):

  1. Panic is not read as a gesture unless a fight is on (g.FightOn): outside a fight both buttons are just two clicks. No refusal, no pop, no card. [Latch]
  2. A refused order never creates a card or a history row, whoever the speaker is. Refusals answer at the click (B7) and stop. [Latch]
  3. An unknown speaker makes no card (N3). A card with no result makes no history row (rule 10). [Latch]
  4. The "Cell" filter chip is removed: what it filtered was the ticker's acks and engine sentences (E5). [Glyph/Latch]
  5. The one cell row worth keeping is a costly cancel, written as an event under All: Watch cancelled · time spent, nothing learned (and the walk's cancel gets nothing).

5. Style rules for HUD text

  1. A decision or nothing. Every string answers "what does the player do with this, now?" If the answer is "nothing", it is not shown. Silence is the default; speak the exception (seen, refused, locked, ready), not the normal.
  2. Numbers are prices or deadlines. Cash, seconds, Trail, Alert (only when it is not zero), a clock time, a count of enemies in a fight. Never a witness count, a ratio, a range band, an id, a hash. A zero price is not shown.
  3. Verb first for what the player can do ("Pay Inez", "Watch Inez first"), noun first for state ("Down", "Closes 8:30"). Results speak in the past tense and may end with the next step ("Inez is turned. Call her when you need the guard moved.").
  4. Short. A chip: ≤ 24 characters beyond its numbers. A card sentence: ≤ 80 characters, one idea plus its trade-off. A result: ≤ 90, one sentence. The hint plate: ≤ 110. If it wraps to three lines, it is two messages.
  5. Say a thing once. One place per fact: a result lives on its card, a price on its chip or card, a lock on its row. No echo in a pop, a strip, a ticker, a history row and a plate. Write a lock as the way out only; do not prefix "Locked:" or "To unlock:".
  6. The player's words, not the engine's. Agents are "the Scout", "an agent"; people are "Inez", "the guard". Never operative, tick, engine, id, kebab-case, hash, "found fault", "Arms". Unknown speaker or code: nothing, not a fallback.
  7. Controls are taught, not repeated. One hint per gesture, in the Next Step plate, retired on first use (§3). State text, chips, strips, tooltips and plates never contain "right click", "select", or a key unless the player pulled the tooltip. Key names come from the controls table.
  8. A refusal is one line at the click with its way out, and is gone. It never becomes a card or a history row. A gesture that does not apply (panic outside a fight, a click on a wall) is not refused: it is not read.
  9. Voices separate. System text is neutral and terse: third person, no "we", no humour. Character is in people's lines, and a cell member speaks only when an order starts something, the plan changes, or things go wrong, never to confirm a click.
  10. Show what the cell knows, as known. Written text, labels and "about" lines carry only what the cell has witnessed or been told; the rest is "?" or absent. A card or row without a result does not exist.
