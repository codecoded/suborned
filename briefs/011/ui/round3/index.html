Brief 011 · HUD round 3: minimal, in-world, for live play

Sky's verdict on round 2 was "way too complicated: simple, Syndicate style, icons on characters, more realtime than strategy". This round keeps the information on the characters and shows prices only while choosing.

Research: briefs/011/research/syndicate-hud.md (Syndicate, Desperados, Commandos, Shadow Tactics, Satellite Reign, with sources). Direction 3 (hybrid) was picked; the key beats carry only it.

Key beats of direction 3 (Hybrid)The four minimal directions (the same moment, 0:58)Character icon set at play-zoom size
Rules held. Signals are the reserved colours and nothing else uses them: cones red, guard and police amber, rivals violet, a red slashed circle only inside a dark window. Text is minimal and cream on ink (14:1). Honest limits. Icons, strip, pill, radial, price capsule, hover card and pops are drawn by the Codex image tool; the capsules for the cell, rivals and officers, the cones, routes and rings are a drawn world layer (the plates carry no cell or vehicles). The Codex clock digits are dot-matrix and some read ambiguously at a glance (3 and 5); strings were checked by eye against the prompts. Positions on the plates were placed by eye. The driver and guard are still placeholders (M-2 casts neither).
