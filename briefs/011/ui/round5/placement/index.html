4 · Flags

  • Keys. Verbs on 1-4 cannot also pick agents on 1-4. These frames show the agents as F1-F4 (portraits and the numerals over their heads), which is my suggestion; clicking a portrait or the street also selects. Sky or Latch may prefer Tab to cycle. The Q/W/E/R frame keeps today's controls.
  • Column height. The column is 42% of the screen height at 66 px portraits (the frame between portraits is real enamel, not padding I could drop). That is a tall strip on the west edge, where the cell start (the getaway car is on the west side). It is narrow (119 px, 6% of the width), and the fade handles the overlap, but it is the biggest silhouette of the three.
  • Fade. In (c) the column drops to about 30% opacity in an ellipse round any agent standing under it (the numerals also hide); it is drawn here as a still. It needs an alpha pass per frame in Unity, not a baked sprite: the kit for step 2 must keep the panel separate from the portraits so both fade together.
  • Hurt portraits. Forma's set has no hurt state, so the four hurt portraits are image-tool edits of her portraits (same face, hair, costume; bruise, scuffs, a tired expression, no red). If Forma wants her own, only four square images change.
  • Smoked lenses. The Enforcer's lenses in her portrait are smoked, so round 5's amber-lens flag is gone (the gold panels remain, as locked; her costume now reads black, white and chrome with no violet).
  • "Variable". Both layouts use the same four portrait windows, verb plate, pill and chip: only the roster frame is a row or a column. Making the placement a setting (left column or corner) costs one more frame asset, not a second UI.

5 · Glyph's pick

(a) with the (c) fade: the left column, in the r04 train-cab finish. It answers "bottom feels invasive" directly: the street's lower half is completely clear, the column sits in the dead edge strip, and the verbs fly out right beside the agent that will use them, so you read the roster and the verbs in one glance (Syndicate's own arrangement). About the same pixels as the bottom stack (2.7% against 2.5%), spent where the eye rarely goes. The dark gauge column also frames Forma's portraits best.

Fallback: (b), the bottom-left corner cluster if the column's height bothers Sky: 3.1% enclosing rectangle against 7% for the column, 162 px tall, no overlap with anything on the plates, and it is the cheapest to build (it is round 5's row, moved). Neither placement covers the playfield; the choice is how much of the west edge Sky is willing to give up. Deco finish works in either; I would still choose cab for contrast against the cream street.
