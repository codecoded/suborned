## Limits

- 60 fps at play zoom on the 15-inch MacBook Air M4. People and vehicles within Redline's proposed 4.5 ms GPU. Use the LODs (cell ≤ 12k near / 4.5k far; crowd 1.5k–3k).
- Characters 40–60 px at 1080p at play zoom. Read from above first; the T follow view second.
- Determinism: models and clips are view only. The Unity match stays full.
- Style: the cell are visitors from a nearby timeline (no 1950s tailoring); stand-ins are temporary and say so in the kit/asset notes. No long coats or flowing hems.
- Provenance: every imported file keeps its `PROVENANCE.json` entry (source, service, cost, sha256).
- Signals stay readable by glow and shape (cones, guard, rivals).
