# PC defaults for third-person action games (Glyph, 2026-10-21)

Community key guides and search snippets, not checked in each game. Treat as indicative. Opened = page fetched; Snippet = search result only.

- GTA V, Opened: https://www.bisecthosting.com/blog/gta-v-controls-guide-pc-playstation-xbox-keyboard-mouse-gamepad . Enter Vehicle F, Context Action E, Aim RMB, Fire LMB, Weapon Wheel Tab, Sprint Left Shift, Take Cover Q.
- The Witcher 3, Opened: https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=101746 . Interact E, Sprint Left Shift, Fast Attack LMB, Parry RMB, Cast Sign Q, Draw Steel 1 / Silver 2, Dodge Alt, Roll Space.
- Red Dead Redemption 2, Opened: https://www.magicgameworld.com/red-dead-redemption-2-pc-keyboard-controls-guide/ . Enter Vehicle / Mount Horse E, Aim RMB, Fire LMB, Weapon Wheel Tab, Sprint Left Shift, Take Cover Q. (Another guide lists F for vehicles; a ComputerBase key PDF says E.) No holster key listed.
- Cyberpunk 2077, Opened: https://www.magicgameworld.com/?p=125476 (Aim RMB, Shoot LMB, Sprint hold Left Shift); Snippet: https://upcomer.com/how-to-change-the-pc-interaction-keybind-on-cyberpunk-2077 (interact fixed to F; a Nexus mod rebinds it) and https://n4g.com/articles/cyberpunk-2077-phantom-liberty-how-to-holster-your-weapon (holster Left Alt by default, per one guide).
- Ghost Recon Breakpoint, Snippet: https://www.magicgameworld.com/tom-clancys-ghost-recon-breakpoint-pc-keyboard-controls/ . Interact E (F is Grab Body), Aim RMB, Fire LMB, Sprint Shift held. No holster key listed.
- Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Snippet: https://www.ea.com/able/resources/mass-effect-legendary-edition/pc and https://www.magicgameworld.com/mass-effect-legendary-edition-pc-keyboard-controls-guide/ . Interact E in ME1, Space in the sequels and the Legendary Edition (per an EA answer); Aim RMB, Shoot LMB.
- Hitman: no default list found; not used.
- Tap vs hold on one button is a known complaint (RDR2; see briefs/038/research/sources.md).
