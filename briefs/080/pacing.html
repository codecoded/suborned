What length? §01 says 8–12 minutes a mission; the Handover closes at 8:30. From memory, unchecked: Syndicate missions ran a few minutes, Commandos and Shadow Tactics often 30–60+ (with constant saves and players choosing their own pace). Ours is real-time on a live clock, so the measure that matters is density, not length. Proposed target: close 7:30 · informed quiet win 3–5 minutes · first try 5–7 · nobody's plan finished with more than 45 s to wait.

Effect on brief 080. The ≥ 30 s window rule stands: guard away (2:54) and Loop (2:00) don't change; the false tip keeps about 2:00 after the rivals' latest arrival. New band: reference plans never wait over 45 s with the whole cell idle. Win bands get re-measured after the clock moves. Every recorded route has to be retimed, so move the clock in the same round as 080's adaptive plans, not before, or it's done twice. All of §07's clock, §04 and §05's times, and T2 go in the same GDD version.
