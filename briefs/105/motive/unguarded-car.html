The unguarded car

Brief 105 · Motive (systems) · 2026-11-10 · a proposal for Sky, not yet in the GDD or the rulebook

In one line: rivals never take Popovic out of a car. They only shoot at it, and only when nobody of the cell is outside. Anyone standing at the car counts, gun drawn or not. If everyone hides inside, the rivals shoot the car until it can't drive. That is the existing "getaway car wrecked" loss, with seconds of visible warning and a one-click answer: step out.

Sky's rulings (2026-11-10, build 0.2.310)

  • "They can't take Popovic from the car. They can only shoot at the car."
  • "Everyone was around the car, can't just make me lose for that." The whole cell stood round the car, no guns drawn, and the game still lost Popovic.
  • The car's step-out button reads "Move" when it should say "Get out". That's a wording bug for Glyph and Fable, and nothing below depends on it.

The current rule (what loses today)

Two rules let rivals take Popovic from a car. Both fail as rivals-reach-vale, cause reached-car:

  1. The walk-in (fight.unguarded (a), engine 0.34.0/0.35.2). In the safe fight, with Popovic in our held car and no drawn agent outside, every rival but the blocker walks to the car, firing, and takes no cover. When an agent outside draws, they go back to cover. Code: GameUnguarded.cs.
  2. The reach (brief 018 §1). Any rival who gets within 1 m of the car's body while no drawn agent stands within reach of it takes Popovic. This also covers Popovic's own car once it is lent to us (route B, the Enforcer at the wheel). Code: RivalReachesVale and RivalReachesLent, called from GameFight.cs:122.

Both count only a drawn agent as a guard (Sky's yes of 2026-10-27). That is why the undrawn cell standing round the car in 0.2.310 lost.

The proposed rule

1 · Nobody takes anyone out of a car

Delete both takes: the walk-in's take and the brief 018 reach, for our car and for Popovic's lent car. A rival at a car's door is just a man by a car. While Popovic is inside, he can't be reached.

2 · People in a car aren't targets; the car is (as in Syndicate)

A shot at someone sitting in a car is a shot at the car. The riders, Popovic included, are hurt only if the car explodes. Today a rider can be shot through the car under cover, which is how the Enforcer went down inside in BAL-YARD-AT-WILL. The car's own law is unchanged: 10 HP (HealthCar), it stops for good at 5 (CarDisabledAt), it burns at 0 and explodes CarBurnS 6 s later, hitting everyone within 5 m (CarBlastCm) for 3 damage (CarBlastDamage).

3 · Who the rivals shoot: people first, then the car

One target rule for the rival crew. A rival fires at an agent he can see within his reach, as today, drawn or not. A rival with no agent in reach, while Popovic is in a car we hold, fires at that car. He fights from where he stands, under the cover law (fight.cover). If the car is beyond his pistol's effective reach (15 m), he uses the existing advance and stops at that reach. He never walks to the door. The blocker keeps his lane and shoots too. Once Popovic is out of the car, the car is no target. Police never shoot a car with the Councillor in it: the loud fight already has the seal.

4 · Anyone at the car counts

An agent standing outside the car, drawn or not, is a person in the fight. The rivals shoot at him, not at the car. Nothing ends the mission because the cell is standing there undrawn. Undrawn agents are shot at and can't fire back until they draw, and that is the whole price.

5 · What a shot-up car does

  • Our car disabled with Popovic aboard: getaway-lost, as today ("the getaway car is wrecked"), with a new cause, shot-out. The mission ends at the disable point, before any fire or blast, so Popovic and the riders are never hurt.
  • Popovic's lent car disabled: as today (LentDisabled). The Enforcer gets out with him and the fight goes on on foot. With Popovic out of the car, the car stops being a target. On foot the old laws hold: a downed Enforcer still leaves Popovic to the rivals.
  • Go: unchanged. It's refused while the car is held or boxed, and a disabled car never drives.

No new values. The walk to the car (rivals.for-car) and the back-to-cover walk (rivals.back-to-cover) go away, because the rivals never leave cover. In their place a log line, rivals.fire-car, records the first shot at the car.

What the player can do about it

  • Step out, any agent, drawn or not. The rivals switch to him at once. Drawing lets him fire back.
  • Keep someone outside until the block breaks. Board last, then press Go.
  • Break the crew or blow the rival car before boarding (existing). Then the gate opens and Go works.
  • Thin the crew early with the Mislead, Pay or a takedown. Fewer shooters means a car that lasts longer.
  • Fair and readable: the threat is guns turned on the car, hits on its health, and a card. The time to answer is 4–12 s against a 1 s answer. Nobody loses for standing next to their own car.
