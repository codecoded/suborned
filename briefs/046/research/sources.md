# Brief 046, round 5: character plates in squad and party games (Glyph, 2026-10-22)

Sky: "try with a divider between characters perhaps? Maybe find some AAA game titles for examples of good character plates and states."
Screenshots are kept on disk only (`shots/`, `wiki/`, `crops/`: git-ignored by the briefs image rule, never committed). Each is a publisher's store screenshot or a Wikipedia thumbnail used as a study; what I read from it is below. Where a source could not be reached or did not show the HUD, it says so.

## How I looked
- Steam store `appdetails` API lists each game's official screenshots (`https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=<id>`); I downloaded the 1920 x 1080 ones and cropped the HUD (`crops/<game>.png`).
- Wikipedia infobox screenshots for the 1993 and 1996 Syndicate games and for Mass Effect.
- Text from guides and manuals for what pictures could not show.

## Principles that came out of it (used for the roster; see `board-research-principles.png`)
1. The block is one portrait plus one thin state line, nothing else (BG3 HP under the face, JA3 and Mutant ticks under the card, Commandos ticks): our icons-under-the-portrait row is the same shape.
2. Blocks are separated by space or by one quiet mark, never a frame per block: BG3, JA3, Commandos use space; Desperados III uses recessed slots; the busy ones (Satellite Reign, Syndicate) use heavy frames.
3. The state row hugs its own portrait and the air goes after the row: proximity says whose icons they are (JA3, Mutant, Gears).
4. Selected = promoted (scale, colour against grey, one frame), never a colour per role (Commandos, JA3, Phantom Doctrine, Desperados III).
5. Hurt and down change the picture first and the bar second (BG3 red fill, ME2 red and grey, JA3 red bar and "!").
6. One status glyph in a fixed slot with a fixed priority; an empty slot stays empty; being shot at or seen is one glyph or one ring pulse on the portrait, not a new element (JA3 "!" and eye, Gears red glow, ME2 red portrait).
7. Words only for the selected one (name beside the big portrait in Desperados III, Gears, Phantom Doctrine); none on the others.
8. Digits only where the game is numeric (BG3, Gears, JA3 levels). Ours is time and chance: pips, no numbers, and no key numbers (Sky removed them; Desperados III and Satellite Reign keep them on a tab).
