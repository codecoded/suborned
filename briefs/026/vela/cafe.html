Where the testbed's building would be better than ours (Sky chooses).

  1. The curved glazed run. The testbed's S-curve is a true smooth sweep with a CAFÉ fascia and bronze mullions; ours is a faceted arc and our awning is polygonal at the corner. This is fixable inside our form by re-segmenting; it does not need the testbed's building.
  2. The roof. The testbed's has an enamel surface, a rolled edge, two glazed skylights over the room and vents; ours is blank. It reads from the play camera when the upper mass fades. Also fixable in ours, and it depends on whether the café keeps a storey above it (the 26 Sep paintings put the Records Office beside it, and the civic block's café is 34.8 m high, with upper floors).
  3. The footprint. The testbed L is more interesting from the air but is a different building (18.5 × 10.5 m, no upper mass). The plan fixes ours at 22 × 11.3 × 34.8 m (check C-0, brief 008) and the paintings agree. Swapping would move Datum's plan, Vantage's layouts and the café door points of the Handover.
  4. The room. The testbed's interior is a real hollow with a modelled counter, cashier and service equipment; ours has more colour and a mural but a lighter counter. Either way the room is seen only through glass.

My recommendation: keep our footprint and form (they are the plan and the painting) and take the testbed's surface method and its parts. That meets "at least the testbed's resolution" without re-planning the block. If Sky prefers the testbed's L, that is a plan change (Datum, Vantage, Waymark) and I would build it as a new piece beside ours.

4 · Plan (ranked; each step shot on the baseline café stops)

  1. Surface pass on our pieces (biggest visible win). Bring PBR (base + normal + ORM) to CafeBay_v2 piers, plinth, belt courses and CafeTerrace paving from the testbed's limestone set, re-coloured to §15; a roof enamel with rolled edge, skylights and vents for the piece's own roof; enamel and brass maps on mullions and counter. Budget about 14 MiB (limestone 2048 + normal 1024 + ORM 1024 ≈ 9.3, roof 1024 set ≈ 4.5).
  2. Curves. Re-segment the arc, the awning and the plinth to 48+ segments (smooth normals); keep the C-0 footprint.
  3. Glazing and interior through the glass. Keep the clear glass and the room; add a roughness-varied reflection streak map, a fluted petrol counter, cashier and equipment parts from the testbed, a real ceiling with cove, the back-bar painting re-checked at 1519×1035.
  4. Terrace. Parasols with panels, seams and a finial (the testbed's method, our seafoam and ivory or coral); baked chairs and tables at 500 and 750 triangles with a 1024 normal (a quarter the testbed's atlas size, about 3 MiB); gathered awning valance; terrace screens and planters with botanical atlas plants.
  5. Plants. The testbed's botanical and foliage atlases (about 8 MiB budgeted) for the terrace planters, palms and ferns; the same set feeds the plants task of brief 026.
  6. Signage. A CAFÉ fascia and a blade sign with the testbed's lettering approach (no readable body text in play; the sign is a world marker, §10).

Texture budget: about 30 MiB for the café on top of the ground budget (testbed total 35 MiB at full size; half of that is the 4096² baked colour, which I would not take). Everything is hash-copied through the kit like the ground set.
