026 Round 2: urn, road and lawn tuning, plant and street-fitting inventory (Vela, 2026-10-20)

Evidence: Blender renders with the same sun and sky on both sides (not the game's light), texture statistics, and the kit catalogue. Unity was not driven; Latch rebuilds to confirm.

1 · Planter_Urn

The old city's urn (left) is a goblet urn with a teal and ivory body, brass bead and a clipped boxwood ball. The kit already had that exact piece (Planter_Urn, 3.6k triangles) and its slimmer Planter_Urn_Play that the world places (92 times). The Play urn was a 10-sided bowl with 14 foliage cards, which reads as an octagon at street level. Rebuilt: 18 sides, 30 foliage cards, 6-sided handles, 1,536 triangles (was 1,112), same pivot, size and materials; LODs 380 and 88.

The sliced look in the round-1 Unity shot is a placement problem, not this mesh. The mesh has outward normals on every band (checked: teal 40 faces out, 0 in). In that shot a tub palm (FanPalm_Play, 195 placed) stands in the same spot as an urn, so the trunk runs through the bowl and hides its bands. For Splice: a planter and a tub palm must not take the same footprint; the urn already carries its own boxwood ball.

2 · Asphalt: detail normal and the pink-brown cast

  • The asphalt detail normal strength is now 0.45 (_DetailNormalMapScale; it was 1.0). The grit still shows at street level but no longer sparkles in sun.
  • The cast came from the road sheet's own chroma: its warm stains and patches turned pink-brown under a warm sun on a violet-grey base. The sheet is now re-coloured with a luminance curve, the target hue and 30 percent of its own chroma, so the mean stays #79757A while saturation falls from 0.096 to 0.037 and the chroma spread (sd of R minus B) from 0.69 to 0.43 levels.

3 · Lawn

The lawn sheet is softened by lawn_soften: low-frequency luminance (the 1 m mown bands and clover drifts) is pulled to 45 percent of its strength, seamless via a periodic blur; chroma is scaled to 0.78; the target mean moves from #6D8A4B to #74875A. Texture statistics against the old sheet: saturation 0.439 to 0.352, band contrast (low-frequency luminance variation) 0.200 to 0.098, blade detail untouched.

4 · Legacy ground pieces no longer cost 64 MiB

Pave_Flags_i, Kerb_Granite*, TreePit_Flags_i and the sett gutters bind the legacy materials DK_Pave_Flags, DK_Gutter_Setts, DK_Granite_Kerb and DK_Granite_Kerb_B by name. The generator now writes those four .mat files itself (keeping the legacy guids, so the FBX bindings hold), pointing at the budgeted, re-coloured sheets instead of the old 4096 x 3 maps. The sheets have the same layouts, so the quadrant and metric UVs still fit. The catalogue now lists only ground_* textures for these pieces (checked in kit.json). Splice can adopt them without touching the budget. Note: Unity's Kit folder still holds six legacy pave_flags_v1* files from when they were adopted earlier; they are stale and can go.

6b · Round 2 shots reviewed (asphalt ripple fixed; new items below the floor)

Read from rounds/round2-20261020-1338/: close/close-street-Kerb.png, close-street-ManholeCover.png, close-plant-Planter_Urn.png, close-plant-FlowerBed.png, close-vending-VendingMachine_Cola.png and office-front-play-30.png.

Asphalt rippled and watery at eye level (fixed in the generator). The manhole close-up shows it plainly: sparkling, stretched highlights across the whole road. The base normal is not the cause (a 512 map with a tilt of about 0.3 degrees, effectively flat). Two things were: the detail normal, which tilts surfaces up to 70 degrees (sd 0.36) and at grazing angles under a lit sky becomes a rippling mirror even at the 0.45 scale, and the mask smoothness, .18 to .50 with glossy patches that read as puddles. Now: the detail normal's tilt is halved in the texture itself (max 53 degrees, sd 0.22; the material scale stays 0.45, so about a quarter of the round 1 strength) and the asphalt smoothness is .13 to .28 (mean .20, was .18 to .50 and mean .30). Base normal stays flat. Textures and the Unity copies are in sync after bun run kit. Not seen in the engine: Latch re-shoots close-street-Kerb and close-street-ManholeCover; if any shimmer remains at grazing angles the next lever is the detail scale (0.45 down to 0.25), not the texture.

Planter_Urn_Play floated its boxwood. In the Unity close-up the topiary is a faceted diamond hovering above the soil with a gap and no stem. The Play urn's foliage core was a 4-point diamond and it had no stem. Now a ball (as the full urn) on the stem, 1,552 triangles. Shot in Blender after the fix (vela/round2/r3/each-Planter_Urn_Play.png).
