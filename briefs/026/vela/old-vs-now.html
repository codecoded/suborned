026 Vela: old city models against today's kit

Vela, 2026-10-20. Blender renders under one light (same as compare.html); columns are now (HEAD settings, brief-007 oxblood and ochre), old (old city's generator settings and enamel hexes), proposed (what is now in the generator). The Post "now" shot is the earlier slim render.

Cause, short. The geometry was cut by the generator, not lost: the old generator source is the same code, and old settings reproduce the old manifest triangle counts exactly (Hydrant 6840, Bollard 5116, PhoneBooth 34744 ...). Three things took detail out: (1) kit.asset(lean=, cap=) from brief 007 round 3: lathes cut to 8 sides and the whole piece collapse-decimated to a triangle target (Hydrant, Bollard, RecyclingBins, CCTVPole, DomeCamera, PhoneBooth); (2) play020.py caps (the robots, CoffeeCart, BusShelter, TramCanopy, CurvedKiosk at 3k) and the brief 008 / 020 slim redraws (FanPalm, Planter_Urn, CafeSet_Tulip, Cypress, PottedTopiary, PlazaSphereLamp, PneumaticPost); (3) the 16 Oct enamel retune (oxblood, ochre) was left in. Materials: DK_Chrome is identical (metallic 1, smoothness 0.95) in the old and current kit; the grey matte caps and the teal rim on the old base discs are what a metal shows of its surroundings, so they come from the Unity scene (reflection probe / environment and sun), not the kit. That is Glint's and Latch's to check: chrome in today's Unity scene has little to reflect.

Triangle cost of everything restored: about +2.65M triangles over all placements (not all on screen; L1/L2 LODs exist for the street, plant and structure categories). The ones placed in the hundreds: FanPalm_Play 195 (+0.61M), Bollard 352 (+0.36M at lean 0.6), RecyclingBins 284 (+0.36M), Hydrant 114 (+0.27M). At 60 fps on the M4 these are for Redline to measure; I took Bollard to a middle setting and left the rest whole.
