Vela, 2026-10-20. Testbed = the old web build served locally (~/Projects/the-suborned/testbed, Chrome headless through Playwright, script in out/vela026/cap.mjs; frames in testbed/, finish buttons Slate asphalt / Powder-blue Streamline). Now = the current Unity frames of round 2 (Glint r2 c5, ../rounds/glint-r2-c5/) plus the same Blender street slice today's ground gives. Proposed = new generated kit pieces rendered in Blender (tools/decokit/review026_testbed.py), same camera (play 38 deg, yaw 30) for every Blender frame. Not the game's lighting.

Cause, short. Nothing was ported. The civic block was rebuilt from the 26 Sep paintings (brief 008) and the testbed was read only for footprints (the Oct 5 manifest in civic-block.adjust.json). The road look is three things in the testbed code, none of them in the world export: (1) a continuous light ribbon set flush at both edges of the carriageway, brass lips, a 3.7 cm pearl lens and a 1 m warm pool at evening and night (civic-road-guides.js, road-ribbon-controls.js: auto / on / off, colour #ffe6b5); (2) a sealed enamel carriageway (powder #aed0e6, 2 m repeat, the testbed's default alternative "Slate asphalt" is dark with white dashes) with pearl lozenges on the centre line and chrome panel seams every 9 m instead of paint (civic-road-finish.js, civic-roads.js); (3) a pearl kerb cap with a gold crown. Today's world.schema.ts Road is {surface, kerbSurface, line, width, level, kerbWidth, kerbHeight}: one surface, one kerb, no edge light, no line or inlay channel (the only light set in a road is the crossings', brief 009). The ground set (brief 026 round 1) then took the old city's asphalt sheet and recoloured it to the painting's #797374, the dot studs are the crossing's. So: never ported, and the road grammar has no slot for it. The textures were not dropped: the enamel and marble sheets still sit in the-suborned-store/testbed-art/district-road/.

What reads as modern in the testbed night frames is the light line that traces the road's shape (it follows every kerb return and bay, so the road is drawn as one clean loop) over a pale, even, slightly glossy carriageway with a few white lozenges: no paint, no dashes. Design test: light set in the road, no zebra, no paint; brass (not the testbed's gold) as hairline only. Decided (Sky, 20 Oct): B.
