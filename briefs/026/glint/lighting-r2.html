Round 2: why the Waiter reads flat, and the contact shade

Brief 026 · Glint · 2026-10-20 · round 2 scene (round2-20261020-1338), shots through GlintRounds (variants in briefs/026/glint/variants-r2/, nothing saved; a control run reproduces the round-2 close-up to 0.2 of 255).

1. The Waiter: not the light. The body has no surface variation.

  1. Colour is not the problem. Body mean (HSV): round 2 hue 37, saturation 0.44, value 0.58; old city hue 41, saturation 0.48, value 0.50. Same family, same hue; the new body is slightly lighter.
  2. Variation is. Pixel spread across the cone body: round 2 about 12, old city about 35. The old body has grain, a patina, a vertical seam and a darker base; the new one is one flat enamel colour with no albedo map, no normal map, no baked occlusion (the enamel materials have map=False norm=False).
  3. Material response is fine. DK_Enamel_Cream / Mustard / Ivory / Seafoam: smoothness 0.68, metallic 0, clear coat on; chrome 0.95 / 1.0 (the ring, tray, arms and dome mirror the cafe correctly). I took the enamels to smoothness 0.35 and 0.2 and the clear coat to 0: no visible change on the cone (the body is lit by ambient in the cafe's shade and a dielectric at that angle shows little reflection). Not a smoothness or metallic issue.
  4. Probes are fine. The Waiter stands at (-142.9, 0.2, -217.6), inside the civic-square probe (box x -188 to -132, z -285 to -215, box projection on), baked with the round-2 build and the B sun, with the district's default cube behind it.
  5. Light can add only a little. The cone sits in shade where the ambient equator is uniform, so the lit side has no gradient. Giving the trilight a top-down gradient (sky x1.0, equator x0.9 / x0.8, ground x0.7 / x0.6) lifts the pixel spread from 12 to 14 / 16 and costs 4.5 / 8.5 levels of play-zoom mean luma (137.8 to 133.3 / 129.3). That is not worth it for one prop, and the frame would drift toward the old city's darkness; I did not change light.json for it.

2. Fix for Vela (a material value, not a hue)

Give the robot enamels what the old DecoKit robots had: (a) a fine-grain detail albedo or normal (a tileable grain at the robot's scale, strength about 0.3), so the cone breaks up under the same light; (b) baked ambient occlusion in the vertex colour or a base-gradient darkening on the cone's lower third and under the skirt (the old base reads 20 to 30 levels darker); (c) the vertical seam and the buttons already on the old body. Keep the enamel hex (cream #f3ddb0, mustard #967509, ivory #efe8d6, seafoam #72b5a7): hues untouched. Smoothness 0.68 with the clear coat can stay.

3. Contact shade in round 2 (plinth offset in)

  • The wall-foot strip now starts outside the plinth and shows as a soft dark band along the plinth and the pavement where the pavement is lit; in a building's own shade (the Records Office front at yaw 210) it is faint because the ground is already dark. Crop below: round 1 tune (top) against round 2 (bottom), 2x.
  • Play-zoom mean luma: round 1 138.5, round 2 138.0 (the five stops differ by -1.3 to +0.3 except the park, +3.8, which is the lawn texture, not the contact); p5 44.0 to 43.7. The frame did not get brighter.
  • Sunlit wall feet read well in the side-street yaw 30 frame (cypress bases, kerb feet, the booth). No change to the contact values: footWidth 1.6 m can stay while the offset is in.
