Brief 026 · the old DecoKit city, rebuilt and captured

Rebuilt 2026-10-19 by Latch in a scratch project (out/old-world/unity, git-ignored): Unity 6000.6.2f1, URP 17.6 with the template settings, glTFast 6.20 and Newtonsoft added; ~/Projects/the-suborned/game/Assets/TheSuborned copied in unchanged (the old tree was only read). The old builders ran as they were: DecoKitMenu.ImportStreet (kit import: 113 materials, 202 prefabs; then the DecoKitStreet city with its street life, district and Southside crowds, traffic, sky traffic, city mass, colourway, road markings, probes and occlusion), DecoKitMenu.RobotReel (faces), and a scratch-only capture script (Assets/OldWorldCapture) for the play-zoom stops and close-ups. Lighting is the old street build's own (HDRI sky, sun 3, ACES, exposure -0.8, soft focus), not the new world's. Build log extract: build-log.txt; prefab counts: inventory.json. New-world baseline: ../baseline/.

What would not build

  • The Handover block in the district (HandoverDistrictBuilder): needs Art/CivicTestbed/ (environment.glb and the six actor GLBs), which is not on the Studio; NullReferenceException in CivicPlazaBuilder.BuildInto. The DecoKit city itself is complete without it.
  • The VAT crowd test (DecoKitCrowdTest): the scene builds (Citizens3D VAT bakes import), but Assets/TheSuborned/Shaders/VatCrowd.shader and VatCrowdBlob.shader are missing, so it cannot render. The city's crowd is the static RetroFutureMobility figures (shown below), not the VAT crowd.
  • Not reproduced: the old project's own URP asset (the template one stands in) and its saved scenes; the old play camera's exact grade at play zoom (the iso shots use the street build's grade with fog x0.35, as the old DecoKitIso did).
