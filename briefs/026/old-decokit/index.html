The old DecoKit city, captured faithfully (brief 026, Latch, 2026-10-20)

The floor (Sky, 2026-10-20). The old project (~/Projects/the-suborned/game, read-only) copied whole into the scratch project out/old-world/unity: its own ProjectSettings, Packages (less com.unity.pipeline, ai.assistant, collab-proxy and multiplayer.center, which draw nothing; Newtonsoft added back, which they had pulled in), URP assets (PC_RPAsset: MSAA 4, SSAO 0.9 / radius 0.45, the Harbour fog full-screen pass, 4 cascades to 90 m, light probe system on, GPU Resident Drawer off) and the SAVED scenes opened as they are: their own lighting data, 26 reflection probes, grade volumes and colour plan. No rebuild. The earlier set (a template-URP project that rebuilt DecoKitStreet) is kept in rebuild-template-urp/ with the settings diff.

What differed from the template-URP rebuild

  • Play zoom: nothing visible. Mean absolute difference 0 to 1.4 of 255 on every stop where the framing matched (square, avenue, park, aerials). The old SSAO doubling (0.4 → 0.9) moved no play-zoom pixel: SSAO does nothing at the orthographic play zoom (Glint's finding holds).
  • Street level: the real settings are darker in contact and shade (MSAA 4, SSAO 0.9, 4 cascades): the urn -12 luma, potted topiary -7, hydrant -36, phone booth -17, porter and shopkeeper robots -19/-20, sandwich board -29, InfoLightbox -14. That is the contact AO the floor carries at eye level.
  • Layout: the saved scene is not the rebuild's: the densest café and traffic-light picks moved (cafe stop (-54, -123.5) not (-57.5, -127); crossing (-8.6, 168.5) not (-8.6, 56.3)), so those four stops differ by framing, not look.
  • The animated crowd renders in Play (DecoKitCrowdTest: 3,000 VAT agents, 617 drawn at street level, 615k triangles).
  • CivicTestbed (the Handover block port of the 2.5D testbed) only exists in Play: its Forward+ pipeline clone, 238 lights and camera are made at runtime. It is the higher bar for the café (see civic/).
