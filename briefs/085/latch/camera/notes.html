What I can see from these stills

  • The cut stops under perspective. The architecture cut only runs on an orthographic camera (ArchitectureFade). At the door at yaw 210 the guard is simply behind the wall under all three perspective views: his marks float on the facade, and there is no cut or silhouette. That is the biggest thing that breaks; a perspective camera needs that work first.
  • Ground marks are sized for the orthographic view. Under perspective they take a fixed scale from a stand-in zoom (HudToolkit.Ground), so they don't shrink with distance. See the Tech's cabinet mark and the signal's road ring in the corner row.
  • The tactical shadow fit is off under perspective (CamProjection turns it off), and the shadows differ from today's.
  • Triangles rise a lot. 2.8 to 5.7 M under perspective against 0.8 to 1.4 M today, and the play view's gate is 2.5 M. Each figure is one frame's Triangles Count at the paused moment, not a performance run.
  • Verticals lean under perspective, most at FOV 40: the door's towers and the café's sign. The figure is the same size only near the middle of the frame.
  • 32° keeps today's cut, marks and triangles and is the only change here that needs no other work. It shows more facade and less paving; a figure stands about 82 px.
  • Orthographic 8 gives about 96 px figures with no other change, and shows a fifth less of the street in each direction.

Figure pixels are a 1.8 m person projected at the spot's centre. Perspective uses half-height 8.9 (the lab's zoom value), which gives about 90 px at FOV 30 and 38°. Not compared side by side with Sky's Desperados III and Shadow Tactics shots; nothing in the game has changed.
