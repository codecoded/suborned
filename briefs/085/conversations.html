2. When does the cutscene play?Once, whole, when the act finishesThe answer is known by then, so it can't contradict the result, and the new action opens as you read about it. The cost is a cut you didn't time yourself, but the clock is stopped and you can skip. The other ways: half at the start and half at the end (two cuts), or all at the start (you'd wait out the act knowing the answer).
