Minimum general correction — proposal only

For Latch's swap: honour exported step length, then join adjacent tile ends with a shared mitre derived from neighbouring track tangents and the existing 2 m width. Apply the same final polygon to mesh ownership, Bed/ClearBed and audit. For Splice's normal export, use the same general join contract at the 1.355 m flush width. Derive neighbours from route/track geometry, never an ID allowlist or these six positions. No route movement, plan-radius change, lateral widening, crossing removal, sim collider or walk change is proposed.

Simply stretching each tile to step fixes only two of six. Simply making all rectangles longer creates coplanar overlaps. The general solution intersects adjacent offset side lines, then clips both ends to the same seam plane: no positive-area overlap at that seam. For ideal arc radius r, half-width w and turn δ per tile, a containing tangent rectangle needs length 2(r+w)tan(δ/2), then mitre trimming. Relative to rδ, full-length extension is 88.608 / 75.770 mm at 2 m width, or 60.446 / 51.636 mm at 1.355 m width (r=23.2 / 26.8). These are formula examples, not hard-coded padding.

Isolated join geometry intersects side lines of the actual rounded tangent placements, sharing vertices between neighbours. It covers all six audit points and all six exact-arc comparisons at both widths; every polygon vertex remains within its original tangent width band. It does not integrate materials/UVs/height, solve curve-to-straight/end-of-route joins, test rail-head continuity, or certify route/walk clearance. Those are later integration checks, not waived requirements.
