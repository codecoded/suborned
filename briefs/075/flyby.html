Old against new now (Latch's read)

  • Better: the landmarks. The Skyport tower's lattice is vermilion (50 s), and the red sphere and gold SpaceHQ crown are in place. The tower sites no longer stand in square punched-window boxes: at 20 s the disc towers and needles read as round shafts. This is the closest the frame has come to the old reel's heroes.
  • Better: colour. The wall tint now holds the street together, as the old cream did. The boulevard at 6 s reads as one family with accents rather than candy blocks. The bands are back to seafoam and teal.
  • Better: road and pavement. At play zoom the powder-blue road carries the old asphalt grain, and the pavement is the old cream. Shade is deeper under the old trilight, which gives the street the old reel's contrast. Night is effectively unchanged.
  • Worse: the haze is too strong for our flight. At 33, 50 and 61.5 s the long-tier haze washes the far city and the horizon ring into a cream fog. The skyline that the old reel builds its last shot on disappears; the old reel keeps crisp mid-ground detail under its haze. Our far ring sits 750 m and more out, behind an empty band (C10), so the haze gets nothing to layer and only erases. The likely fixes are the mid-ground massing (C10) or a lighter long haze for this flight: Glint's call.
  • Different: the sky is pale cyan, not teal. The sky is the old one, but our flight looks level or down, where the old sky is pale. Glint noted this, and lowering _MidHeight would be a design change.
  • Different: the sea. The sea is a deeper blue-teal, closer to the old reel than 074's flat teal. There is still no glitter, because our sun is behind the camera (Glint's note).
  • Still missing: the 8 to 10 storey glass-slab canyon (C1, the height-cap question for Sky), the lush close palms (C7), people and traffic (075-D), and a textured sky-rail beam. The pink box beam still reads as the flattest thing in every frame (C9).
