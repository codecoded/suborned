Concept

The plan in seven moves

  1. The old DecoKit town at its own coordinates: Crown Circus at the origin (old z 206), the boulevard on x 0 with its walls at x ±18 and 28 m deep blocks, Market Street, the lanes, the four crescents, Orbit Gardens, the harbour terrace, steps, promenade and quay, the piers, the landmarks.
  2. The Handover zone byte for byte: the civic block, its streets (Rocket Lane, Starline Street, Garden Row, Orbit Walk, Pylon Row, Apron Road, Satellite Street, Lantern Lane), its plots and gates, and today's neighbours on every edge of the plots in its walk window.
  3. Seams: Satellite Street's east pavement is the boulevard's west wall (boulevard-west, old W1); Starline Street runs on as the old west lane through it; Market Street meets the boulevard at (0, -410); Lamplight Lane (today's line) fronts the garden terrace and the West Terraces; the garden walk crosses Circus Road into Sphere Park.
  4. The boulevard runs through to the Sea Gates (old Comet Row and Satellite Street on its axis); Southside east of it (Constellation N2, Orbit Square, Starline Green, SS1–SS4); the Lantern Quarter south of Market Street.
  5. West Row west of Sunset Way (the old procedural massing's place): an access street off Circus Road, lanes, old-grammar blocks; Western Park north of Circus Road to Crown Road.
  6. T1 tram: street running from the West End down Sunset Way and Market Street, up the boulevard to the circus, where it turns on the ring road (R 25). No overhead wires.
  7. S1 sky rail on the circus axis from the West End (Western Park and Orbit Gardens stations), north past the Sphere; the old main loop is planned data (oldtown.skyrail).
  8. S2 sky rail (Harbour Line) from the south over the water, Harbour station on the Market Pier's landing stage (the T1 interchange at Crown Circus), and a terminal loop round the Skyport Tower with the Skyport station on the Skyport apron: the old harbour line's sweep round the Skyport.
  9. SpaceHQ closes the axis at the old place (0, 244), the gate towers either side of the forecourt, the Skylon, the Sphere, the Skyport on its caisson at the pier head, the liner at the Market Pier.
