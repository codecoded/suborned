Goal (Sky, 2026-10-11): "the vehicles, pedestrians, the heart and vibrancy of the city". The built world (004) comes alive: trams and sky rail on their timetable, traffic on lanes, and streets busy at ≈ 0.12 people/m² (§15), every figure going somewhere for a reason.

## Fixed
- **Everyone near the player is a person (§04, §09):** routine + need + temperament + memory, generated at build time from the world. Workers come from the trades and staffed kiosks on their block, residents from housing plots, riders from stops and stations. Beyond the simulated set, a background crowd only walks, runs and cheers (§06), and fills the streets cheaply.
- **No AI at runtime; exact replay (§01).** Routines, traffic and timetables are generated at build time, and the simulation steps them deterministically. Same start state + orders → same city.
- **Vehicles on lanes (§11):** cars, vans, hover-cars and service carts on road lanes; trams on track; sky rail on beams. Timings come from the plan and the r008 timetable parameters.
- **The rules engine stays separate from Unity (§11).** Unity plays back and renders; the simulation decides.
- **Graybox first:** people are capsules with a colour block by job; vehicles are boxes by type. Forma and Vela dress them after review.

## Decisions (Aster, 2026-10-11)
- B accepted. Density basis = foot ground (pavements, median, malls, walks), target ≈ 0.12 from §15 "density ≈0.12 people/m² reads busy".
- `harbour-district.life.json` is generated by scratch scripts under out/life/. Splice moves the rules into `contracts/world/harbour-district.life.rules.json` and the builder into `tools/life/` (`bun run life`) before anything reads life.json; out/ is never a source.
- **A accepted (Keel, city-sim-architecture.html).** One C# engine as a Unity-free library: Unity runs it live, build tools run it headless via `dotnet` (Sky, 2026-10-12: yes, if optimal and efficient; Keel measured 0.62 ms per sim-second vs 37 ms for TS-in-Unity). 10 Hz fixed tick, integer state, 2 m grid per level, ≤ 600 simulated people + ≤ 2,000 background, replay = orders + checksums. Simulated set = the mission's block + whoever the rules pull in near an operative; never the camera. Zoom fixed at 14 (§10, §15).
