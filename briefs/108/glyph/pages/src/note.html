Skip to Begin: my choice

A quiet "Skip to Begin ›" on page 1, where Back would sit, shown only after the first play (a restart after a loss, or a new game). A first-time player always gets the three pages, because that's the introduction Sky asked for. A returning player shouldn't have to click through it again on every retry. It's a text link, not a button, so Next stays the obvious path. Page 1 still shows first, so each run opens on the story. The dots already let anyone jump to page 3.

What leaves the briefing

  • Money and Heat: gone. The HUD shows the cash in play, and Heat is zero on the first mission.
  • The nine ways to fail: gone. Page 3 covers the dangers, and the end card says what went wrong.
  • The checklists: behind the way chips, and in the Journal.
  • The five-stop timeline: the rivals' 2:35 is on page 3, and the 5:30 and 5:50 are on the way chips. The chime is already the HUD's next-event line.
  • The cell strip: the Cell tab.

Kept on every page: the weather pill and the clock at the top right, the folded "Dial DEV" chip at the bottom left of the street (it opens today's panel), and the tabs (Cell, Job, Menu).
