# Parked ideas (Sky)

Ideas Sky has raised for later. Not design yet: nothing here goes into the GDD or a build until Sky picks it up.

- **Retro-future personal transport** (Sky, 2026-10-22): "I might introduce hover boards / retro future modes of transport later on." Context: getting about on foot feels slow and lowers exploration. Fits the world (1950s style, centuries-ahead tech). Open questions for then: who notices a rider (running is noticed only by cameras, robots, guards and police since 2026-10-22), can a rider act or fire, where boards are kept or found.
- **Three movement speeds** (Sky, 2026-10-22, "not 100% on this idea yet"): walk; jog when a weapon is drawn; sprint when holstered. Try as playground tabs (brief 041) against a brisker walk and a hold-to-fast-forward before deciding.
- **Queued orders** (asked 2026-10-22, Sky: "not yet - need to focus on capstone"): Shift + click to queue an order behind an act in progress.
- **Unity features** (asked 2026-10-24; brief 056 has the studies): Adaptive Probe Volumes over the civic block, ground decals (target rings, body and Trail marks) and small Particle System effects (steam, sparks from a looped camera), HDR colour grading with a 64 LUT, a timed connector graph so the sim has deck and sky-concourse levels, and audio (Sky: "later"). Perf work in 056 (Air run, SSAO and shadow cuts, texture sizes) is not parked; it serves the 60 fps limit.
- **Turned people fight with taken guns** (Sky, 2026-10-25: "Park it for now"): a downed guard's weapon can be taken and given to a turned person who isn't armed, so the people you turn back you in fights and later on. Already in the GDD: the bravery ladder (Informant → Helper → Accomplice → Fighter, §04), "your side" (Accomplice and Fighter contacts, crowd joiners, §06) and Arms from "arming joiners" and "hijack". New: Take the gun (from the body) and Give it (to a person within reach), each with its price shown. Aster's suggestions, for Sky to rule on when it opens: only a willing Fighter takes a gun (a Helper refuses, and anyone turned by Pressure never fights: the content line); allies get one order before the fight and then fight on their own nerve, with no fifth portrait; a police gun found on a captured ally, or named by a Talker, is evidence that leads back to the guard. Open: do taken guns carry over as campaign Arms?
  - **The capstone slice** (Sky, 2026-10-25: "like the idea of the driver helping you if you turn him and have knocked out the guard to get his weapon, so fighting rivals gets interesting and player can see in the capstone now game evolves"): in the Handover, turn the driver (route B), knock out the door guard and take his gun, give it to the driver, and he fights the rivals with the cell.
