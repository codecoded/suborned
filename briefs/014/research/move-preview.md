# Move-preview references (Glyph, 2026-10-16)

Searched 2026-10-16; the web gave little on the move-command preview itself, so I mark what is sourced and what is from memory.

**Sourced**
- Desperados III: the enemy's view cone is shown on demand (hover or right-click the enemy), in zones: a part a crouching cell can avoid, a part that always detects, a faded part blocked by obstacles. Holding right mouse can mark a point and highlights guards that can see it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desperados_III , https://www.pcgamesn.com/desperados-3/gameplay
- Invisible Inc.: tiles in a guard's sight are marked on the grid, peripheral sight in a lighter red ("noticed"); observing a guard shows its full path. Patch notes: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/569390373?p=7 ; community notes: https://kleiforums.com/topic/40825-gameplay-x-ray-vision/

**From memory, not verified here** (treat as a hunch, not evidence)
- Shadow Tactics: a held key shows the enemies' cones; the move order draws a path with a destination marker; no per-move numbers.
- Invisible Inc.: moving shows the action-point cost on the destination tile and the exposed tiles in red.

**What I took from them**
- The cone and the exposed stretch are the answer to "seen by whom, where"; the number is secondary. (Invisible Inc., Desperados III.)
- Detail on demand (a held key) and the quiet default (a marker, not a panel). (Shadow Tactics, Desperados III.)
- None of them uses a sentence in the preview.
