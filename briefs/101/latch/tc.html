Changes from Glyph's spec, and why

  • No Esc. Sky's call: piece 3 is dropped, and the capsule's hover names only T.
  • Pictograms at 64 px, not 128. The kit's singles are 64 × 64 @2x, so Glyph's drawings were reduced with Lanczos. They read cleanly at the tab's 15.6 px.
  • A face's tab stays up 0.6 s after the pointer leaves the face. Without it, the tab hides before the pointer can cross the pips to it.
  • The face's label row starts after the tab's slot on every face (x 138), so labels never jump when the selection moves. In third person it starts after the capsule. While a tab or capsule is hovered, that face's row gives way to its words.
  • No tab or capsule under a modal (book, menu, briefing, end card) or in a conversation, where T isn't heard either.
  • Code homes. The eye's read is in HostDrive.cs, not HostLess.cs: it's only read for the driven agent, and r29's 100 s stays out of the delta. The chevrons moved out of HudToolkit.Less.cs (1140 → 1132 lines) into the new HudToolkit.TakeControl.cs (135).
