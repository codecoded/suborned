## What changed
- **The police:** each officer is his own target, keyed by his engine id (`security-1`, `security-2`).
  - The pick, the ring's anchor, the outline, the stack dots and Fire's order now land on the officer under the pointer.
  - The bare `security` still means the unit (the engine's nearest officer with a line), for scripts and the panel guard.
  - Everything that read `security/0` was checked:
    - the feed cards, the police crucial card and the bubble anchor keep `security/0` (they're the unit's, not a target);
    - the attention views already map each officer.
  - **A change in play:** "Fire at the police" used to go to the nearest officer in line. Now it goes to the one you point at.
- **`bookAgentRows`:** the agent pages are sampled on a fixed grid (every 150 ticks) along route A, whatever ticks its orders fall on.
- **`r085listenApart`:** asserts §10's pick for the geometry as it stands.
  - The profile two-shot if any of its 20 lenses holds the pair clear, else the play pitch at 7 m (and the camera at that pitch).
  - Here that's 0 of 20 clear, so "high".
- **r083 without focus:** while the input checks run, the proof drives the Input System's own virtual devices.
  - A `ProofMouse` and a `ProofKeyboard` are added with `AddDevice` and made current, fed only by queued state events.
  - The OS's pointers and keyboard are disabled meanwhile, with `IgnoreFocus` and `runInBackground` on.
  - HUD clicks go through an `EventSystem` with `InputSystemUIInputModule`, which UI Toolkit takes its events from while it's up.
    - UI Toolkit's own default event system skips every frame while the player is unfocused (Unity 6000.6 on macOS), and its switch is internal.
    - The clip and portrait clicks now arrive through the UI ("device"), not their handler fallbacks.
  - The proof itself is launched behind (`open -g`), as the batch's other players are with `--behind`.
- **r083's two other failures were the checks, not focus.** In 0.2.302 the run was focused for every frame and both still failed:
  - `r085doorPrice`: the pressed route stands the Fixer in front of Inez, and your own agent under the pointer always wins a click.
  - `r085outlineOfficer`: one officer stood in front of the other.

  Both checks now point at a part of the target the pointer really reaches (the hover reads it there, and none of the cell is under it), turning the view a quarter when needed. What they assert is unchanged.
