Brief 096 · Glint · SSAO at play distance, prototype (pictures only; nothing changes in the player)

Sky: "ok", prototype option 1. All frames are the built player at play zoom, 0.2.248's look, the same ticks and yaws. Each option is laid over for one launch by the review switch, which swaps a project copy of URP's SSAO shader into the renderer feature.

It works once two blocks are cleared. (1) URP's depth bias, 0.004 × eye depth (SSAO.hlsl 359–360), is about 0.64 m from the play camera's 160 m stand-off, more than any useful radius, so no sample occludes. The copy uses 3 % of the radius in the orthographic view and drops the distance fade there. (2) With After Opaque off, the AO never reaches the frame: the play scene's materials don't apply URP's occlusion texture (no pixel changed with either shader, even at full resolution, while the pass cost about +1.8 ms). With After Opaque on (URP multiplies the AO over the opaque frame, the renderer's own setting as built) it shows: URP's shader gives a little (4–9 % of pixels); the copy gives corner, wall-foot and under-table occlusion.

Recommendation: the mild setting (O3) is the one to look at. It gives contact under the tables and chairs, at the planters' feet and along the wall foot, but a fine dotted noise sits on thin mullions and planter rims: half-resolution AO through the 0.125 m half-precision depth steps at 160 m. The strong settings are too dark and dirty. Before it could ship: compute the depth in full float inside the copy (or move the stand-off closer), to clear the dots; check the signals, which draw after post and are unaffected; and measure on the M4 Air.

Half resolution reads within noise of the baseline here; full resolution reads about +1.8 ms. Brief 008 measured URP SSAO at full resolution on the M4 Air at +1.2–1.9 ms, so expect roughly +0.4–0.9 ms on the Air at half resolution. That's not about 0 ms: it needs an Air run before Sky decides, and the zone's contact shade (A1, now in light.json) stays the 0 ms route.
