Brief 096 · Glint · Redline's option B: the camera's stand-off from the tallest roof (comparison only)

Sky wants to compare before deciding. The built player at the same ticks and yaws. Option B is laid over for one launch by the review switch: the orthographic play camera stands 70 m back along its own view instead of 160 m (in the log: height 43.1 m, pitch 38°, near plane 0.3 m), so the framing is identical, and the shadows use one cascade over the fitted distance (about 87 m at play zoom) on the full map. Nothing in the player's defaults changed.

What it looks like: visibly crisper shadow edges. The lamp post's long shadow, the umbrellas' rims and the planters' edges are tighter, and edge contrast in the Office 2× crop rose about 9 % (round 1's option A: about 0.5 %). The frame is otherwise the same; the other differences are timing (the crossing light, figure poses).

Near-plane clipping: none seen in these frames. The Office tower and the café block are whole at play zoom and in the planning zoom-out. That holds here because the Handover zone's roofs stand below the 43 m camera height. Taller buildings elsewhere (SpaceHQ, about 250 m) would clip at 70 m, so a real version sets the stand-off each frame from the tallest roof in the frame column, as Redline proposed, not a fixed 70.
