Audit: what the play view uses today

  • Night: the play scene (OldTown) has no LightPhase, so the shot runner's night switch only tints the day frame. Compare night-ldr with night-hdr in img/ to see the HDR shift. There's no night look to retune in the player until night exists in OldTown.
  • Flyby, golden view, evening white balance: not shot this round. The flyby and golden view are presentation cameras, rendered by the editor reel tools (GlintVintage on OldTown), which need their own Unity launch. Evening runs on the lens's evening data, which may override the play white balance during dusk. All three stay on the list.
  • Signals: still not proven. Holding the detail layer (Alt, via PeekLatch) at tick 300 shows the crossing heads and rings but no camera cone. A camera's cone probably draws only once the Scout has learned it (the "?" over an unlearned watcher). A frame needs a state where the entrance camera is learned and live: a Latch shot row.
  • Kit gloss, the cause: the kit enamels are Complex Lit with a full clear coat (mask 1, coat smoothness about 0.97) and environment reflections on, so every bin, panel and robot carries the teal-grey reflection cube. K2 is the treatment the camera got in round 1, applied to all 79.
