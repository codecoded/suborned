Recommendation: A1 now, then a zone-only rerun of the game's own contact-shade builder with stronger, wider values (Latch, OldTownSwap plus World/Light/ContactShade.cs on the Handover zone's plan footprints). The zone already has contact shade (brief 026: building feet, kerb feet, blobs under street pieces, cut into OldTown); it's just too faint to read as ambient occlusion at play zoom. A1 doubles its strength, costs 0 ms, and has no artefacts. A2/A3 show where more helps (the café's façade foot, the planters, the Office's inner corners), but my quick prototype took ground and road pieces for buildings and laid hard rectangles across the paving: a builder must use the plan's footprints, as ContactShade.cs already does, not mesh bounds. Baked lightmap or vertex AO would also shade walls and inner corners, but needs lightmap UVs on the kit pieces (Vela) and a scene bake (Latch); it isn't the cheap step.
