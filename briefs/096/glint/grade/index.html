Audit: what the play view uses today

  • Grading mode is LDR (PC_RPAsset m_ColorGradingMode 0, LUT 32). Every colour control runs after ACES on clamped 0–1 display values. URP's HDR mode grades in ACEScc before the tonemap, the documented choice for an HDR pipeline, at no extra per-frame cost. The build strips the HDR-grading shader variants (URP ShaderScriptableStripper: _HDR_GRADING is kept only if a pipeline asset grades in HDR), so HDR grading can't be a runtime toggle: it's a one-line change to the asset.
  • ACES, contrast 10, saturation 35 and a warm lift are all compensating for each other. ACES skews hue and flattens the shoulder: lit cream renders H42 (yellow) against the paintings' H30 (peach), and shaded stone renders olive-khaki (H43–49, V0.45–0.49) against the paintings' light warm shade. Saturation 35 then strengthens the olive.
  • Shade is half the brightness of sun. Shaded paving is V0.45–0.49 against lit V0.92 (ratio about 0.5); the street paintings sit nearer 0.85. Sun ×3.0 at shadow strength 1.0 with a grey-blue trilight sky (#B5BAC2) gives dark, olive shade on yellow-cream albedo.
  • SSAO is dead in the play view. The renderer has it (intensity 0.4, radius 0.3, after opaque, falloff 100 m), but the play camera stands 160 m back, so URP's ShouldSkipAO returns nothing (brief 064), and TacticalShadows turns it off at ortho. Nothing gives contact or form except the sun's shadows.
  • Unused: White Balance (0/0), Split Toning, Shadows/Midtones/Highlights (all 1), Channel Mixer, Curves except the lens's greens hue-vs-hue, Color Lookup (no LUT), Vignette (0). Depth of field, flare and fog are off at play by design (§15).
  • Reflections: glossy enamels (smoothness 0.62, environment reflections on) mirror the play scene's reflection cube: dark street, walls #7D8785 (H168), wall base #2F3A3B. That teal-grey sheen is why ivory enamel (the litter bins, the hovering camera) reads grey-green in shade.
  • Kept as is: bloom threshold 1.05 / clamp 4 (brief 079), shadows fitted by TacticalShadows (2 cascades), Forward+, MSAA off, HDR target R11G11B10. The play exposure +0.02 is the designed value: OldTown has no LightPhase, so the lens uses base 0.15 − 0.13 (lens.json r085).
  • Signals: the red cone ring visibly changes with the grade (in the pastel trials it went pink), so not all signal geometry draws outside the post grade. For Glyph and Latch to check before any grade ships.
