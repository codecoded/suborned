Brief 096 · Glint · lighter AO, two strengths (pictures only; nothing changes in the player)

Sky on O3: "its too much". The speckles on window bars and trim showed too. Today first, then about half and about a quarter of O3's darkening. Each is the built player at play zoom, laid over for one launch by the review switch with the project copy of URP's SSAO.

What changed in the copy: (1) the depths and sample positions are full float (URP rebuilds them in half precision, which steps 0.125 m at the play camera's 160 m stand-off: that made the dotted bars and trim); (2) the AO is weighted by how much the surface faces up, so façades and fronts take none, only the ground at wall feet, under tables and chairs and around planters; (3) radius and intensity lower. The darkening is measured on each frame against today, as a share of O3's on the same frame: F2 44–52 %, H2 24–28 %. Most of the drop is the façade mask.

To look at: F2 for a visible ground contact, H2 if even that is too much. Neither shows the speckles; both leave the walls as they are.
