Audit: what the play view uses today

  • What blocks SSAO in the ortho play view (URP 17, ShaderLibrary/SSAO.hlsl lines 359–360, SSAOCommon.hlsl line 55): each sample's obscurance is dot(v, n) − kBeta × centre depth, with kBeta = 0.004. With the camera about 160 m back, that bias is about 0.64 m, larger than the sample radius (0.3 as built, 0.6–1.0 in these trials), so every sample clamps to zero. The renderer's falloff of 100 m (ShouldSkipAO) was a second, independent block, and the depths are in half precision, which steps 0.125 m at 160 m. The feature setup, the depth source and ortho support are not the cause: the pass runs, costing about +1 ms here, and returns nothing.
  • Making it work needs code, not a setting: either a project copy of URP's SSAO shader with an ortho-aware bias (bias from the radius, not the depth; same cost as SSAO, which brief 008 measured at 1.2–1.9 ms full-res on the Air, about half that at half resolution), or a camera that stands much closer than the tallest geometry allows. A cheaper form cue, generated at build time with no per-frame cost, is the game's own Contact Shade strip along façade bases and prop feet, as under the street pieces and the hovering camera. I recommend the strip for the Air budget, with Vela and Latch.
  • Why shadow strength 0.9 came out lighter: two writers. AtmosphereLens set lens.json's value in its begin-render hook, then OldTownPlayLook (baked by SwapPlayLook, subscribed later, when OldTown loads) overwrote it with its own sunShadowStrength of 1.0. So the play view always rendered at 1.0, and 0.2.247 never had B's 0.78. Any review value under 1.0 read lighter, and 0.9 is still lighter than 1.0. Fixed: the lens publishes the value it wants (shader global _SubornedLensSunShadow), and OldTownPlayLook uses it when it's set, its baked value otherwise (Runtime/AtmosphereLens.cs, AtmosphereLens.Play096.cs, OldTown/OldTownPlayLook.cs). The fix changes 0.2.247's shade: it's B's fill, as Sky picked.
  • K2 in the generators: kit enamels via tools/decokit/unity_mats.py MATTE_KIT; `bun run decokit --mats-only` changed 63 enamel .mat files and nothing else, then `bun run kit --extra out/decokit-077` (the 077 extras pin the same materials). The old town's own enamels (77 Generated/Paint colourways and 13 DecoKit materials under the git-ignored OldTownArt/) get the same rule in Editor/OldTown/DecoKit/DecoKitMaterials.cs (Matte096), which takes effect at Latch's next OldTownSwap.Build; until then they stay glossy in the player. These frames lay K2 over them through the review switch.
  • Not covered: the cone frame (still not shown: the shot runner can't put the camera into a learned and live state), the flyby, the golden view and evening.
  • One review launch crashed (native segv in a managed call as OldTown loaded, review switch with K2 wildcards). A retry of the same launch ran clean, and so did the plain player. The crash log was overwritten by the retry; the stack showed the fault under mono_runtime_invoke from UnityPlayer, right after the HUD set up.
