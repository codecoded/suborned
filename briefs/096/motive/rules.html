1. Bribes on camera

The rule

A Pay or a Pressure is recorded when a live camera watches the Fixer or the person being turned at any second of the act. Live means not looped, not disabled and not shot out. Watches means the same test the watchers' log and attention use: in its cone, a clear line from its lens to the head or chest, and no crowd in the way. One act is one incident, however many cameras or people see it. The record lands when the camera's desk next looks at that screen, + 3 s. A Loop, Disable or shot afterwards doesn't undo it: it's on tape. Ask, Listen and Help stay private (Q1). Any camera, any mission.

2. Boxed in, and the kerbside stop

The rule

A car standing on another car's way out boxes it in. That car's Go and Drive are refused, and the refusal names the car in the way. A Drive is also refused into a stand whose way out is blocked: it can't get in. A car that's driving blocks nothing. The way clears the moment the car in it leaves or explodes. A disabled or burning car still stands, so it still blocks. The rule holds for every car the mission simulates: a city car that's boxed in waits and tries again each second.

The kerbside stop

A turned driver who finds his drive taken (your car on it, or driving there) pulls in at the kerb short of the drive. He stops clear of the drive and of your car's way out, facing the way he was driving. The Take, the carry and (later) the split's lend use that stop exactly as they use the bay. Where, from data, so a generated mission gets the same:

  1. Go along his own route toward the bay, to the last point before the drive's kerb line (civic-block drives[].kerb) where his car, grown by 1 m all round, touches neither the drive nor the taken stand's way out.
  2. Move it across to the kerb the drive joins, until the car's edge is 0.5 m off that kerb.
  3. If another standing car is there, step back along his route until the spot is free.

In the Handover that gives about (81.4, −19.9), nose east. That is 1 m clear of the drive, 2.5 m clear of your car's way out, outside the pickup camera's reach (21 m from its pole against 18), and clear of the rival tail on the far lane. Splice prints the exact point from the grid; the tests check these properties, never the number. This replaces rulebook cell.driveCar's "the turned driver stops at the drive's mouth", and the car.blocked words.

3. The parked car is noticed, and the wait

The rule

A car of the cell's standing on private ground counts as a loiterer. Private ground is a drive, or an official or service bay. Every security watcher that keeps attention clocks keeps one for the car, with the same marks and the same report: live cameras, the door guard (unless he's the cell's ally), the patrol robot, and the response's officers and robots. The clock runs whether anyone is in the car or not. A car in a public bay or at the kerb keeps no clock. Agents sitting in a car keep no clock of their own, because they're off the street. Their own clocks run again as soon as they step out beside it.
