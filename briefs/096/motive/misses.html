In short.
  • The veteran (0 %) is a rule-reading bug. The police shoot any agent who has ever fired, which they can't know. Fix V3: "fired" counts only if they saw the shot, or once the cell is placed. Popovic then gets out on 61–65 % of seeds, but the other three are caught on nearly every one. Whether that's a fair price is a question for Sky (Q2).
  • Quiet-b's three left behind (56.5 % all four out): the 60 s police clock is too short for a fight that breaks about 26 s after Popovic is out. The fix is 75 s, plus a plan fix (Go before the 8:05 close). Measured: 69.5 % all out, 0.52 caught a win, in band.
  • Thinned worse than plain B: cause found, and it's the plan, not a rule. The plan never actually thins a posted man (0 of 196 seeds), and its late Listen puts the Scout 4 m from a rival as the crew springs. Rewritten so the posted man is paid early: 89 % all out on the seeds where he takes money, against B's 69.5 %.
  • Popovic out 100 % and quiet-g 87 % are my estimated bands being wrong, not the game. The lend makes Popovic's Go safe in a quiet fight by design. Route B's risk now sits on the other three, and every mistake still costs the job. Bands change, no rules.
  • Route A's 13 s fight is preparation paying off: Inez's warning lets the cell shoot first. Moving the floor to 10 s is a question for Sky (Q1).
  • Loud on the lend (35.5 %): L6 holds. A loud fight comes to the car with Popovic. The lend is route B's quiet tool, and the front door stays the loud route (66.5 %). Keep it: Q3.

1 · The veteran's shot: 0 %

Cause. In the police's list of reasons to fire on an agent (ai.intent), "fired" means the agent has fired at some point in the mission. After the street fight at 2:55, the response arrives at about 3:36 at what is filed as a gang shooting, with nobody placed. Any agent who fired, holstered or not, is shot on sight. The cell fights the police long before the Take, and the Enforcer goes down on 192 of 200 seeds. V2 (the careful placing rule) never gets a chance to matter.

Fix, V3 [S]. An officer or robot counts "fired" only if it saw the shot itself, or once the cell is placed. Until then a holstered agent who fired earlier is just a person in the street. Drawn guns, seen violence and known faces work as today. This is the world's rule: information moves through people (§01), and a cop arriving at an unseen shooting doesn't know who did it. The minimum Keel probed is enough: "fired" counts only while no gang shooting is pending. I re-ran it on all 14 plans: it changes only the veteran.

Recommendation. Build V3 now: without it the shot Sky asked for can't be won at all. Read the veteran's band as "Popovic out 40–70 %, captures shown". It stays an option for Sky only if Waymark finds a plan that works.

The walk to a hidden listening spot takes 40 s, the Listen 30 s and the Pay 35 s, so it never lands before the crew springs (0 of 196 seeds paid him). So "thinned" in today's bands only means the lookout is paid instead of misled. Either way three men stand at the square: the only difference is the crew breaks at 2 down instead of 3. Worse, the late Listen has the Scout 4 m from rival-2 when the crew springs (hit at 950 ‰): 77 seeds lose an agent, against plain B's 51.

Fix (Waymark, no rule change). Thin a posted man early. After the driver's Listen (about 2:20), the Scout Listens to rival-2 at his post (heard about 4:15). The Fixer pays him if he's a Mercenary ($30,000; with the Help, $70,000 of the $100,000). The lookout is misled at 4:30 as in quiet-b. If rival-2 is Loyal, the plan plays plain quiet-b.

6 · Route A's final fight: median 13 s against 15–40 s

Cause. F1 and F2 work: the fight now ends at the break. It is short because route A is the best-prepared fight in the game. Inez's warning shows the ambush before it fires, so four agents draw first and the guard joins. The first volley lands from 6 m and the crew (four on foot) breaks at three down. 91 of 200 fights end in 10–14 s, with a second group at 25–45 s (p75 27 s, p95 42 s). Agents down per win: 0.04.

Recommendation (Sky, Q1). Move the quiet fight floor to 10 s: median 10–40 s, 95 % under 90 s. Sky's word on 2026-10-30 was "try the shorter". The 15 s was Aster's reading of it. Thinning route B's crew also lands at 10–11 s, and in both cases the short fight is what preparation earns (preparation > improvisation). Alternative: keep 15 s and have Pulse make the warned crew spring from cover, so the first volley meets a half-shield. Unmeasured, about +5–10 s. It takes value from turning Inez.

Tried and dropped: breaking at three quarters instead of more than half. No change for A (four on foot already break at three), and it cut thinned's all four out from 51.8 % to 26.6 %.
