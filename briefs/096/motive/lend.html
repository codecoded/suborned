The lend: Popovic leaves in his own car, the other three get out through the block

Motive, brief 096, addendum to the rivals page, 2026-11-05. For Keel to build right after engine 0.41.0. Design only: no engine, GDD or rulebook file is changed. Every number is a starting value [S]. Times are on the 5:30 shift. Values: briefs/096/motive/lend.json. Measured on 0.41.0 (200 seeds per plan): briefs/096/motive/lend-probe/summary.txt, with the probe plans and scripts beside it.

No question for Sky. Every rule here follows a call Sky has already made: 071 Option A (2026-10-26), 082 calls 1–4 (2026-10-30), the rivals page (2026-11-04) and "build the lend now" (2026-11-05). Where my own earlier values got in the way, I have changed them and say so: the wheelman (F1), and the 071 lend's seats (L3).
In short.
  • The lend. When the in-car Take ends in the service bay (about 6:45), Popovic's car is the cell's. The Enforcer is at the wheel and Go is offered on that car. Popovic's car takes only the Enforcer. The win is fixed the moment it leaves. The crew posted at the square springs as it leaves.
  • The other three get out in our car, through the block. The rival car has boxed our car in since about 2:53. The three break the crew (the wheelman then drives off) or blow the rival car up, then board and Go. The police close in 60 s after Popovic is out. Anyone not in a car that has left by then is captured (Sky 082.1).
  • Route A's long fight (median 50 s) isn't the fight itself. It's the cleanup afterwards. The wheelman gets out at the spring and is shot, so on 138 of 200 seeds the car has to be blown up. Beaten men also keep counting as fighters while they walk off. The fix: the wheelman stays at the wheel and drives off the moment his crew breaks (Sky's call 4 already says "still in the car"), and a beaten man is out of the fight at once. The band stays at 15–40 s.
  • The veteran's shot (0 %) loses after the street fight, not in it. The cell wins the street fight with nobody down on 164 of 180 seeds. It is then placed on 176 of 200. The police "see" any agent anywhere during their 60 s at the scene, and the cell keeps its guns out chasing a beaten man. The fix: the beaten man runs off, and the police place only someone within 30 m of the scene, a gun out where security can see it, or a known face. Expected 15–35 %.

2 · The veteran's shot (hard but possible, 15–35 %)

What fails today (0.41.0, 200 seeds): enforcer-down 174, sealed 26. The street fight isn't the problem:

  • The car is disabled at a median 2:55.6. The crew breaks 8.5 s later, and on 164 of 180 seeds no agent goes down.
  • Then the cell is placed on 176 of 200 seeds. On 95 it's "a new incident (gunfire)": guns still out chasing a broken man, a drawn weapon seen by the patrol or a camera, then a shoot-out with the patrol. On 67 it's "the police saw the fixer at the scene", which today means any agent in sight anywhere during the 60 s hold.
  • Once placed, the seal is 145 s away, and the police fight the cell long before the Take.

V2 placedWhile a gang shooting is pending, the cell is placed by:

(b) during the hold (60 s from the response's arrival), an officer or robot seeing an agent within 30 m of the scene;
(c) a known face, at any time;
(d) a later seen shooting, a new incident, or Alert 3.
The scene is where the shots came from and where the car stopped.		The police look at who is near the scene, not at everyone on the map. 30 m is about the square. The crew breaks about 8.5 s after the disable; holstering and walking 30 m takes about 19 s; the response arrives 35–45 s after the first shot. Tight, seed-dependent and earnable. The intent stands: an unseen shooting of rivals alone seals nothing until the cell is placed.

After the shot. A crew whose car is destroyed walks off for good (0.41.0): no block, no posted three, no lookout. What's left is route B on the lend at Alert 2 with a loud final fight. A placing after about 4:20 seals only after Popovic's car has gone, so the danger is the first two minutes after the shot. Levers: over 35 %, set unclaimed-scene-m to 40 or the hold to 90 s. Under 15 %, set the scene to 20 m.
