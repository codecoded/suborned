Conversations in full sentences

A sample, before anything changes in the game. Three conversations, rewritten under the looser rules you asked for, each beside today's version. Same people, same facts, same outcome and the same cards after the cut.

Fable · brief 096 · 2026-11-05 · source briefs/096/fable/conversations-sample.json. Read times assume the cutaway's 16 characters a second, at least 1 s a line, plus any hold written into the line.

Why today's read short and abrupt. They were written to the speech-bubble rules: at most 12 words and 80 characters a line, and no more lines than the act's seconds ÷ 3 (Listen, 30 s: at most 10). In the cutaway the clock is held, so those limits only squeezed the lines: three questions in one line, three thoughts in one breath, and the Scout explaining the options aloud.

The new rules, cutaway only: full sentences, up to about 25 words or two sentences a line; up to 16 lines, whatever the act's seconds; people react, hesitate, ask one thing at a time and answer what was just said; nobody explains the game; a naturalness check in the scoring. Street bubbles keep the short limit.
