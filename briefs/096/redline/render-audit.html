Render audit: the Handover zone in OldTown, play view

Redline (code and performance), brief 096, 2026-11-05. Sky's question: "what else can we do to improve performance and looks? We using shaders to their fullest on surfaces? What about materials and textures?" Read and measure only: no asset, setting or code was changed. Nothing committed.

Short answer

No, the surfaces don't use the shaders to their fullest. Of the 200 materials OldTown draws with, 140 have no base map. The Lit shader they run on can take normal, occlusion, detail and emission maps, but only a few materials use any of them. Draw calls aren't the problem: the play view is about 109 SetPass and 218 renderers. On the Air the frame is limited by the GPU's per-pixel work and the shadows. The best value is to add surface detail that costs almost nothing per pixel, and to remove the per-pixel work nobody can see.

  1. Baked vertex-colour AO from Blender: soft contact shade in every moulding, band and joint. It gives most of what SSAO promised for about 0 ms. (Vela, plus a small shader change.)
  2. Fit the shadow map to the play frame: sharper shadows, by about 1.3× now and up to about 2× with a frame-fitted camera stand-off, for the same or lower cost. (Glint, Latch)
  3. Take the windows and lobbies off clear-coat Complex Lit (3,068 material slots): they stop mirroring the teal-grey probe, which is the problem K2 fixed on the enamels, and lose a second specular lobe per pixel. (Vela, Glint)
  4. A painted albedo variation (one shared world-space tint texture): breaks up the flat colour that covers about 79 % of wall area, in the "less clean" pastel or comic direction. One texture fetch. Concept art first. (Vela, Glyph)
  5. Move the 27 matte Complex Lit materials to the Lit copy (14,733 slots, no visible change), then finish brief 095, strip unused variants and prewarm shaders. Fewer variants and fewer first-use hitches. (Vela, Splice, Latch)

Before any of these: the OldTown play view at zoom 10 has never been measured on the Air. Brief 086's sitting didn't run because the screen was locked, and brief 091 step 0 is still open. Every Air figure below is an estimate from the M5 Max counters and the older Air run (0.2.122); §4 lists what to measure once Sky OKs a sitting.

2 · Shaders

2.1 The cutaway copies

  • DK_LitCutaway and DK_ComplexLitCutaway (generated from URP 17 Lit / Complex Lit) are SRP-Batcher compatible: per-material values stay in URP's UnityPerMaterial via LitInput.hlsl, and the cut's values are globals. Both include DOTS.hlsl, so the GPU Resident Drawer can instance them. OK.
  • The cut is a multi_compile_fragment _ _DK_CUT. Only renderers the cut can reach get the discarding twin (FadeReach), so everything else keeps the GPU's hidden-surface removal. That matters on Apple's tile-based GPUs. Good design; keep it.
  • Two generations still ship side by side (Shaders/DecoKit/ and OldTownArt/DecoKit/Shaders/, different GUIDs and hlsl). Brief 095 records 7,488 variants for the newer cutaway against 1,152 for the old one at a cold compile. That costs build time and causes first-use hitches. It isn't a steady-state cost.
  • Unused keyword families still compiled into the forward pass: _LIGHT_COOKIES, _DBUFFER_MRT1/2/3 (no decal feature), _SCREEN_SPACE_IRRADIANCE, _ADDITIONAL_LIGHT_SHADOWS (one light), META_QUEST_*, and the soft-shadow LOW and MEDIUM variants when only HIGH is used. They can be stripped with an IPreprocessShaders step or the URP asset's stripping settings. Keep _SCREEN_SPACE_OCCLUSION: the perspective views keep SSAO.

2.2 Complex Lit vs Lit, after K2

Complex Lit without _CLEARCOAT runs the same BRDF as Lit. The only difference is the UniversalForwardOnly tag, which costs nothing extra under Forward+. So the 27 enamel materials K2 turned matte (clear coat keyword off, mask 0, environment reflections off) render the same on the Lit copy. One trap: the Lit copy reuses _ClearCoatMask as an environment-reflection scale (1 − mask, brief 028). It must stay 0, and it is 0 on all 27.

3 · Recommendations, ranked

Ranking: look gain per frame-time cost, then effort. "Air" figures are estimates at 1920×1080 until measured (§4). Tags: perf look effort.

1. Baked vertex-colour AO on kit pieces (Blender, at build time)

look: highAir: ≈ 0 to +0.1 mseffort: medium

What: tools/decokit bakes ambient occlusion (and optionally a cavity or edge term) into each piece's vertex colour: Cycles AO baked to a colour attribute, or a cheap ray-cast per vertex. The Lit cutaway multiplies albedo or occlusion by vertexColor.r behind a material keyword, as the leaf variant already does with _DK_VERTEX_COLOR.

Look: soft contact shade under cornices, in band joints, round window reveals, at the foot of bollards and benches. This is the "form" Glint wanted from SSAO. It also fits the pastel or comic look, because it's soft, hand-painted shade with no noise. It holds at every yaw, with no screen-space halos and no flicker when the cut opens.

Cost: no texture and no prepass. It adds one vertex attribute (4 bytes per vertex at 1.3 M triangles), and with occlusion on, a multiply in the existing indirect term. Check that DecoKitTileMerge / KitColumns CombineMeshes keep the colour channel when merging (all parts need it, or white is written).

Owner: Vela (bake in the DecoKit generator, pieces), Splice (kit import and hash), Latch (shader keyword in the generator make_cutaway_shaders.py, scene regen), Glint (strength). Done-check: before and after at zoom 10, yaw 30 and 210, sun and shade. Same SetPass, same triangles.

Baked AO in lightmaps for the static zone tiles is the alternative: higher fidelity across pieces (building-to-pavement contact), but it needs UV2 on every merged tile, lightmap memory (~30–60 MB for the zone at a useful texel density), a bake step in the scene build, and a re-bake whenever the zone moves. Vertex AO first. Try lightmapped AO only on the ground plane if building-to-pavement contact is still missing.

2. Fit the shadow map to the play frame

look: sharper shadows, 1.3×–2×Air: 0 to −0.5 mseffort: low (A) / medium (B)

Now: at play zoom 10 (frame about 35.6 × 20 m) the street cascade covers depth 100–200 m. URP's bounding sphere is r ≈ √(17.8² + 10² + 50²) ≈ 54 m, so 108 m across 2048 texels ≈ 5.3 cm per texel ≈ 2.9 screen px at 1080, then blurred by the 7×7 High tent. Cascade 1 (0–100 m) covers air in front of the street and holds only roofs taller than about 37 m.

A (tune, no code): the ground's depth spread across the frame at 38° is only ±12.8 m, so farBand 40 leaves about 27 m of empty depth. farBand 40 → about 16 makes r ≈ 43 m, ≈ 4.2 cm/texel (1.26× sharper) at the same cost. Scale the bands with zoom, so the planning zooms (14–18.5) keep their reach and play zoom 10 gets the tight fit.

B (frame-fitted stand-off): the ortho camera stands 160 m back to clear the tallest roof anywhere. Set the stand-off each frame from the merged tiles' bounds in the frame column (in the Handover zone about 70 m); moving an ortho camera along its axis doesn't change the image or the cut's capsules. Then 1 cascade over about 86 m: r ≈ 48 m on the full 4096 ≈ 2.3 cm/texel (about 2.3× sharper), and one shadow pass instead of two (fewer caster draws).

Then: with texels 2× smaller, try soft shadows Medium (9 taps) instead of High (16 taps). The penumbra in metres stays close to today's, and every lit pixel saves 7 shadow taps. Estimate on the Air: 0.2–0.5 ms. That's Glint's eye. Crisp shadows suit the comic look.

Owner: Glint (fit values, soft-shadow call), Latch (TacticalShadows bands per zoom; stand-off for B). Done-check: shots of the café terrace and the Records Office steps at yaw 30 and 210, no shadow swimming while panning (keep the texel snap), casters count, Studio A B A B.

3. Windows and lobbies off clear coat

look: medium (no teal-grey mirror)Air: −0.1 to −0.4 mseffort: low

DK_Window_Film, DK_Window_Tower and DK_Lobby (3,068 slots: the most-drawn surfaces after the enamels) are opaque Complex Lit with a full clear coat (mask 1) and environment reflections on. They mirror the same teal-grey street probe that K2 removed from the enamels. Options: (a) the Lit copy, smoothness about 0.8, reflections on; or (b) the existing DK_GlassSky treatment (already on 652 slots), which paints a sky reflection under the art direction and avoids the probe entirely. A night emission mask (lit and unlit panes) is the same material's next step.

Owner: Vela (rows in unity_mats.py / DecoKitMaterials.cs), Glint (sign-off). Done-check: before and after shots, day and night. Variant count down.

4. Painted albedo variation (one world-space tint texture)

look: high on walls and groundAir: +0.1 to +0.3 mseffort: medium; concept art first

A single shared 512–1024² tiling texture (soft sun-fade, faint wash, a hand-painted pastel mottle), sampled in world space (XZ on the ground, the face's major axes on walls) at a large scale (about 4–8 m per tile) and multiplied into the base colour by a per-material strength. It sits on the flat colour and the 072-F detail pair, so every enamel and stone face gets broad variation without UVs. It looks painted rather than procedural, the "less clean" direction from the 073 concepts. Keyword-free, behind the same global switch style as _SubornedSurfaceDetailOff, so the Air A/B is clean.

Owner: Vela (texture via Codex stills first, then baked), Glyph (keeps signals readable over it), Latch (shader). Rule: concept art to Sky before any texture is built.

5. Matte enamels to the Lit copy; finish brief 095; strip variants; prewarm

Air: ≈ 0 ms steady; fewer hitcheslook: noneeffort: low to medium
  • The 27 matte Complex Lit materials go to DecoKit/Lit Cutaway: their rows in tools/decokit/unity_mats.py (kit) and DecoKitMaterials.cs (old town) ask for the Lit shader when coat == 0. Check _ClearCoatMask stays 0 (it scales reflections in the Lit copy).
  • Brief 095: one canonical shader family. Then a variant-stripping pass (§2.1 list) and a count before and after.
  • Prewarm: Unity 6's GraphicsStateCollection (PSO tracing and warm-up) or a ShaderVariantCollection recorded from a proof run, warmed behind the start page. The Air's loud-fight max of 57.7 ms (briefs/064/air-0122) is the kind of spike first-use pipeline compiles cause. Check it with the frames CSV before claiming that.

Owner: Vela (material rows), Splice (stripping step, variant count in the build report), Latch (prewarm, regen). Done-check: identical shots (pixel diff), SetPass at play, variant count, first-fight p99 and max on the Studio.

6. Render-scale policy: test FSR against bilinear at the cap

look: sharper at the same costAir: 0 ms at equal scaleeffort: low

RenderCap caps the 3D pass at the Air's pixel count (5.37 Mpx), so Sky's 4812×2706 Studio display draws at 0.65 (3128×1759), and the Air's shipped size draws at 0.75 of 3840×2410. The upscaling filter is Auto, which is bilinear at non-integer scales. FSR 1 (spatial, with sharpening) costs about one full-screen pass and gives crisper edges and HUD-adjacent detail at the same 3D cost. Avoid STP or any temporal upscaler: the cut's dither and the 1 px signal shapes would smear. The 086 kit already holds fsr.rows.json and scale.rows.json: run them in the same Air sitting. Remember the bar is measured at 1920×1080 (2.07 Mpx), while the Air's shipped full screen draws about 5.2 Mpx, 2.5× more pixels. The scale Sky plays at is his look call. Owner: Latch (asset filter switch), Sky (look).

8. Checked and fine: don't spend time here

  • Draw calls and instancing: 109 SetPass and 218 renderers at play. The tiles are merged at build time; the GPU Resident Drawer (instanced) is on with static batching off and BRG variants kept; every custom lit shader includes DOTS.hlsl. Street furniture is already instanced or merged.
  • SRP Batcher: all custom shaders declare UnityPerMaterial (directly or through LitInput.hlsl). FacadeLit takes it from its include.
  • Texture compression and mips: Standalone overrides are BC7 (colour and masks) and BC5 (normals), mipmaps on. Apple-silicon Metal samples BC natively, so ASTC would change nothing on the Mac. The kit caps 4096 sources at 2048 in the player; that's the import's decision, not a cut (§12 rules: no downscaling without Sky).
  • LODs: 2,192 LOD groups. At play the triangle count (1.29 M) is far under the 2.5 M gate; the triangle problem is third person (brief 086), not the play view.
  • People: figures are separate skinned draws (about 20 in the zone), under 1 % of the frame. The crowd at scale is a later question.
  • CPU, host and HUD (UI Toolkit): main thread 3.7 ms on the Studio, 6–9 ms on the Air against a 15–21 ms frame, so the play view is GPU-bound. Live widgets repaint every frame (HudToolkit.Live.cs MarkDirtyRepaint on the cue, eye and stance per agent); worth one Profiler capture on the Air's development build, but not ahead of the GPU items.
  • SSAO: rejected by Sky. The feature stays off on the ortho camera (TacticalShadows); not recommended again. Rec. 1 is the cheap substitute.
