# 091 · Trim sheets: texture every wall without paying for it

Owner: Aster. Opened 2026-11-03. Sky asked whether the game shares textures and meshes (trim sheets) and whether their absence is why it's so expensive, then said "yea" to this brief.

## Why
- The kit already shares materials: about 370 palette materials (`DK_Enamel_*`, `DK_Limestone`, `DK_Chrome`…) serve thousands of pieces, and play zoom draws about 111 SetPass against a limit of 375. Draw calls are not the problem.
- There are no trim sheets. DecoKit models its detail as geometry (bands, bevels, mouldings, `trims()` in `tools/decokit/skyline.py`); `DK_Chrome` alone is about 142k triangles in the civic block (`briefs/077/build/numbers-day.json`).
- 79 % of the wall area seen in the play streets is the flat procedural facade: one colour per face, no maps (brief 072, Vela). Sky, 2026-10-27: "Anywhere with a flat color, no texture, we need to give it one, as long as it doesn't hurt performance".
- Redline (brief 086): at play zoom the Air is GPU- and pixel-bound; in third person the cost is triangles (6.9–7.9 M against 6 M), mostly because the old town's 9,251 placed pieces have no LODs. No Air frame time exists yet for OldTown at zoom 10 and 1080.

So trim sheets are the cheap way to meet brief 072's texture goal and to move detail from triangles into maps. They are not expected to fix the play-zoom frame time on their own.

## Objective
Find out, on one building and then the play streets, whether a shared facade trim sheet looks at least as good as DecoKit (the quality floor, `briefs/026/old-decokit`) while costing no more frame time than today's flat walls, and fewer triangles where it replaces modelled detail.

## Steps
0. **Air baseline first (Splice, Sky).** Run the brief 086 Air sitting on the current build: OldTown, zoom 10, 1920 × 1080, play, tp-street and tp-worst. Sky unlocks the Air and keeps it awake for about 45 minutes. This is the number every later step compares against.
1. **Concept art (Vela, Glyph for the sheet layout).** Two or three concept stills of a street wall dressed only from a trim sheet: cream stone courses, seafoam bands, a thin brass line, window and door surrounds, a cornice and a plinth. Codex image generation first, at $0, through `tools/paid/paid.py codex`. Show Sky the pictures; he picks before any sheet is built.
2. **The sheet (Vela).** One façade trim sheet, made at build time in `tools/decokit` (Blender bake from modelled strips, so the maps match the kit's own geometry): albedo, normal and mask (metal, AO, smoothness), power-of-two size, horizontal strips that tile on U. Colour stays in the palette materials or a tint per strip, so one sheet serves every colourway (brief 074).
3. **One building (Vela, Latch to place it).** Re-dress the procedural facade of one civic-block building on the trim sheet: walls, bands and surrounds as UV-mapped strips on flat quads in place of modelled detail where the camera can't tell. Before and after: screenshots at play zoom (yaw 30 and 210) and third person, triangles, SetPass and texture memory.
4. **Cost (Redline).** Frame time on the Studio for the one building, A B A B against the flat facade. Then, if Sky likes the look, one Air row for the play streets.
5. **Roll out (only after Sky's OK on 3–4).** Every procedural facade in the play streets, then the district; the kit pieces whose modelled bands and mouldings are the heaviest (chrome first) are candidates to move onto the sheet.

## Done-check
- Air baseline row on OldTown at zoom 10, 1080, in `briefs/091/` (or the reason it couldn't run).
- Concept stills shown to Sky, and his pick recorded here with the date.
- The trim sheet generated by a `bun run decokit` step, deterministic, its files hash-checked in `contracts/world/kit.json` like any kit piece.
- One building on the sheet, with before/after shots and a cost table (triangles, SetPass, texture MB, Studio frame time), in a self-contained `.html` report.
- Pass bar: looks no worse than DecoKit to Sky; frame time no worse than the flat facade (within run noise); triangles equal or lower.

## Rules that hold
Concept art first; Sky sees pictures before any look decision. Exteriors only. No AI at runtime: the sheet is made at build time. Paid APIs only if Codex fails, and say why. Files stay under 500 lines. Delta only: the batch runs just what the change touches. No load while Sky is playing (`bun run guard`).
