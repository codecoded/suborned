Brief 098, law L9: Bodies

Keel, 2026-11-07, engine suborned.sim/0.46.0, branch t3/simulation-body-spacing. Sky (2026-11-07, three guards inside one another on a call box and the rival crew on one spot): "our people bunch up together too much - we need to think about real tactics from this, and ensuring NPC's don't overlap each, they have real boundaries."

In short. Every person on the street has a body, and in no tick of the law's tests or of 315 probe runs across the dial did two bodies on their feet stand inside two radii. A stop holds one person and the next queues or stands beside it. Guards sharing a post take crew spots 2.5 m out, each facing his own sector. The rival crew at the Office front spreads 2.5 m apart, fanned over the street. The game's balance barely moves: wins by dial setting change by −1.3 to +1.7 points. The tick costs about 0.11 ms at the dial's x4 (it was 0.02–0.03). Mission record c056 holds. Some calls are Sky's; they are listed at the end.

How the law works

Bodies

  • Who has one: everyone upright on the street: the cast, the patrol, the cell, the rival crew, fight bodies, the response unit's officers, police units and the city's people, the dial's added guards among them. A body on the ground stays where it fell. Anyone carried, inside, or in a car is off the street.
  • Size (rulebook bodies, marked proposed): radius 30 cm, so no two bodies on their feet stand closer than 60 cm centre to centre. That equals fight.spacing.clear (60 cm) and the crowd occlusion's body width. The rulebook refuses a fight.spacing.clear under two radii, so the fight's spacing and the law cannot disagree.
  • When: at the end of every tick, after every rule has moved its people, and once at the start. Each body's offset from the place its own rules give it (its lay) is stored and read by its pose through the next tick: CrowdPos, MemberPose, Hostile.Pos, SecOfficerPose, UnitPose, GuardPose, DriverPos, ValePos, PersonPos, PatrolPose and an operative's Pos. Rules and host read the same spaced bodies. A lay belongs to the plan it was laid on: when a rule plans again (a new walk, a stop, a fright) it plans from where the body stands.
  • Order and the guarantee: tenets are laid first and never moved, then the armed and the cell, then the city's people. Within each, those standing come before those walking. Every body keeps clear of where every other stood last tick, so waiting where it stood always fits and the law is never forced into an overlap. The only exception is two tenets placed together; that is counted and came to 0. Integer cm, fixed candidate lists, a fixed order, a 1 m spatial hash, no floats and no general solver: the same seed and orders lay the same bodies, and lays are in the checkpoint hash.

Space

  • The city's people keep personal space by choice where there is room: 80 cm walking, and 80 cm from anyone standing. In a queue at one stop it is the queue's step, 70 cm. When there is no room they fall back to two radii.
  • The cell and the armed are on business and keep two radii, as the fight's spacing does.
  • Moving: each body heads for its own place. If that isn't clear it takes the first clear goal from a fixed list: hold its lay, a stop's formation, a sidestep, rings round its place. It moves at 4 m/s when it must make room now, at a walk to a spot it chose, and eases back to its own place at 0.6 m/s. It slides round whoever is in the way, up to 90° off its course.
  • A body behind its own place follows its own walk, not a straight line through walls. The patrol follows its reroute and alarm walks the same way.
  • Held: a body held for a second (it stood still or slid to and fro) does one of two things. If two bodies hold each other, the one with the lesser right of way gives way: the city's people before the armed, then the law's order. It backs 2 m down the way it came for 2 s. Otherwise it walks round, on the walk grid, with the cells of the bodies near it shut. A way much longer than the straight line is refused at first, as in a queue at a door, and each second held allows 4 m more. The last resort: a body that gets nowhere for 10 s, whatever it tries, rejoins its own place. It is counted (BodyRejoins). On today's settings it happened once in 45 probe runs, and it is listed below.

Places

  • A stop takes one body. The next queues behind it in 70 cm steps, up to five, then stands in an arc beside and behind it.
  • A chair holds one sitter from the moment they sit until they leave. A person due in a chair someone still sits in waits beside it.
  • A door lets one person out of a refuge at a time, a queue's step apart: a crowd coming back out of shelter files out instead of appearing on top of itself.
  • Guards sharing a post point. The dial's added guards still take separate points before any share. When two share, the one standing nearest the point holds it. Each other guard takes the nearest free crew spot in sight of it (no wall between) and keeps it while he stays: 250 cm out at ±60°, ±120° and 180°, then an outer ring at 500 cm staggered between those. Each faces his spot's sector: ±45°, ±90°, behind. When no spot is free he keeps personal space nearby.

Groups spread, with tactics

  • The rival crew drawn to the Office front stands 250 cm apart along one line (it was 90 cm). The middle man faces out from the front to the street, the next ones turn 45° out to their side, the flanks 90°.
  • The response unit at the end of its walk and police units at their destination stand at crew spots round the place, each facing his own sector.
  • Fights keep brief 081's rules: the kite's step off the line, cover and fight.spacing. A man stepping off the line of fire is the fight's.

The tenets, which win where they conflict

  • Inez and the door guard's meeting ring: he stands at it and the law never moves him off it.
  • The server at a table, and anyone seated, is a tenet. The driver at his car and the cast standing at their places are never moved.
  • Vale's walk: standing at his place he is never moved; walking, he steps round people as the armed do. Vale held is a metre off the Enforcer and never laid apart, the escort grip. The carried go wherever the carrier goes.
  • The cell's orders: an operative's walk waits for his body, so it ends where he arrives. If whoever holds its end lets him come no nearer, it ends within two radii and a step (1.4 m), for example when a car has pulled in on it. An operative at work, or leading Vale, is a tenet.
  • The takedown's reach is unchanged. The target stands where the law laid them, and a player's takedown out of reach walks in, as before.

What Latch must change in Unity

  • Positions now come spaced through the same reads (the poses listed above). No new call is needed, and figures stop standing in one another. Expect people beside their stop (queue, arc, crew spots) rather than on it, guards sharing a post 2.5 m apart, and the rival crew at the front 2.5 m apart.
  • Facings: guards at a shared post, the rival crew at the front and spread officers face their sectors through the existing facing reads. HostCrowdSpread (Runtime/Play, the eye-only spread of people the engine put on one point, 0.7 m spots) is no longer needed: the engine now spaces them, so turn it off or it spreads them a second time. The same goes for the relief's step that folds small jumps into the drawn spread.
  • New host reads, optional and never hashed: BodiesNow(), BodyInfo(id) (where it stands, its own place, its stop, class, form, sector facing, crew spot), BodyLayOf(id), BodyIsTenet(id), BodyStrain(), BodyOverlapsNow, BodyRejoins. R.BodyRadiusCm (30) is the figures' footprint for selection and contact.
  • A last-resort rejoin moves a body to its own place in one tick. Blend it (a short fade or a cut while off camera); don't animate a walk.
  • The rulebook changed, so the inputs hash changed: 0.45.0 saves and recordings won't replay on 0.46.0.

  • Sky: the cell no longer walks through crowds. Its walks step round or wait; the cell was held under 0.1 s a run in the probe. Dense crowds (people=64) need the last resort about 7 times a run; whether a person rejoining their place now and then is acceptable is a feel call.
  • Vantage/Datum (the site): the gap between Vale's car stand at the official entrance and the facade is one body wide, and a staff post in the recess there (p-records-office-74, at 76.25, −30.0 m) pinches it further.
