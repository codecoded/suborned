The laws

Fighters hold cover (rulebook fight.cover)

One law for every fighter in a fight: the rival crew, police officers, the response unit and the police units. The blocker keeps his lane, as he does.

  1. A fighter who reaches cover that shields him from the threat holds it while it still does. He leaves only for a reason: flanked or exposed (unshielded from his target for leave, 2 s, or not shielded from a shooter he can't answer), his crew's plan (an advance, the press, the walk to a held car, the break), his target out of reach or sight for lapse (1 s; then he advances, and the advance closes), or the cover lost.
  2. A move out of a shooter's line commits him to where he moved. On the walk and once there, he takes no new step out of that shooter's line while the place still shields him from where the shooter stands now, nor out of another's line it shields him from too (Gun.Commit). A walk to a blind point holds no cover point.
  3. Cover is somewhere to fight from. A fighter whose target is in his reach steps only to cover within reach (1000 ‰ of his weapon's effective reach) of that target, with the line to him. With none, he fights from where he stands.
  4. A move for a reason is walked through. An advance (on the car or an operative) and a police body's close are never turned back by a step out of a line. On the walk of a move out of a line, his target out of reach is no reason yet. A foe in reach still stops him, and he fights.

New setting: cover-hold-reach-pct 1000. leave and lapse are ai.cover.leave and ai.engageLapse, one value each. Code: GameCoverHold.cs (new), GameAi.cs, GameKite.cs, RulesCoverHold.cs (new).

A doorway is a way (rulebook bodies.door)

Every entrance has its way: the ground in front of its door, out to depth (300 cm) along the open side of the foot ground round it, and half (80 cm) either side of the door's line, two people abreast. A guard at a door takes a post beside it. The post is the nearest point to the door on its open side that stands a body's radius outside every door's way. It leaves a passage of two personal spaces (160 cm, centre to centre) from the door's own people: the cast stands, the door's waiting points and its going-in point. No beat point stands in a door's way. A guard at a post takes no place in one. The door's own people keep their places (the cast's door guard stands at his door). Code: DoorWays.cs (new), Population.cs, GameBodyForms.cs, GameBodySides.cs. population.json's beat.doorOutCm is retired.
