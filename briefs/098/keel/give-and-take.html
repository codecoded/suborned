Brief 098, law L9: give and take

Keel, 2026-11-07, engine suborned.sim/0.47.0, branch t3/simulation-body-spacing (on L9's d8354c1a). Sky, on L9's report: "Do all but the jumping back to spot should be better to dealt with". Kept: bodies 60 cm apart at the closest, personal space 80 cm (70 in a queue), the cell steps round people or waits. Gone: the last resort (a body held 10 s put back on its own place in one tick), and no fade or off-camera cut in its place.

In short. Nobody jumps. In 315 probe runs across the dial (63 million tick-to-tick moves watched) no body moved farther in a tick than its own pace, and no two bodies on their feet stood inside two radii except two counted squeezing past each other, never closer than 40 cm. On the same probe, engine 0.46.0 made 13,000 to 94,000 such jumps per setting, and its last resort fired 315 times at people=64.

What a player sees in their place:
  • people step aside to let someone by, then step back;
  • in a gap one person wide, two people turn side-on and pass slowly;
  • someone who can't get to their table takes another, or waits nearby;
  • someone whose way is jammed goes round the block, or picks another door when the one they ran for is packed;
  • a person held back walks to their door before they go in, so nobody vanishes in the street.
The game's balance barely moves: wins against 0.46.0 change by −2.3 to +0.3 points across the seven settings. The tick costs the same as L9 (0.09–0.10 ms at x4) and its worst ticks are lighter. Mission record c058 holds.

How the law works now

Nobody jumps (BodyPaceCmS). Every body the law lays moves at most its own pace on foot a tick, and the law's own pushes are capped there too:

  • the cell and the cast at the cell's run (3.6 m/s);
  • the city's people at the city's run (2.6 m/s);
  • the rival crew and fight bodies at a rival's following pace (2.8 m/s);
  • officers and police at security's pace (2.2 m/s);
  • the patrol and robots at a robot's (0.85 m/s).

In 0.46.0 a push could reach 4 m/s plus the body's own step.

  • Someone new to the street (out of a door, a car or a refuge) is placed, as before.
  • Someone already on the street whose own place jumps walks there from where they stood. Causes: a fright's end, a leg timed faster than a run, a new walk, a patrol switching jobs. When a wall is between, they take the walk grid's way back (a Catch). Before, the law laid them at the new place at once, which is about 80 rule-made jumps a run on today's settings.
  • A person of the city leaves the street only from the place they leave it: a door, a refuge, the sheet's edge. Held back when their routine takes them in, they walk to the door first (within two radii and a step of it they go). Before, they vanished wherever they were held.
  • A body walks off ground no body stands on: a car pulled in on it, or a door point in a facade its own place left it on. It also steps into its own place when that is off the free ground, for example a post in a recess.

What Latch must draw

  • No more blends. Nothing ever moves faster than its own run now, so remove the blend L9 asked for at a rejoin. BodyRejoins is gone from the engine.
  • Same pose reads. Positions keep coming through them (CrowdPos, MemberPose, Hostile.Pos, SecOfficerPose, UnitPose, an operative's Pos). The give and take needs no new call to place people.
  • Squeeze. BodySqueeze(id) gives (with whom, facing). The facing is across the way the person was going, toward the other, and FacingNow(id) already returns it for walkers. Draw a shoulder turn (the figure yawed side-on, shoulders along the gap) and a slow side-step: the engine moves them at 0.5 m/s, and the two pass 40 cm apart centre to centre.
  • Making room. BodyMove(id).RoomFor is who they stand aside for, and RoomAt the spot. It's an ordinary walk to the side and back; a glance at the one passing would sell it.
  • Changes of plans. BodyMove(id).Replanned, Alt (where they went instead) and AltChair (a chair they took instead of their own). Where the host seats people by their routine's chair, it must seat them in AltChair when set; FacingNow gives that chair's facing.
  • Going in. A person whose routine has gone inside can still be on the street, walking to the door. CrowdPos keeps returning them until they are within two radii and a step of it, so draw whatever it returns; don't hide people by the routine.
  • Other reads (never hashed): BodySidesteps, BodySqueezes, BodyReplans / BodyReplansBy (counts), BodyHeld(id), BodyKeepsPlace(id).
  • Inputs hash. The rulebook changed, so 0.46.0 saves and recordings won't replay on 0.47.0.
