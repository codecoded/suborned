Keel, 2026-11-08, engine suborned.sim/0.48.0, branch t3/int-098-data (Aster’s integration of Pulse’s people and rounds and Vantage’s car stand and palms, on work/098-living-playbook). Every “before” is engine 0.47.1 (835c71bf) on this same data, built from its own archive; every “after” is 0.48.0.

What a player sees.

  • The Office door is no longer a scrum round the door guard. The clerks coming out at the shift change, the dial’s visitors and anyone sheltering there after shots now go in and come out 80 cm to his front-left, not through him. On the forecourt, holds of 5 s or more fall 12 → 2 at today’s settings, 35 → 3 at people=64 and 79 → 30 at x4; the longest 37.6 → 16.7 s and 73.5 → 20.0 s.
  • At the café door people go in at the north end of the doorway, 81 cm from Inez, instead of on her shoulder.
  • Less changing your mind on the spot. A shuffle (three moves straight back within 2 s) happens about half as often on every setting: 7,058 → 3,714 over the 315 probe runs, 3,358 → 1,157 at people=128. Moves straight back fall 87,558 → 57,700. A body stepped aside now keeps to that side for a moment, and a spot the law picked for it stays picked while it is clear; before, a walker between two others re-chose right or left every tick and swung 13 cm a tick for two seconds.
  • Guards, police, robots and officers step aside for each other, as the city does; enemies never move for each other. At guards=64 the probe’s holds of 5 s or more fall 41 → 6 and the longest 54.0 → 28.4 s. Vantage’s police-3, 81.6 s behind a response robot, was on his branch’s data and does not recur on this branch’s; the law that would have freed it is in: the give-and-take test counts the armed making room for their own side (robots and police for guards among them) and fails any that make room for an enemy.
  • Nobody jumps, no pair stands inside two radii and the engine’s own overlap and tenet counts are 0 on all seven standard settings (315 runs). At people=128, outside the 315, pairs inside two radii fall 593 → 4 (on 0.47.1 this data jammed the Office forecourt at the shift change: 14,115 of the law’s own overlaps).
  • The bands hold: wins move −0.7 to +0.7 points by setting; the mission record c060 holds with the same red lines as c059.

What still looks wrong (the main remaining long holds, all at the café front): the cell standing across the café’s one lane while it works Inez. In quiet-a the Scout and the Fixer stand 85 cm apart between the east palm and the tables; nobody can pass between them, and by today’s law the cell never makes room for the city. Inez waits 32 s behind them (today’s settings), a customer 84 s (people=64), a passer-by 117 s (x4); 0.47.1 had 32.2, 70.8 and 117.2 s. This round moves who waits longest, not the jam. Whether an idle operative makes room for the city (an agent who is undercover would) is a design call for Sky and Pulse: it is not in this brief, so I did not make it.

A body keeps to one side for a moment (GameBodySides.cs)A slide round someone never swings back toward the other side for a moment, as long as a held body waits before it looks for a way round (1 s): 0.47 remembered only the last tick, so slide, wait, slide straight back was allowed. A spot the law picked for a body (a sidestep, a ring round its place, a queue’s spot) is kept while it stays clear by the standard it was picked by (personal space, or two radii), instead of picked again each tick; its own place clear again takes it back. It forgets once it gets on, takes another way, makes room or plans again. Not the cell’s spots: an operative’s walk waits for his body, so a spot kept from it swung with him (the Enforcer carrying a body in N52 swung 17 cm a tick). Tried first: holding every move to its side; it cut shuffles by two thirds but packed crowds harder (people=128: 1,844 law overlaps), so it was dropped.
The armed make room for their own side (Aster, after Vantage’s police-3)The cell for the cell, security for security (the patrol, the city’s and the dial’s guards, officers, police and robots, the fight’s police), the rival crew for the crew; never for an enemy, never a tenet (the patrol on its sweep is one, as the rulebook says), never one carrying someone. Face to face in a gap, the lesser right of way still backs off as before. Making room now ends once the one it made room for stands settled at its own place, or its own way has moved on (a guard taking over a post never passes the one leaving it; 0.47 kept that one aside the full 6 s while his beat walked 5.8 m on).
Found on the wayA response officer or a rival on foot joining the final fight keeps his body: 0.47 placed his fight body afresh on its walk’s first cell, up to half a metre off (a jump the probe found once officers began stepping aside for each other). A seated person’s held count resets: 0.47 kept the count they sat down with, so seated people showed as held in every “held body-seconds” figure (Vantage’s tables included). Compare spells (5 s+, 10 s+, longest), not held body-seconds, across 0.47 and 0.48.

The rulebook’s bodies.rule says all three in words; no value is new (the moment is the law’s own DetourTicks; the clearance is bodies.space; the reach is two radii and space).
