What Sky gets

  • The panel, bottom left on the street beside the start page (the book's Job page at 0:00), only while the start is up: per kind (Guards, People) −10, −, the number, +, +10, today's number with the fewest (the cast never leave) and the most beside it; Today puts both back; Play › builds the street with those numbers on the same seed and begins. The page's own Begin does the same once a number changed, so no start ever plays numbers the panel no longer shows. Clicks only: no key does any of it. A changed number reads in turquoise; the line under says Changed: Play builds this street, or what plays now (Now: guards 19 (+3) · people 150 (+36)). Pictures: pics/start-panel-more.png, pics/probe-dial-2-changed.png.
  • Which counts a game plays. Replay and Restart keep the counts of the game just played (as they keep its seed). The panel's Play takes its numbers. Else -counts as given. Else, for a person playing, the numbers last played (PlayerPrefs suborned.dial.guards, suborned.dial.people, so the numbers come back at the next launch). A proof, a batch row or a probe plays today's world unless it passes -counts, and never reads or writes those numbers (HostSeed's rule for the seed; HostNight's for the night look).
  • Unchanged numbers are no counts. A kind at today's number is left out of the start; a start with none set is today's game byte for byte (the probe: Today then Play gives the first game's start hash exactly).
  • Saved with the run. The counts are in the start ("start": {"seed": …, "counts": {…}}), written by the engine's Script.Write like the seed, so seed + counts + orders replay; Continue rebuilds them.
  • The census goes to the player log at every start: per kind asked, plays, today's, the fewest and the most, the note, who was added and who left.
  • -counts guards=19,people=150 (':' works for '=' too) for the shot rig and perf runs; an unknown kind is refused (unknown = reject).
  • The added people are walkers like anyone: guard and passer-by figures through the job families (no pool by id), walk, sit at free café seats, run and lie down; the hover reads them through the engine (a guard, a passer-by); the views are built from the game's walkers at every start, so a street rebuilt with fewer keeps no view of anyone who left.
