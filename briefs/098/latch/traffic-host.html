What Sky gets

  • The Cars row on the start page's dial, under Guards and People: −10, −, the number, +, +10, with "today 0 · 0–64". Play builds the street with that many cars on the same seed (71 ms at 8 cars in the probe) and begins. A count back at 0 is no traffic: today's game byte for byte. The count is saved in the run beside the seed (Replay and Restart keep it); -counts cars=16 sets it for the shot rig; a person's last numbers come back at the next launch, cars included. Picture: pics/traffic/start-page-cars8.png ("Now: guards 16 · people 114 · cars 8 (+8)").
  • The cars, from the engine every tick (Game.TrafficNow), eased between ticks like the mission cars, heading included:
    • a sedan is one of the mission cars' own bodies (Sedan_Official, Sedan_Getaway) in Pulse's palette (presentation.json paints, the kit's DK_CarPaint_* materials, swapped into the body and roof slots as kit.json carPaints says). The body and paint come from the car's id, so neighbours differ and a replay draws the same street;
    • three of Pulse's eleven paints are kept off every passing sedan because they would pass for a mission car: tropical-turquoise (CIEDE2000 11.3 from Vale's teal), dusk-pink (13.6) and tangerine (14.3 from our coral). The two bodies share one chassis and read alike from above, so the rule holds for both. The first cut (a per-body CIE76 rule) let a turquoise-and-cream getaway body through, and on the road it looked like Vale's car;
    • the rivals' plum Sedan_Rival is not used for traffic: before 2:35 it would read as the rivals arriving;
    • a van is the old town's box truck (DK_BoxTruck). BoxTruck_Play is still a kit candidate, not copied into Unity; once Vela adopts it, the build takes it with no code change;
    • a scooter is Vela's hover scooter, 0.35 m up with a slow bob;
    • the kit cars are one mesh each with the wheels in it, so nothing spins.
    A car that leaves gives its view back for the next trip of the same model, so a warm street instantiates nothing.
  • What they do (the engine's law, drawn): they keep their lanes, wait for people crossing, queue at junctions, brake at the first shot, then flee or duck by the driver's nature, and drive out at a lockdown while the cars outside wait at the edges. A driver who gets out joins the crowd and is drawn as one of the crowd. The crowd's spread grows in place, so nobody else's spot jumps.
  • The hover: the HUD's one hover card, the verb pill. With no way offered on the car (the Scout, say), it says what the car is and what it is doing, in plain words read from the engine (TrafficWhy and the car's state): "Van · waiting for someone to cross", "Van · waiting at the junction", "Scooter · following a car", "Passing car · driving through", "… · braking hard", "… · the driver is ducking", "… · fleeing the shooting", "… · pulled up for the lockdown", "… · wrecked, the driver ran", "… · on fire". With an armed agent selected, it shows the Fire offer as for Vale's car ("Fire at the passing car" / "the van" / "the scooter"). Picture: pics/traffic/cars16-hover-van.png.
  • A car is a car: the pick (and its 28 px near-pick), the fire order, the fire line's name, the reticle, the car effects (smoke, fire, the blast) and the engine's cover all take a passing car as they take the mission cars. The host's hit-chance preview now reads the engine's live cover when traffic runs, so a standing passing car counts there too; without traffic it reads the site's cover as before.
  • Sound: a quiet engine loop on the nearest moving passing cars, at most four at once. It reuses the rival car's loop (cues.json rival-car-engine: its clip, bus, 3D distances and speed bounds) at 35 % of its volume, with pitch by speed (the scooter higher, the van lower). It pauses with the clock and stays silent with the sound off. Chime can tune it or swap in a pack cue.
  • A fix on the way: the car effects' char (brief 078, HostViews.Scorch) wrote one colour block per renderer, so every material slot of a kit car (tyres, glass, chrome) took the body's colour once the car was hit. That hit Vale's car and ours too. It is now one block per slot: a hit car keeps its chrome, whitewalls and glass under the smoke. Picture: pics/traffic/probe-shot-car.png.
