  • A wreck pushed aside slides. A mission car on its leg pushes a wreck 15 cm a tick, and the views' ease draws that. A Go is different: the engine pushes each wreck on the way out off it on the Go's tick (up to 6 m), and for our car that tick ends the mission. So the wreck stays where it stood until our leaving car's body comes up to it. Then it slides to its pushed place, eased, at the push's speed or the car's. The leaving car brakes to 5 m/s while it shoves the wreck, then drives on. If the car passes it by, or is not drawn, the wreck slides on its own at 1.5 m/s. The same holds for Popovic's car leaving (the lend). This is HostViews.TrafficShove. The Leaving views apply the brake.
