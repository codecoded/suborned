Why the squeeze is a turn, not a side-step

Every figure imports with Optimize Game Objects (brief 069 A: Redline, about 15 % of a person's frame), so the skeleton lives inside the Animator and only Head and Hips stay as transforms. A pass after the clip can't turn the spine, and Animation Rigging's constraints work on bone transforms the crowd's figures no longer have (and would put a rig on every one of about 130 figures for a two-second event). The light route left is the figure's own yaw over the existing walk. A true side-step needs a strafe pair in the clip set (the Mixamo library on disk has Left_Strafe_Walk and Right_Strafe_Walking): Forma's.
