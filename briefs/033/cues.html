2 · The nine states, with their cues (3x, and true size beside)

Motion loops every 3 s here so you can see it; in play the motion runs only while the state holds (held at least 1 s, 033's priority table; the first true wins). Each state has a different shape as well as its motion: orbit arc, ripple, spiked ring, rays at the gun, a cracked ring with a shake, a dashed heartbeat ring, a slash, a one-off sweep.

3 · True size, as the player sees it

64 px, 1:1. Squint test: you can tell downed (slash, grey), under fire (spikes), badly hurt (dashed ring, one pip), hurt (crack, one pip hollow) and success (a sweep) from calm without reading the face; calm, focused and noticed differ by the orbit arc and the eye badge.

4 · Rules

questionanswer [S, tune by play]
priorityAs brief 033: downed, badly hurt, under fire, hurt, firing, noticed, success, focused, calm; the first true wins; held at least 1 s. The eye badge, the sidearm mark, the busy chip and the pips are not states: they show whenever they are true.
noticedTrue when this agent's highest AttentionStage is 1 or more (never the cone test). The state cue is the ripple and the eye badge appears with it; at stage 2 and 3 the badge adds ticks, the ripple repeats once per step up. It cools with the 2 s delay.
under fireA shot at this agent in the last 1.5 s. The ring is replaced by 8 short spikes that jitter at 4 Hz.
firingThree rays at the sidearm corner, flickering at 2 Hz, in time with the shots; the sidearm mark is filled.
hurtOn a hit: one lost pip flashes alarm-orange and goes hollow, the ring shows a crack (a zigzag across the rim) for 0.6 s and the face shakes 2 px once. It then shows the health baseline.
badly hurtHealth at most a third: the ring is dashed and beats 1 Hz (thickness swell), the remaining pips pulse.
downedThe face desaturates to 20 % and a diagonal slash crosses it; the ring turns solid dark; all pips hollow; the sidearm mark hides. Held until carried, rescued or the mission ends.
successA beat of 2 s: one bright arc sweeps the ring once (0.6 s) and three short ticks sit at the top.
focusedA short arc orbits the ring (2 s a turn) while a Watch, Listen, talk or timed act runs; the busy chip carries the seconds.
budget0 added at rest: calm draws nothing. The layer sits inside the existing 64 px portrait plus a 4 px halo and the 14 px eye badge. No text on any portrait except the key cap and a seconds count on the shouted sidearm mark.
colourCream and ink for shape, the cell's turquoise for selection (existing), and the alarm orange only on the hurt flash and the pip flash, where the shape already says it. Greyscale check is on this page.
