1 · The quick click: the chip says what it will do

A. Fixer selected, Inez's temperament known: Pay is the fitting way, so a quick right click pays. The chip is the one that exists today (verb, one time for walk plus act, $, Trail, Alert, the eye), with a three-dot mark at its end: other ways are one hold away. The cursor takes the ring shape.
B. Gun drawn, a hostile under the cursor: the cursor is a crosshair, the chip is the fight chip (down in 4 s, hits, Trail, Alert), there is no ring. Press fires; holding that press is the going-loud hold. The portrait mark is filled.

3 · Draw and holster: the portrait mark

Four states on each armed agent's portrait, bottom-left of the plate, 28 px. 1 holstered (hollow); 2 drawn (filled, turquoise); 3 a watcher shouted: the mark pulses with the seconds left to holster (click it or press F); 4 the engine will holster after the fight (small dotted ring), only for guns drawn by a click. A click on the mark toggles that agent; F toggles the selection.

4 · Keys on the roster and the plate

Portraits carry 1 to 4 (select; in third person, take control). The action plate of the selected agent carries ⇧1 to ⇧4 on its slots and a small Tab glyph at its edge. Tab and Shift+Tab walk the slots (none is the contextual default) and never move the camera or the selection. Z steps targets. In third person ⇧1 to ⇧4 work only while standing.
