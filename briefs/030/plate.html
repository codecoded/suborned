Brief 030 · how far the action plate can shrink

Glyph, 2026-10-21. Asked by Sky: "I don't really like the plate; it's too disconnected from the context of the action for me." Acting already lives on the contextual right click and the ring at the target (brief 030), so the plate is what is left to justify. Screens are 1080p at half scale (960 x 540); detail frames are 1:1. No code, data or GDD edited. Rule it answers to: GDD §10 HUD ("simple, Syndicate-style, no sentences in play, at most two icons over a person") and the HUD budget (15 % at rest, aim under 10 %).
