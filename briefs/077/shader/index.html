077 · the leaf shader: vertex colour, two-sided leaves, the ORM gloss

Latch, 2026-10-29. Unity 6000.6.2f1. The testbed's leaves and petals (three.js MeshStandardMaterial, vertexColors, side: DoubleSide, roughness 0.49 / 0.64 x ORM.g) in our DecoKit cutaway shader, on two of Vela's candidates in an isolated review scene. Review only: nothing is placed in the game, no catalogue, plan, sim, collider or nav change; OldTown.unity and HarbourDistrict.unity were opened read-only and never saved.

Verdict. The three missing pieces are in, as a sibling shader, DecoKit/Lit Cutaway Leaf, with two opt-in keywords (_DK_VERTEX_COLOR, _DK_TWO_SIDED) and a build-time mask map from the testbed ORM. Existing materials render byte-identically: DK_LitCutaway.shader is unchanged (sha256 44623fab0e019539…, = HEAD), all 242 of its compiled Metal programs are unchanged, and 13 of the 14 proof frames are bit-identical. The 14th is a frame that is already noisy run to run. SRP Batcher: compatible (code 0) for both shaders. Cost in the review scene at play zoom (estimate): +1 forward, +1 depth-normals, +1 shadow SetPass; draws and triangles unchanged. The leaves now read as the testbed's: darker, varied greens from the vertex tints, and coral and gold cups that keep their colour from every side. With today's shader the petals go pale blue-white, because their back faces reflect the sky.
Why a sibling, not a keyword in the existing shader. I tried the keyword route first: the same two keywords in DK_LitCutaway.shader, its four pass includes switched, line count unchanged. Every program of the passes I edited compiled to the same bytes. But the ShadowCaster and DepthOnly programs with the cutaway's global _DK_CUT did not: 36 programs changed, in passes the edit never touched. In HarbourDistrict, every frame changed. The towers' shadows lost their glass strips (below: 1.5 M of 2.07 M pixels differ). Putting the shader back to HEAD restored the old frames exactly. The cause is in the data: the two new local keywords enter the shader's keyword space at index 33 and 34, ahead of every global keyword, so each global's index shifts by 2. The variant the shadow pass picks under _DK_CUT then changes. A sibling leaves DecoKit/Lit Cutaway's keyword space alone, and it costs the same forward SetPass (a new variant and a new shader both get their own SRP batch).

The mask (ORM roughness to URP smoothness)

URP Lit with _METALLICSPECGLOSSMAP reads metallic from R and smoothness from A (x _Smoothness = 1). The mask is derived at build time from the testbed ORM, deterministic, at the source's own size: R G B = 0, A = round(255 - k x ORM.g). One mask serves leaves and petals because the atlas keeps them apart: leaves are rows 0-3 (v 0.5-1, k = 0.49), petals rows 4-7 (v 0-0.5, k = 0.64). The import confirms it on every LOD: leaf UVs v 0.506-0.994, petal UVs 0.006-0.494. Soil: k = 1. It's a format conversion, not a texture cut. The ORM is not imported, so texture memory stays Vela's 9.0 MiB.

Look read

  • Day, play look. With the shader on, the troughs read as the testbed's: a dense, dark, varied green with coral and gold cups standing clear. Off, the green is lighter and flatter, and the blooms vanish into pale blue-white specks. That's the "flat and too light" in the brief. At play zoom the planting is small (a 4 m run is about 150 px), so the bloom colour is most of what reads, and it only reads with the flip on.
  • Gloss. The mask gives the testbed's glossy leaves: sharp sun highlights on the broad leaves. Under our play sun they show as small white glints at play zoom, a little hotter than the testbed's (three's light units differ). Glint should judge them in the grade before Sky sees it.
  • Darker than the domes, as Vela warned. The vertex tints average 0.40-0.49 linear, so the leaves sit well below today's candy domes. That's the testbed's own look, not a bug.
  • Night. Under the lamp pools the leaves stay dark green with a lamp glint and the petals read warm. Off, the petals glow pale cyan, the clearest failure of the old shader. Outside the pools the planting goes nearly black in the play look. Glint's call, with the real lamps, in the placement round.
  • Presentation look (old grade and bloom): the same story, warmer and softer. The pale strap and fan leaves read as cream (the atlas's variegated region), as in the testbed.

Open issues

  • Measurements: SetPass and draws are estimates. Redline measures the placed set in Unity and on the Air (next round).
  • Soil casts a shadow: Vela's spec says the soil doesn't, but its ShadowCaster pass flag doesn't stick (URP's material validation re-enables it), and soil shares the renderer with the leaves. Cheap (2 to 54 triangles), but it's a shadow draw per piece. Vela can split the soil out, or the kit writer can set it in the .mat. The soil has no vertex colour in the joined mesh (it imports as 0): it must never take _DK_VERTEX_COLOR.
  • The keyword-space finding (DK_LitCutaway's _DK_CUT shadow variant moves when local keywords are added ahead of the globals) applies to any future edit of DK_LitCutaway, DK_ComplexLitCutaway and FacadeLit. Redline and Splice should know: add keywords at the end, or prove the shadow pass after.
  • The sibling must follow DK_LitCutaway.shader: it's generated from it (its header records the source sha256). A check that fails when they drift belongs to Splice.
  • SH per vertex: with EVALUATE_SH_VERTEX / MIXED, URP evaluates part of the ambient per vertex with the front normal. The flip covers the per-pixel part (our desktop setting).
  • Placement, the kit materials (unity_mats.py / the catalogue) and the dome rejection are the next round (Vela's pieces, Splice's rule, then Latch).
