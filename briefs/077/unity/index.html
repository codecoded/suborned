How the swap was made

  • Targets (the pinned export out/scratch/latch/075swap2/harbour.world.json, the Handover zone): CafeUpper_3 ×1, RecordsTower ×1, PlanterArc ×4, CafeTerrace ×1, PlanterRun_4_A ×5, PlanterRun_4_B ×4, PlanterRun_4_C ×3, BedTuft_A ×31, BedTuft_B ×32, BedTuft_C ×29.
  • Baked pieces (all but CafeUpper_3) leave the civic batches by exact world-triangle match: each piece's LOD0 placed as KitPlacer.Bake placed it, at 1 mm, with a second grid offset by half a millimetre for rounding edges. Matched 72592/72755. What's left unmatched is 2 leaf triangles on one island, plus tuft fragments, which the bed rule takes out with the hedge. The rebuild's stone slots take the material the dome's stone was batched in (the restyle's), by slot name. The rebuilds' stonework lands on the dome's exactly (stone match below).
  • CafeUpper_3 (a fade piece, an unpacked instance): its renderers are off, and the rebuild takes its transform, materials and rendering layers.
  • Civic beds: the hedge prisms (centroid inside the hedge ring, above the kerb top) and the tufts leave the batches (facade-southwest 82, facade-southeast 62, office-west 123, office-north 73, dropoff-west 99 triangles). CivicBedPlanting_* goes in at the manifest's fit, with the soil at the kerb top + 3 mm (beds.ts).
  • Companions at their parents' transform. One GlobeLamp_TB per PlanterArc at the manifest's arc-frame offset.
  • LODGroups: L0 plus each LOD file, switching at the row's own lods[].screen. The last level culls by KitLods' rule, and a level that would switch in below the cull is dropped, as in KitLods (CafeUpper_3's 0.003). lodSize 4 is applied where the row sets it. Every soil object is shadows off.

Where the cost comes from. The four SetPass are forward +2 (the leaf shader's variant, and the soil's _METALLICSPECGLOSSMAP _NORMALMAP variant of today's shader, which nothing used before), depth-normals +1 and shadow +1 (the leaf shader's). The draws come from each piece now being its own LODGroup instance (2-3 sub-meshes and a soil renderer at each level), where the domes were merged into the civic batches. The triangles come mostly from the Office front's planted crown trough and civic beds at their gameplay level (RecordsTower_Planting 19,430, the beds 6,151-17,138).

Open, and what adoption needs from KitLods (Latch)

  • KitLods: it needs to build L1-L3 from these rows, honour lodSize, and set the soil objects shadows off from objects[].shadows. Watch out: LODGroup.SetLODs resets the size, so lodSize has to be set after it, which this probe does. The play-zoom guard fails any civic-layer group whose L1 switches nearer than 36 m. These rows switch at play zoom by design (Sky: gameplay LODs at play zoom), so the guard needs an exemption for planting rows. Otherwise the build fails.
  • Batching: the baked domes cost no draws of their own. If the extra draws matter on the Air, the gameplay level of the small pieces could be baked into the civic batches at the price of their LODs. That's Redline's call from the Air numbers.
  • Night: waits on Glint's lamp light (#fff1d8, 5.8 m, decay 2, no shadows, 2.31 m) and the opal's night emission.
  • Before the kit copy: nothing to clean. The probe's GUIDs differ from the kit GUIDs.
