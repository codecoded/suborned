Brief 078: car explosion VFX (Latch, 2026-10-30)

Shuriken on URP's particle shaders, one pooled rig per damaged car (cap 4), stepped on a fixed 1/60 s grid of the mission clock and seeded from the car id, the window's start tick and the part. It's presentation only: the engine's DisabledAt, BurningAt, BlastAt and Blown decide everything. Made by Suborned.World.Review.CarFxShots078.Run (batch editor, Play mode, through the playing guard). The checks are in fx-range.json and fx-handover.json. Captures stay local (git-ignored).

Known capture artefact: in the play-zoom composites the HUD blast ring is drawn about 600 px too high. That's because batch-mode Screen.height is 480 while the picture is 1080 (HudToolkit.Proj flips by Screen.height). It isn't the game's behaviour; Glyph should confirm on a player build.
