Verdict. In the simulation the Handover is healthy. It ships: 893 plays, MC-0 to MC-15 all pass. All three quiet routes win, and so does the loud route, with the price the design intends (quiet: 4–7 Trail, Alert ≤ 1, Standing ≥ 2; loud: 26–27 Trail, Alert 2, Standing −1, a raised investigation). Every §07 fail can be reached, and every run replays exactly.

It is not yet proven as a game a person plays. Nobody has played it since Sky's 0.2.122 on 26 Oct. Since then these have all landed, checked only by the sim and by screenshots: fight B, the rivals' drive-in, the new walking pace, squad boarding, sticky targets, cones on demand, the 3-storey café, the old-town world (brief 075) and the planting (brief 077).

One real bug blocks the next build. The square's mast camera was renamed in brief 075, which breaks the sim's own checks, so bun run batch stops at its first step. The mission's text also points at the camera's old name.

The things most likely to hurt a real player: the timing on route B is very tight (4 s of slack after 5:00), the quiet final fights are still short (8–21 s), and the two-car getaway that §07 route B describes has not been built.
