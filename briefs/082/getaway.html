The getaway: two ways to the car

Motive, brief 082, for Sky, 2026-10-30. A design only: no game file changed. Times use the new clock and move with the rivals' ±15 s.
In short. Today the rival car does almost nothing: moving your car is free, and winning without the false tip is about as likely as with it. So the rivals now react to your car. Way A, the turned driver: the Enforcer drives off in Vale's car, and getting the other three out is the climax. Way B, driving round: quick and cheap, but the patrol can see the car and the rivals box it in, so you shoot your way out, perhaps blowing up their car. The same rule answers your question about the team running for the car: a car that moves is a car they see.

Driving off to meet him is way B. Their rule is the same in both: they watch your car, not you.

2. Blowing up their car, and the tip

  • Today's numbers: 10 HP, stops at 5, burns at 0, explodes 6 s later: 5 m, 3 damage, everyone with a line. The three, unhurried, need about 15 s by the sums; a scripted bot took about 30 s in brief 078. The Enforcer helps a lot (2 damage) once Vale's in the car.
  • The price: any shot at it is a shooting: +2 Trail once, Alert 2 at once, police come. So it makes your run loud. People caught in the blast: +2 Trail each, or +4 if they go down, untraced in the final fight. Seen by a civilian: Alert 3, +1 Trail, −1 Standing.
  • New: the wreck clears the road only when it blows. Stopped, it still boxes you in. So disabling isn't enough.
  • New: a blast wrecks any car within 5 m. Today it only hits people. Your own car wrecked fails the mission, as now. The ring shows for the full 6 s, and the fire chip warns if your car is close. Their block sits at least 10 m out, so this only bites a careless player.
  • The tip buys the window, but only before they've seen your car move. Given first, there's no car to block you for 3:50, and the posted three are still there: this is preparation paying. Once they've boxed you in, it's refused: "they've seen you".
