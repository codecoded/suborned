# 100 · The why: every alert and every police call says what caused it

Owner: main session (as Aster), opened 2026-11-07 on `work/098-living-playbook`.

## Sky (2026-11-07, playing)
"It would be good to explain why an alert raised, or why police came"

## Today
- **The engine knows every cause.** Its log carries it (`bun run sim play tools/game/pins/loud.seed3.json`):
  - `security.called` "Shots are heard in the district. Security is coming from the north street."
  - `report.started` "p-records-office-29 radios it in."
  - `report.landed` "…'s report lands: dead (guard). Alert 2."
  - `drawn.seen` "The security sees the scout's weapon drawn. Alert 2."
  - `seal.starts` "… from gunfire:guard (reported by heard)"
  - missed check-ins, a camera's record of a Pay or a Pressure, a found knockout, a missing person.
- **The game shows little of it** (`HostFeed.cs`, `HostLess.cs`):
  - "Police are coming · From the north street" never says why;
  - a report is at most "Reported. Alert 2";
  - a witness walking to a call box shows nothing, though stopping them is the play brief 098's law L4 promises ("news travels on foot").

## Objective
The player always knows why the street got hotter: what was seen or heard, by whom, where, and what it set off. They can act on a report that is still on its way.

## The plan
1. **A why on every rise.** Each Alert change and each police call gets a card, or the existing card gains a line, with its cause in plain words, who and where. A click on it pans to the place. Examples:
   - "Alert 2 · A clerk found the guard's body and radioed it in"
   - "Police are coming · They heard shots by the café"
   - "Alert 1 · The patrol missed its check-in"
   - "Alert 1 · The entrance camera saw the Fixer pay Inez"
2. **The report on its way.** A witness heading to report is marked on the street: the person, where they're going, and when it lands ("Going to the call box · 6 s"). Stop them, scare them or block them, and the report never lands.
3. **The meter remembers.** A click or hover on the Alert meter lists what raised it so far, newest first, each line with its time and place.
4. **The debrief reuses it.** Brief 099's timeline and "the city's answer" use the same causes and words.

- Clickable, never key-only.
- It reads the run and never changes it: a replay is untouched.
- No new clutter: a why rides on the card or pop that already shows.
