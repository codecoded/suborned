# 039 · A fight worth playing: three ways (proposal, 2026-10-26)

**State:** for Sky to pick one. Rules by Motive; Glyph adds controls, feedback and mock frames below. Nothing is built or put in the GDD until Sky picks. Not in it: parked cars as cover ("not yet").

**Sky, 2026-10-26:** "The fight should be a lot more fun." On 0.2.122: "I wasn't sure whether to be pressing just X or hold RMB, and when the rivals move I kept losing the target on them."

## The fight today (engine 0.33.1, measured)
- **Decided in the first volley.** The safe fight runs 24–47 s to Go, but the car is free 3.5–9.5 s after the ambush: three sidearms drop the blocker at once (in the open at 11 m a first shot always hits; a rival has 3 health). The rest is the Enforcer's walk.
- **Loud is a wall.** The crew, drawn to the Office front by the first shot, presses the Enforcer on his 50 m walk with Vale. Every scripted player lost him, the plainest at 13.5 s.
- **Targets get lost.** Giving one order per target in the unprepared fight, 3 of 10 orders end by themselves (past 16 m, or 3 s out of line).
- **Four controls for one wish.** Drawn agents fire on their own, the right hold orders, X stands down, V cycles stances.

So the fight needs a longer contest, not a longer clock.

## In every option
1. **One input.** F draws or holsters; drawn agents fight on their own (whoever shoots them, else the nearest, and never anyone who isn't fighting the cell). A **right hold** on a hostile is the **focus** for every selected agent who can fire. To stop, holster. Retired: X, V and its stances, the both-buttons panic, the weapons-free switch. Rules: weapons free always on; the stance, cease, panic and free orders go; the focus is today's fire order with the sticky end below.
2. **Sticky focus.** It ends only when its target is down, broken or gone, or on a new focus, an act or a holster. Out of reach or line it waits while the agent fights on its own, then takes him back (1.0 s to aim). It revises Sky's "keep them firing until out of range" (2026-10-22). Measured: 3 lost orders become 0.
3. **Readable cover.** Half-shield marks on the cover within 10 m of a drawn agent in a fight; a move ending within 1.5 m of one snaps to it. Cover works only inside its arc (a flank beats it), never inside 5 m.
4. **The floor** (tuning.md §17.3): operatives 5 health, the Enforcer 8, so nobody drops before the player can answer.
5. **One move each,** an order with its price, ready again after 20 s (C: at every beat):

| agent | move | rule | buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scout | **Mark** | a hostile she sees within 30 m: for 10 s his cover doesn't count, and his next move shows | a rival in full cover, three on him: 1.0 s, not 2.5 s |
| Fixer | **Talk down** | once one of the crew is down, a hurt rival within 12 m breaks and counts to the crew's break; 2 s shout, no firing; never police | one kill fewer to break five |
| Tech | **Steam** | opens a heat-main grille within 24 m: no line passes within 4 m of it for 8 s | the Enforcer walking Vale under four rivals: 11.5 s, not 4.0 s |
| Enforcer | **Covering fire** | 4 s one-handed at a hostile in his carbine's reach, walking Vale too: everyone within 3 m of it is pinned (fires at half, holds); never hits | the same walk: 5.5 s |

Replays stay exact: health, cover and the pin are integer engine rules; the beat is host time, like the pause. A pause or a slow-down serves the commander over the avatar, so it fits that tiebreaker; what it tests is real time, which T1 answers.

Left alone on purpose: the first-shot rule (the count starts at 500) is what makes the first volley decisive, but starting it at 0 breaks the loud plans' opening shot (tuning.md §17.2); and reinforcement timing, since the crew's car already arrives before the ambush on both safe routes measured, and the loud dial sends a unit every 40 s (moving either is Pulse's, and re-times the routes).

## Recommendation
