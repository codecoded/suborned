# 039 · A fight worth playing: three ways (proposal, 2026-10-26)

**State:** for Sky to pick one. Rules by Motive; Glyph adds controls, feedback and mock frames below. Nothing is built or put in the GDD until Sky picks. Not in it: parked cars as cover ("not yet").

**Sky, 2026-10-26:** "The fight should be a lot more fun." On 0.2.122: "I wasn't sure whether to be pressing just X or hold RMB, and when the rivals move I kept losing the target on them."

## The fight today (engine 0.33.1, measured)
- **Decided in the first volley.** The safe fight runs 24–47 s to Go, but the car is free 3.5–9.5 s after the ambush: three sidearms drop the blocker at once (in the open at 11 m a first shot always hits; a rival has 3 health). The rest is the Enforcer's walk.
- **Loud is a wall.** The crew, drawn to the Office front by the first shot, presses the Enforcer on his 50 m walk with Vale. Every scripted player lost him, the plainest at 13.5 s.
- **Targets get lost.** Giving one order per target in the unprepared fight, 3 of 10 orders end by themselves (past 16 m, or 3 s out of line).
- **Four controls for one wish.** Drawn agents fire on their own, the right hold orders, X stands down, V cycles stances.

So the fight needs a longer contest, not a longer clock.

## In every option
1. **One input.** F draws or holsters; drawn agents fight on their own (whoever shoots them, else the nearest, and never anyone who isn't fighting the cell). A **right hold** on a hostile is the **focus** for every selected agent who can fire. To stop, holster. Retired: X, V and its stances, the both-buttons panic, the weapons-free switch. Rules: weapons free always on; the stance, cease, panic and free orders go; the focus is today's fire order with the sticky end below.
2. **Sticky focus.** It ends only when its target is down, broken or gone, or on a new focus, an act or a holster. Out of reach or line it waits while the agent fights on its own, then takes him back (1.0 s to aim). It revises Sky's "keep them firing until out of range" (2026-10-22). Measured: 3 lost orders become 0.
3. **Readable cover.** Half-shield marks on the cover within 10 m of a drawn agent in a fight; a move ending within 1.5 m of one snaps to it. Cover works only inside its arc (a flank beats it), never inside 5 m.
4. **The floor** (tuning.md §17.3): operatives 5 health, the Enforcer 8, so nobody drops before the player can answer.
5. **One move each,** an order with its price, ready again after 20 s (C: at every beat):

| agent | move | rule | buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scout | **Mark** | a hostile she sees within 30 m: for 10 s his cover doesn't count, and his next move shows | a rival in full cover, three on him: 1.0 s, not 2.5 s |
| Fixer | **Talk down** | once one of the crew is down, a hurt rival within 12 m breaks and counts to the crew's break; 2 s shout, no firing; never police | one kill fewer to break five |
| Tech | **Steam** | opens a heat-main grille within 24 m: no line passes within 4 m of it for 8 s | the Enforcer walking Vale under four rivals: 11.5 s, not 4.0 s |
| Enforcer | **Covering fire** | 4 s one-handed at a hostile in his carbine's reach, walking Vale too: everyone within 3 m of it is pinned (fires at half, holds); never hits | the same walk: 5.5 s |

**One input.** F draws or holsters; a drawn agent fights on his own (his weapon slot, the tracers and the 049 ring's ticks say at whom). A right hold on a hostile is the focus.
- "X or RMB?" goes because there is one verb and it stands at the cursor: the pill's head word is "Right hold". The roster loses the stance tag and the weapons-free toggle. One hint, once, at the first draw in a fight: "Right hold to focus." (Fable's).
- The pill is the game's own, `Right hold · ↓N s [faces]`, "Let go" in turquoise-light when charged; the faces are every selected agent who can fire (question 2 on N). The cursor over a hostile is the crosshair with its odds ticks (built) plus a shield badge: his cover counts, counts half, is struck.
- "Losing the target" goes with the lock reticle (067 D, built) and the sticky focus: a disc over his head in screen space, so cover and crowds never hide it, with an edge chip off screen. When he can't be shot it dims with a dashed ring turning and the shooter takes him back by himself; new on D, the disc says why (aiming, walking in, no line). It ends only when he is down, broken or gone, on a new focus (a hand-off line), an act, or F. Third person: the same pill and reticle (board 0, bottom row).

**Cover.**
- Quiet until it matters: a shield disc at 55 % on cover within 10 m of a drawn agent. Live when held: a ring for the holder's side (turquoise-light the cell, violet a rival, amber police: 049's) and a ground arc for the side it shields, heavy for full, thin for partial. Struck when it doesn't count (flanked, marked).
- The snap: a move ending within 1.5 m of cover leaps to the kneel point and lights the shield. The flank: hovering a move draws a dotted line of fire to the hostile in cover, and his disc strikes if it comes from outside his arc. Your own cover reads the same way against the red under-fire arc (built).
- A round cover takes shows chips on the prop. Pinned (B; Covering fire in all): a pin disc beside his pips (the kit's down chevron), its ring draining over 2 s and restarting with every shot at him; he fires at half and his post holds.

**One move each** is an order like any other, no new key.
- Click the target (the ring: the move first and lit, Fire dashed last); or hold on it (it takes the move when it is the only calm way); or click the move's disc beside the portrait, which lights every valid target with the move's glyph. The Enforcer walking Vale: his right hold is Covering fire. The pill is the price (verb, time, face); the disc is the state: ready (a ring), cooling (an arc over 20 s; C refills it at each beat), shut (a lock).
- Shown: **Mark**, brackets round him, a 10 s arc, his shield struck, his next move drawn in the rivals' route language. **Talk down**, a ring out to 12 m, the hurt rival drops his gun and runs, and the crew tally (a pip each under the clock, a brass tick where the crew breaks) counts him. **Steam**, the 4 m footprint on the grille, a cloud, an 8 s marker. **Covering fire**, a 3 m ring, three tracers ending past him, pin discs on all in it.

**The beat (C).** At each of the four moments: a marker over the subject (the window-marker plate: the cause's glyph or the arriving man's face, "¼×", an underline draining over the 8 s), a ring leaving him, aim lines for who aims at whom (dotted, in the shooter's colour), a ring refilling every move disc, the clock's chevrons at ¼, the lab's turquoise rim. Any order eases it out in 0.2 s. Nothing grades or freezes the street; it slows because it runs on host time.
