The test

Would this person say it aloud, now, to whoever is there, for their own reasons? If the line’s job is to tell you what the game will do, it fails, however natural it sounds.

Your words: on “He bought it. He’ll be back, and he knows my face.”, “that’s exactly the kind of scripting that bugs me. ‘I will remember you.’ lines etc - so unnatural”.

The standard is the lines you kept: “You paid my debt? I’m yours. Say when.” and “Civic Police! Drop your weapons!” Both tell you something, a turn and an arrival, because that is what the person would say.

A person can still say what they mean to do now, in their own words (“Say when.”, “I’m getting the police!”). What they can’t do is tell you what the game does next: that they’ll remember you, that he’ll be back, that a search could find the body, that the next offer costs more.
