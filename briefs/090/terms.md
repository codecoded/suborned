# 090 · Voice services: the terms relied on (read 2026-11-02)
All three were called through Runware (`paid/090-voices/`, ledger). Runware's terms (https://runware.ai/terms):
- §2: you own your Outputs as between you and Runware, subject to the Model Licence. Partner Models are licensed to Runware "under a commercial agreement with the model owner".
- §10: commercial use of Outputs is allowed, and for Partner Models Runware has obtained rights that "permit you to use Outputs generated using those Partner Models for commercial purposes".
- §12: outputs may have no copyright.
Runware's model search lists all three as `source: official`.

| Service | Model | Own terms, on its own pages | Plainly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inworld | `inworld:tts@1.5-max` | Terms §4 (https://inworld.ai/terms): "we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest in Outputs". Service Specific Terms 1(c)(ii) (https://inworld.ai/service-specific-terms): "you retain all rights in and to your Output". The licence to *use the service* is "for your internal business purposes", and Inworld takes a broad licence to use Materials, Outputs included, to run and improve its services | Output is ours to ship. Nothing on AI disclosure |
| Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign (Alibaba) | `alibaba:qwen@3-tts-1.7b-voicedesign` | The model card (https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign) lists Apache-2.0, which puts no limit on outputs. On Runware it is listed `official` | The clearest licence of the three |
| Fish Audio | `fishaudio:s2.1@pro` | Terms (https://fish.audio/terms): "if you are a user of Paid Services, you are licensed to use the Services for commercial uses". Free use is non-commercial. They don't say who owns output. They encourage disclosing AI content and bar presenting it as human-made | Commercial use is allowed on a paid footing. Whether Runware's Partner clause gives us that footing is my inference, not stated. Ownership is unstated |

Not used:
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS:** Runware deprecates it on 2026-11-17.
- **Eleven v3 on Runware:** deprecated, listed `community`. ElevenLabs direct needs a key we don't hold.
- **MiniMax Speech 2.8:** its terms page wouldn't render for me to read.
- **Fish's voice library:** full of clones of real people, so I used only its eight stock voices.

**Steam:** the game's AI-content disclosure will need to name the voices as AI-generated (brief 090).
