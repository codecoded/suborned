## Summary: what v0.23.0 changes

1. **Takedowns** (§02, §03, §05, §06, §07, §08).
   - Anyone can be targeted. The Enforcer can knock out (6 s) or kill (3 s), silently.
   - Bodies stay where they fall and can be found. Any operative can carry, restrain or stash a body.
   - Only a container or a service door hides a body.
   - A found body brings officers and holds the doors in sight of it.
   - A hidden kill becomes a missing person and an open case. Open cases raise the district's security level.
   - The response is a dial, S0–S4, kept as data. The Handover runs at S2.
2. **Witnesses** (§05).
   - People react by temperament and walk to a phone to report.
   - A silent act raises Alert when its report lands, not when it is seen.
   - Telling a tie indoors needs a phone.
   - A missing tie raises the alarm.
   - One incident gets one dispatch.
3. **Private turning** (§04, §05). Ask, Pay, Help and Listen have no witnesses. Pressure is still seen.
4. **Standing** (§01, §04, §06, §08).
   - Every district starts at +2, leaning Hero.
   - Word of mouth moves Standing between missions, then it drifts back toward +2.
   - A public fight moves Standing once, depending on who the cell fought.
5. **The cones are the Scout's skill** (§03, §05, §10). Watch teaches a watcher's sight and check-in marks. A watcher not yet watched shows "?".
6. **Less reading** (§10, §15, §14).
   - No sentences in play except speech. Prices are one line each.
   - World markers replace text, and the plot-scope minimap holds the persistent state.
   - The feed shows story beats only; Alt shows the detail; the objective is one line.
   - Every capture is checked against a HUD budget.
   - The briefing is modal. No keycaps on buttons.
   - G switches walk and run. Destinations are pins, and Alt shows the paths.
   - The move ring is colour-coded. Known people are discs under the pill. The notes key is bottom right.
7. **The café** (§07, §09). One waitress stands outside. Customers come and go. New checker rule P-16.
8. **The cell starts in view** (§07, §10), and the camera opens on it.
9. **Signal colours** (§10, §15, §14; a correction in §12).
   - Signals no longer own their colours, and the ΔE ≥ 20 reservation is dropped.
   - Signals stay readable by being drawn unlit and emissive, each with its own shape or motion cue.
10. **Carried over: two of your rulings v0.22.0 got wrong or left out.**
    - Only Listen reveals a temperament (you ruled this on 10-15; the engine already does it).
    - Inez and the door guard are lovers, and that is the core of the capstone. v0.22.0's log wrongly lists it as parked.
11. **Parked, not decided:** camera rotation and a third-person try-out; the raygun as the Fixer's sidearm.
