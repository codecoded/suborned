# How squad games earn attachment: notes for brief 012 (cell characters r2)

Gathered 2026-10-15 by Forma. Source quality is thin: mostly reviews and press, few developer statements. Treat as supporting colour, not authority; the GDD stays the source. Items marked (inference) are mine.

## What the sources say

**Desperados III** (https://pcgamer.com/desperados-3-characters-abilities-skills, https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/Desperados; the PC Gamer page body would not load, so these facts come from the search extracts)
- Five outsiders, each a one-line archetype (drifter, strongman, methodical bounty hunter, disguise artist, mystic).
- Each has a colour code and an audio stinger (Cooper brown/teal and guitar, McCoy black/purple and harmonica, Kate rosy peach and violin, Hector dusky orange and whistling, Isabelle green and trumpet).
- Backstories hang off wanted posters, so each has a price on their head.
- (inference) Silhouette plus one colour block plus one prop-sound is the identity set. This matches the GDD's "job readable at distance = silhouette + one colour block".

**Shadow Tactics / Shadow Gambit** (https://gamingbolt.com/shadow-gambit-the-cursed-crew-interview-setting-characters-abilities-and-more, https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2017/08/shadow-tactics-blades-shogun/)
- Mimimi's designer: "characters that are fun to be around and have great banter is what really makes people fall in love". Cast is deliberately varied by origin and archetype ("a ninja cook to a Prussian quartermaster").
- Reviewers: banter mid-mission expands identities; the mercenary Mugen is praised for his arc; the team splits into clear niches (ninja, samurai, spy, urchin thief, one-legged sniper with a pet).
- (inference) Odd, specific people (a one-legged sniper with a raccoon-dog) beat generic soldiers. Our hooks follow that.

**XCOM** (https://gamesbeat.com/xcom-enemy-unknown-soldier-progression/, https://pcgamer.com/xcom-2-will-offer-greater-soldier-customisation)
- Firaxis (Garth DeAngelis): investment comes from "permadeath and aesthetic customization"; the player's "internal narrative" about a soldier ("Crater Guzman is not going to go through that again!").
- Critic: customisation can make soldiers feel like dolls; attachment comes from growth the player did not fully choose.
- Authored cast of Chimera Squad reportedly harder to bond with because their set personalities leave less room to imprint (https://aftermath.site/star-wars-zero-company-permadeath-xcom/, secondhand).
- (inference) For the Suborned: the cell's look should leave room for the player's story (the Fixer especially: the Story spine leaves their gender and name open); history (GDD "history, not backstory") does the rest.

**Hitman** (https://gameinformer.com/games/hitman/b/pc/archive/2015/09/02/technology-is-responsible-for-agent-47-s-iconic-bald-hitman-head, https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/io-wanted-hitmans-agent-47-to-be-the-coca-cola-of-stealth-games, the VGC page returned 403 so its claim comes from the search extract)
- Agent 47's bald head came from a technical limit (hair was hard) and became the canvas for a barcode. IO wanted a "Coca-Cola" brand: recognisable from any angle.
- (inference) One strong, simple read beats detail; a constraint can make the icon. At 40-60 px the same applies.

**Invisible, Inc.** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invisible,_Inc., https://www.gamereactor.eu/invisible-inc-review/)
- Search found no design commentary. Known only: agents are individuals with permadeath and a small named roster. Not used.

## What we take from it for the cell
1. One-line archetype per person, readable in a sentence (our "personality hook").
2. Silhouette plus one colour block plus one telling prop; not tactical costume.
3. Varied origins, ages and builds; the odd specific detail beats the generic.
4. Leave the player room: faces with attitude, not fixed backstory; history comes from play.
5. Voice (banter, stingers) carries a lot of attachment; hand to Chime and Fable. Art alone will not do it.

# Round 3 addendum: "everyone has a favourite" (silhouette, signature item, colour identity)

Gathered 2026-10-15 by Forma for the round 3 brief (Tech in line vs four icons). Same caveat as above: reviews, press and wikis, very few primary design documents; several publisher pages returned 403 (SCMP, pcgames.de, Netflix Tudum body text), so those claims come from search extracts and are marked secondhand. Items marked (inference) are mine.

## Overwatch
- Lead concept artist Arnold Tsang, 2016: every character should have a different silhouette so a player can tell which hero is approaching from far away (secondhand, SCMP via search extract: https://scmp.com/tech/apps-gaming/article/1951451/latest-blizzard-first-person-shooter-video-game-fresh-take ; also reported by https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/technology%2Fgaming%2Fnew-overwatch-video-game-gets-diversity-just-right-1.1836393 ). I could not retrieve the original transcript.
- Tracer: "the character's body size and running pose were considered during character design to make each hero easily identifiable" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracer_(Overwatch)). Her identity set is orange trousers, goggles, a trail of light: one colour block, one prop, one motion.
- Ana got a triangular cape to separate her silhouette (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_(Overwatch)); Moira's and Mei's early prototypes were reworked because they resembled Zarya and another hero too much (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moira_(Overwatch), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mei_(Overwatch)).
- Overwatch 2 art panel: for Tracer the team kept "her color palette, silhouette, and sense of motion" as core identity while redrawing the model (https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23189038). A Blizzard concept artist (secondhand, https://devtrackers.gg/overwatch/p/004a3f0d-what-it-s-like-to-work-on-overwatch-as-a-concept-artist): skins should not radically change a silhouette; big shapes sit near the top of a character so they read from far away.
- Cook and Becker's write-up of the design pillars: readability asks whether a new silhouette looks too much like an existing hero (https://cookandbecker.com/en/article/378/designing-overwatch.html). 80.lv critique: silhouette and material reads are strong, team colour is weaker (https://80.lv/articles/comparing-team-fortress-2-and-overwatch-art-direction).
- (inference) Test = shadow test, applied to every hero against every other. Colour is the second read, motion the third.

## Apex Legends
- No primary source on silhouette or palette found. What exists: a Legend starts from a gameplay mechanic (design director Jason McCord, Loba: "a loot-based character"), then narrative builds the backstory (https://www.pcgames.de/Apex-Legends-Spiel-62166/Specials/Interview-Charaktererstellung-Loba-Respawn-Entertainment-1350774/, German, via search extract).
- Lead animator Moy Parra on Lifeline's early male concept: a character must be "fun", not boring or bland (secondhand: https://sportskeeda.com/esports/characters-can-go-direction-apex-legends-character-developer-reveals-lifeline-s-original-design-male-legend).
- The Fuse plagiarism claim shows how a shared set of traits (eyepatch, one mechanical arm, a colour pair) is itself the identity, and how fast two characters collide (https://primagames.com/news/apex-legends-bulletville-nowwa; claim by the studio, no Respawn reply in the results).
- (inference) Role first, look second: our four are already role-first. The signature item should be the role made visible, not a costume.

## Persona 5
- Shigenori Soejima designed the Phantom Thief outfits around the idea of "masks", the inner rebel; sharp lines and high-contrast colours; the thief motif reaches the UI and the silhouettes (secondhand extract of https://www.pcgames.de/Spiele-Thema-239104/Specials/Charakterdeisgn-Klamotten-JRPG-japanische-1393927/4/ , German; page returned 403).
- The same feature: the hard part is connecting the characters while each keeps individuality; the school uniform unifies them; red is the signature colour of the whole game, and colour characterises each member (same source, secondhand).
- Joker's look is "a more eccentric version of the Shujin uniform"; Soejima compared him to a black panther (https://ultimatepopculture.fandom.com/wiki/Joker_(Persona), fan wiki, weak).
- (inference) The strongest "team thread" example in the set: one shared motif plus one personal colour each. This is option 2's model. The thread here is the school uniform and the thief look; ours is white on charcoal, chrome, matte technical fabric.

## Arcane
- Fortiche art director Julien Georgel: palettes and lighting follow the story arcs; the Hexcore shape and colour language recurs (https://www.netflix.com/tudum/features/arcane-season-two-behind-the-scenes, only the title loaded; via the AWN piece https://awn.com/animationworld/riot-games-and-fortiche-push-every-possible-boundary-arcane-season-2 search extract).
- City palettes: Piltover whites, blues, gold, Art Deco symmetry; Zaun neon greens, purples, pinks, smoke (secondary summaries). Jinx: exaggerated lanky limbs and an oversized jacket carry her personality (tutorial source https://cypaint.com/article/how-to-paint-in-the-style-of-arcane, interpretation, not an official note). Vi: bandaged arms and scars tell her history (secondary).
- (inference) Costume as biography: one worn thing that says where a person has been (bandages, an oversized jacket). This is our "one detail that says who they are". And a faction has a palette: our cell's palette is the visitors' flag.

## What this means for round 3
1. Test silhouettes against each other, not alone: Overwatch rejected designs for resembling another hero. Round 3 therefore measures pairwise overlap of the four black fills in each lineup.
2. One signature item per person that nobody else carries (Tracer's goggles, Ana's cape): pack, case, visor, rifle and coat. Never the same item twice.
3. A personal colour is the third read, after silhouette: small panels, not whole garments.
4. A unifying thread plus personal difference (Persona 5): white on charcoal, chrome, matte technical fabric for all; accent, cut and item for each.
5. Favourites come from a worn detail with a story (Arcane's bandages) and a pose with attitude; keep faces open.
6. Source gaps: nothing primary for Apex; Persona 5 and Overwatch statements are secondhand. If this decision matters, GDC Vault talks and the artbooks are the next place to look.
