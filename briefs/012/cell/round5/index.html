1. What changed against round 4

Kept: the space-age cut (moulded collar-hood and pack, ring collar and waist circle, quilted tunic with a rigid bell panel and porthole visor, moulded shoulder-yoke cuirass), the four faces and bodies, the signature items, the porthole lamp (now a faint pale aqua, small). Taken out: candy and pastel accents, high-gloss vinyl and patent, white boots, the bright and optimistic wording, the expressive poses. Three small departures from the round 4 outfits, on purpose, all of them toy or costume tells: the Scout's bare legs are covered by matte tights under the shorts; the white go-go and moulded boots are dark matte boots with ring buckles; the Tech's bell is a dark or metal moulded panel rather than a pale flared skirt. Each prompt says the space-age character is in the form (ring collars, porthole discs, moulded panels), not in the colour, and carries no date.

7. Animation notes (estimates from the drawings; no rig built)

The cut is round 4's, so the bone count is round 4's: the Scout's pack and collar-hood (+2), the Fixer's ponytail, cable and case (+8), the Tech's visor discs (2 hinges) and a rigid bell panel needing 2 to 4 hem bones or a split front and back panel (+4 to +6), the Enforcer's rifle socket and cuirass (+1); about +16 over the 65-bone humanoid. Differences by direction: matte fabric needs no vinyl or patent shader and no cloth sim (all rigid shells skinned to the body). In c the metal collar, shoulder plates, chest plate and gauntlets are rigid parts on the body bones (no extra bones); the Tech's chrome bell is the one flared piece and stays a moulded panel. The lamps stay emissive materials, driven by the 'acting' state.

8. Iteration log

First pass of all twelve takes and three lineups (kept in _iteration/v1/). Measured on the painted lineups it failed the gate in two places: a's slate-blue Tech was 17.5 from the rivals' violet and a's teal Scout 17.5 (4 to 9 percent of those figures under 20), and c's blued-steel Tech was 19.2 (3 percent). The slate-blue target itself was 16.8 from violet (I had not checked the targets before painting), and the teal target (24.2) painted greyer than aimed; mid-tone desaturated blues and teals sit near violet in dE2000. Redrawn with a pale ice-blue Tech and a greener pine-teal Scout in a, mirror chrome for the Tech in c. At the same time the prompts were changed to say that the space-age character is in the form, not the colour, and c's metal was made bigger and placed differently on each person for the 50 px read. v1 colour numbers: a lowest rivals 17.5, c lowest rivals 19.2.
