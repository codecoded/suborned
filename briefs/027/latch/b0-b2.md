# 027 · Latch: fight playground B0–B2 (host side)

Latch, 2026-10-20. Spec: `briefs/027/playground.md` §1–§5, `briefs/027/keel/host-interface.md`. Everything here is EXPERIMENTAL, a playground switch, not a design decision. Engine `suborned.sim/0.10.0` (working tree) with Motive's tuned rulebook.

## How Sky starts each mode

Open the P panel (**P**). The new section at the top is **Fights (brief 027 playground)**: `p-panel-fights.png`.

1. **Preset:** `today` (the lab off, the game as Sky has played it) or `lab` (Motive's defaults: health ×10, pips, two stances, charge, the loadout). The preset's engine part is the run's start state, so it applies from a restart. The panel shows the button **Restart with the lab preset**.
2. **Checkpoint:** `none`, `CP-LOUD` (2:36.5, the Enforcer's shot on the guard) or `CP-SAFE` (5:53.8, out with Vale before the ambush). **Load CP-… with the … preset** restarts there. The run opens paused on the fight with no briefing; **Space** runs the clock.
3. **Fight mode:** `realtime`, `freeze` or `target`. It switches live, with no restart.

| mode | keys |
|---|---|
| **realtime** | As today: right click fires, F draws. With the lab: **X** holds fire (`stance hold`) and **V** panics (gated by the going-loud card; hold V to commit). The `pauseInFight` toggle is off in the lab preset, so Space doesn't pause a fight (pure real time); in the today preset Space still pauses. |
| **freeze** | Freezes on its own at the triggers ticked in the panel. **Space** during a fight freezes by hand. In a freeze, select an agent and click as usual: each agent holds **one** queued action, and a new click replaces it. **Return** releases them all on the frozen tick. **Esc** or **Space** leaves without orders. |
| **target** | **Y** opens target mode, which also opens on its own at the targetAuto triggers. Select an agent and left click a combatant: +1 shot, up to 6 and what the charge pays. Right click removes one. **Y** or **Return** fires. It needs the lab preset; with today's rules there is no volley order. |

Each finished try writes one line to `out/play/fightlab-outcomes.tsv` (§5): preset, mode, checkpoint, result, the cell's health left, downs on each side, Trail, Alert, Standing, freezes, volleys, wall seconds and the session file.

## What was built

- **Engine sync:** `bun tools/sim/unity.ts --tree` (the 0.10.0 engine and the fight.lab rulebook are uncommitted). It copied DLL `355e5e8ce0e5` and the inputs. Headless: 198/198 scenarios pass with identical replay. Unity `Suborned/Sim/Run Scenarios`: **198/198 match headless at every checkpoint, 198/198 replay identically, 198/198 pass**. EditMode tests: 6/6 pass, including `SimScenarioTests` and `ModalGateTests`.
- **B0 freeze** (`Runtime/Play/HostFightLab.cs`):
  - A freeze is host time. `Update` stops calling `StepOnce` the moment `Frozen` is set, even mid-frame.
  - In a freeze, `Issue` keeps each agent's order in host memory: one slot per agent, plus a cell slot for panic.
  - **Release** issues them with `Game.Issue` on the frozen tick: the cell's first, then scout, fixer, tech, enforcer. A driven agent would go first, after its `DriveRelease`. In practice there is never one, because a freeze lets the drive go when it opens (§1.4).
  - Auto triggers are read from the step's own log lines: `drawn.seen`, `shot.*` on an op, `fight.ambush` / `fight.loud`, `response.reinforces`, `<op>.down`. The going-loud card opens the freeze first and shows inside it. `freezeMinGapS` is checked after every release. While driving, the trigger flashes in the status line instead.
  - `freezesPerFight` caps manual freezes only. The beat-end trigger is **not built** (off by default).
  - **What a freeze shows** (`FightLabHud.cs`, an IMGUI playground layer for Glyph to review):
    - a banner;
    - the queue strip, with each slot's action, its `PreviewFire` price and the charge bar;
    - ice-blue ghost lines from each agent to its queued target or destination;
    - each hostile's current focus (`Gun.Target`), dashed violet for rivals and amber for police.
- **B1** (lab):
  - The start state takes the preset's `fightLab`, copied field by field. A checkpoint takes the preset's lab fields, as Keel says.
  - The existing HUD's health pips become **segments of 10** with the lab, on combatants and on the portraits.
  - The chip comes from `PreviewFire`: `●●●○ −10 ×3`, plus `▮` charge, the walk-in, distance band and Trail. It has no seconds; its clock icon is dropped in the lab.
  - The hovered target shows a damage chunk: one hit's damage lit on its bar.
  - X sends `stance hold`; V sends `cell panic` through the going-loud gate.
  - The today preset keeps today's chip, X (cease) and V (stance cycle).
- **B2** (target):
  - The forecast under each targeted combatant is `PreviewVolley`'s ●○●● plus the chunk it takes off the bar, or DOWN.
  - The strip carries each agent's `ChargeOf` bar.
  - Release issues `volley {op, target, shots}`.
  - `targetPlayback` slows the host's tick rate while a volley fires; the ticks are identical.
- **Checkpoints:** `briefs/027/checkpoints/*.script.json` are parsed, given the preset's lab fields, and their orders are played to `until` without `Finish`, so the fight stays open. Those orders stay in the session, so the saved session replays from tick 0.
- **Today preset unchanged:** the today preset's start hash with CP-LOUD is `4ba773b727aa2690`, the same as the checkpoint's own `expect.start`. The lab off adds no bytes, and a normal start is `Engine.New` on the same Start as before.

- **A fix the freeze proof needed** (`HandoverHost.Save`):
  - The problem: a session saved while a public fight is still open wrote its closing checkpoint from the live state. A replay's `Finish` closes the fight before hashing, so every mid-fight save failed its last checkpoint. This was there before brief 027 too.
  - The fix: the closing checkpoint is now played headless from the session's own start and orders, and only while a fight is open.

## Known gaps and notes

- **Fixed along the way:** the robot face and idle components threw every frame in Play (`IndexOutOfRange`). A domain reload brought their private caches back as empty arrays; they are `[NonSerialized]` now and rebuild when empty. Every Play test before this fix ran with that error spam.
- **B3 territory:** a click on a response officer or robot (`unit` views) still only labels it, so B1's chip shows on `security` and final-fight hostiles but not on units. The rings, the filled cursor and the drive soft lock are B3 too.
- **Target forecast while out of range:** the forecast is "if they stand", read from where the shooter stands. Out of range it shows misses (○○○) even though `close` will walk the agent in first. Keel or Glyph to say what it should show.
- **For Glyph:** the strip and forecast are small IMGUI text, as a playground layer; Glyph's B1/B2 review should set their reads.
- **Not built:** `freezeOnBeat`, target mode's per-shot right-click removal by cursor position (it removes the last shot), the drive soft lock, and frame time on the Air.
