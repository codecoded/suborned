Brief 027 · fight overlay (B0 to B2)

Glyph, 2026-10-20. Reviews Latch's debug layer (briefs/027/latch/) against GDD §15, the HUD budget and the signal rules, then proposes the finished design for Latch to build. Frames below are drawn in true 1080p pixels at 2× zoom. Nothing in the game was edited.

1 · Verdict on what is built

The shots are 1280×720, so sizes are scaled ×1.5 to 1080p. The mechanics are right: one action per agent, the volley count, the hit chip with damage and hits to down. The look is a developer layer: grey translucent boxes, a different typeface, sentences, and three colours that already mean something else.

2 · The finished design

Five parts, one grammar. Teal enamel, cream type, brass hairline, the same plate as the rest of the HUD. No grey boxes. The cell is always turquoise (rings, our lines, charge). Rivals are violet, police amber, cones red: only for what they already are.

A · Freeze: the queue ladder, the mode pill, the lines

Frame A (2×). A 3 px turquoise rim pulses round the screen; the world keeps its grade. Top centre: a 28 px pill with the trigger's glyph (here the shot glyph, "fired on") and "Go ⏎" / "Esc" as keycaps, no sentence. Left: each portrait has a queue pill to its right: action glyph, target glyph, pips. Empty = dashed outline. Rival lines march toward the Enforcer with arrowheads; our line is still, with an F4 badge at the target end. The Enforcer's ring pulses because he is being aimed at.

PieceSpec (1080p px)
Rim3 px, cell turquoise at 60 %, 1.2 s pulse. Auto-trigger freezes pulse the pill's glyph once. Hand freezes: no glyph, just "❚❚".
Mode pillTop centre, below the clock. 28 px tall, 96 px wide. Glyph + keycaps. Freeze: pause glyph; target: crosshair glyph. Trigger glyphs: shot, eye (spotted), drawn gun, siren (reinforce), cross (down), horn (loud order).
Queue pillTo the right of each portrait, 150 × 28, 14 px type. Action glyph (move, draw, fire, hold), target glyph (side coin), pips + "−10 ×3" for a fire. Replaced by one click. A "Go ⏎" chip, 60 × 28, below the last pill.
Ours lineTurquoise, 2 px, dashes 6/4, static; 14 px badge with the F-number at the target end; goes to a ring or a ground mark for moves.
Theirs lineSignal colour (violet rival, amber police), 2 px, dots 2/5 marching toward the target; 8 px arrowhead; target ring pulses once. Only in freeze or target mode (engine truth).
OverlapOffset each line 4 px sideways perpendicular to the path. Lines always end at the body's chest, not the feet.
Budget4 pills 150 × 28 + Go + mode pill ≈ 22 000 px² = 1.1 % of 1080p, against about 3.7 % now. Gone again on release.

B · The hit chip and the health bar

Frame B (2×). Over a guard of 30 health, with the selected agent's next hit lit as a hatched chunk. The chip sits down-right of the target so it never covers a head. At the right, the five states of a bar.

PieceSpec (1080p px)
Chip26 px tall, pill, teal enamel with a brass hairline, 15 px cream type. Order: side coin · ●●●○ · −10 · ×3, then a divider and ▮15 (charge, only with the lab on) and +4 T (Trail; Alt only, as brief 027 says, or always, see decisions). No seconds, no words. The footsteps glyph appears before the pips only when the agent walks into range first.
PipsFour 7 px dots. Cream filled = chance, outlined = not. This is the chance, not the forecast.
Side coin18 px. Police: amber with the shield; rival: violet with the diamond; the shape differs so it reads without colour. It also carries the range: filled ring = effective, half = long, hollow = out.
Health barOver the head, 10 px above it. 8 × 5 px segments, 2 px gap, one segment = 10 health. Guard 3 = 26 px, Enforcer 6 = 58 px. Dark ground with a 1 px side-coloured frame (cell turquoise, rival violet, police amber). Fill cream. At most one icon next to it: the bar counts as the one icon of the two-icon budget.
Segment statesfull = cream. Hit = lost segments flash white for 0.25 s, then empty (dark, outlined, so they still read in a red cone). Preview = the next hit's chunk hatched diagonally, pulsing at 1.2 s. Down = every remaining segment hatched plus a small cross at the right end. Wounded cell: the same bar, plus turquoise charge under it on the portrait only.
Cell bars on portraitsSame bar along the portrait's lower edge, 6 px segments so six fit in 40 px. Charge: a thinner 3 px turquoise bar below it.
Show whenIn a fight: every combatant and the cell. Out of a fight: no bars on anyone, and the old red pips are retired.

C · Target mode: forecast and charge

Frame C (2×). F1 has queued three shots on the near rival. Above the rival's bar: the three shots as pips (two hit, one miss), and on the bar the damage they will do, hatched, with the second rival at DOWN. F1's charge bar on the ladder shows the spend hatched. A second shot at the far rival shows the reserved range state (open item below).

PieceSpec (1080p px)
Volley lineOur turquoise dashed line, badge "F1 ×3": F-number and shot count. +1 shot per click, −1 per right click, the badge counts.
Forecast pipsAbove the target's bar, one 7 px dot per queued shot, in firing order: filled cream = hit, outlined = miss. A row of at most 6. They are only a forecast "if they stand" (brief 027 §1.3); the row is drawn dotted-outlined so it never reads as a result.
Damage on the barThe hatched chunk is exactly what the hits will take. If it empties the bar: DOWN (all hatched + cross).
Charge barThin turquoise bar, 3 px, 10 charge per segment, 10 segments = 100. The volley's spend is hatched; a volley the charge can't pay is refused: the click flashes the bar and adds nothing (no sentence). Also drawn on the queue pill. Never drawn for a slot with no shot.
Mode pillCrosshair glyph and three keycaps: left click "+", right click "−", Y "➤". No words.
BudgetPill + pips + badges: under 0.4 %. The ladder is shared with freeze.
Left open for Keel: what the forecast shows for a target out of range, where today it shows misses even though the agent will walk into range first. The design reserves the slot: the side coin's range state (filled / half / hollow) and the footsteps glyph before the pips, plus a dashed placeholder under the far rival in frame C. Whatever Keel decides, no new element is needed, only which pips fill.

D · Tokens, and the colour rules

MarkColour and cue
Cell (rings, our lines, charge, bar frame)turquoise #4fe3e8. Dashed or solid, still.
Rival (focus lines, bar frame, coin, tracer)violet #8a4fd0. Dotted, marching. Diamond coin.
Police / guard (same)amber #f0a020. Dotted, marching. Shield coin.
Conesred #e03a2f. Only the cone and its sight line; never for damage.
Health fillcream, for everyone; side is the frame. Lost segments: dark, outlined.
TracersMine to rule. Cell: cream core, turquoise glow, 2 px, 80 ms streak. Rival: violet core. Police: amber core. A tracer is a solid fast streak; a focus line is dotted and slow, so the two never read as one. A miss: a 6 px spark on stone and a pale ricochet streak; a hit: the target's bar flashes.
Signals in the shaderAll hostile lines, tracers and rings are drawn unlit and emissive, outside the post grade, on the signal layer, not with IMGUI lines.
TypeThe HUD face already used on the plate, 14 px minimum at 1080p, cream on teal enamel (contrast 9:1). No translucent grey plates.

3 · For Sky to decide

#QuestionMy pick
1Health fill: cream for every side with a coloured frame, or filled in the side colour (turquoise, violet, amber)?Cream with frame. It reads inside a red cone and a hostile bar never looks "ours". Side-coloured fill is louder in a crowd.
2Freeze queue: a ladder on the roster (1 % of the screen, tied to portraits) or a bar across the top (3.7 %, with room for words)?The ladder. A bar needs words, which §10 forbids.
3Trail on the chip: always (+4 T), or only with Alt, as brief 027 says?Alt, so the resting chip is five marks, not six. Keep it in the playground until played.
4Freeze look: the world keeps its colours (rim only), or desaturates a little as in Desperados?Rim only. A grade shift touches the signals, and the signals must stay outside the grade.
5Key legends: glyph keycaps only (as drawn), or a one-time word legend the first two fights?Keycaps only. A word legend can be a tutorial beat (Fable), not the HUD.
6Charge in turquoise under health on the portraits (brief 027 P12, option A), or its own element?Option A, as P12 already says. Needs a yes before the playground ships it.
7Out-of-range forecast (Keel's decision): leave the design slot as drawn, or add a fourth pip state?Not mine to choose before Keel; the slot is ready for either.
