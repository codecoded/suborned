# Brief 020 · the café (Vela, 2026-10-18)

The 9 Oct "Caprice Grand Café" look as two DecoKit pieces, generated in Blender by `tools/decokit/cafe_grand.py` (`bun run decokit --only CafeBay_v2,CafeInterior --out out/decokit-020`), registered in `contracts/world/kit.json` as `candidate` (sha256, `sourceScript`), same pivot and frame as `CafeBay`: block (-10.7, -29), yaw 90, front -Y, z 0 = pavement top. Concept: `the-suborned-store/scratch/Concept/Handover/cafe-corner.jpg`. Old code read: `HandoverCafe*.cs` (placement of the fit-out, pendants, the shopfront heights); nothing hand-placed.

## What is in them

- **`CafeBay_v2`** (5.0k tris; replaces `CafeBay`): clear glazing (`DK_Glass_Clear`, alpha 0.12, the 9 Oct material) in thin chrome mullions with brass sill, transom and head hairlines, reflection streaks (`DK_Glass_Sheen`) on every pane and door leaf, glass doors with chrome push bars, cream piers (now also where the arcs meet the side walls), the seafoam belt course at z 4.40 to 4.70 under the generator's upper mass, the **coral and ivory striped awning** with a scalloped valance and a seafoam edge rail (0.46 m stripes; coral `#FC9C84`, the civic coral, and cream), the blade sign. The 008 canopy was a flat cream slab with no stripes, so the awning replaces it; it projects 1.7 m, not 2.45 m: at 38° the 2.45 m canopy hid everything but the bottom 40 cm of glass. Edge z 4.02, hem 3.78.
- **`CafeInterior`** (6.5k tris, companion at the same pivot and yaw): the room, 22 x 11.3 m (x -11..11, y 0..11.3; C-0 unchanged), floor 0.04 m over the pavement (the door stays flush), ceiling at 4.6 with an amber cove, under the mass that starts at 4.7. Limestone floor in an ivory and seafoam checker (0.6 m cells); back wall with the **back-bar painting and the botanical mural** on flat quads (never on curved glass), the **counter** (petrol enamel front with fluting, limestone top, chrome edge, brass footrail, register, machine) with six coral-seat stools, a **window row of tables and chairs** 1.35 m inside the glass (clear of the door aisle) and a second row, **two petrol-velvet booths**, **opal pendants** on chrome rods, four potted plants. It also draws what no piece drew before: the **side and back walls of the ground storey** (the ground mass is drawn by the piece only; the generator's facade has `NoGround`), cream stone with the seafoam base course and belt, a clear window in each side wall (y 6.5 to 10.1) and the kitchen door at x 3 (block (-22.4, -26)).
- **Lit without a light**: every surface inside is an `emit` material at 0.45 to 0.85 (`DK_Cafe_*`, pendants 5, cove 3.2), so the room reads through the glass at any hour and under the awning's and the ceiling's shadow. Glint may swap them for real lights. Brass stays a hairline (sill, transom, head, rail, belt lines, bench caps); the stone is cream, the bands seafoam, the metal chrome.
- Exteriors only: it is dressing seen through glass, never entered; nothing in it is solid, walkable or sim data.

No existing material changes.
