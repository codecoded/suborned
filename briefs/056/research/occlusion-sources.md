# Occlusion research notes (brief 056 occlusion), 2026-10-24, Glyph

Web search for the named games returned little first-hand material: no developer talk or patch note was reachable for Desperados III, Shadow Tactics, Commandos: Origins, Satellite Reign, XCOM 2, Diablo IV, Hades, Frostpunk, Disco Elysium or Tactical Breach Wizards (GDC Vault's Shadow Tactics postmortem is behind a login). So each row below says what a source states and what is only my recollection of the game (marked **memory, unverified**). Nothing below changed the GDD.

## Memory, unverified (no source reached)

- Desperados III and Shadow Tactics: characters behind scenery drawn as a coloured silhouette; floors of a building you enter cut away or hidden. Believed, not confirmed.
- Diablo IV: wall pieces near the player fade; hidden characters keep an outline. Not confirmed.
- Hades: a fixed isometric angle with arenas built so nothing needs to fade (the level is the occlusion solution). Not confirmed; no camera talk found.
- Frostpunk: steep pitch, buildings low, rotation with Q/E or middle mouse (confirmed by a guide at https://twinfinite.net/guides/frostpunk-rotate-camera-how/); no fade, a known "peek round the building" nuisance.
- Tactical Breach Wizards: small hand-built rooms whose walls are modelled low or open to the camera. Not confirmed; no source found.

## Our own files read

- `unity/Assets/Suborned/Shaders/DecoKit/DK_Cutaway.hlsl` and `Runtime/ArchitectureFade.cs`: today's fade is a world-space disc on the ground (radius 0.8 of the half-height, 11.2 m at zoom 14) round the screen centre, plus 4 m discs at the cursor and target and 3.2 m clean discs at people; "column" mode ghosts everything above a ghosted part; ghost 0.2, 4x4 Bayer dither, 2 m feather. `Runtime/Play/HostTurn.cs` (FadePoints) lists the points.
- `briefs/044-overlay-style.md` reviews of 0.2.94 to 0.2.99: the grey slab, the dither column over the door, "yaw 210 reads, yaw 30 does not".
