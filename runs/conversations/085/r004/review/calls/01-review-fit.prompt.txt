# Brief 085, conversation scenes: Motive's review

Fable's scenes are proposals for Sky to read. You're checking fit with the rules, not taste. For each scene:
- Does anyone do, promise or know something the rules don't allow? (§04 approaches, temperament and knowledge; §07 routes, the partners rule, the driver's job.)
- Does a line imply a single right way to win this person over?
- Does a line state a number, deadline or power the rules don't give, or the wrong time for something the player times?
- Does a line point at something the sheet's "What the player sees" doesn't show, or have someone pay, trade or gain what the game doesn't do?
- Do `learned` and `opens` tell the truth about what the player can do next?

Report only real faults, each with the line and a fix in one sentence. Colour that changes no rule (a stamped card, a telephone) is fine. If a scene is clean, say nothing about it.

Read the street at a glance › The conversation scene Spec
When Listen, Ask, Pay, Help, Pressure, Call or Mislead lands, the camera cuts to a close shot of the two people and the whole conversation plays once, its outcome included, since the act has settled it. The mission clock pauses while it plays, since the player can't move. The scene changes nothing the engine records, so the same seed and orders give the same result whether it plays, is skipped or is turned off.
It is a cutaway, not play: a hard cut in and out (a 0.12 s dip), and back to exactly the view the player left (map or ground, the same agent). The framing is a profile two-shot of the two people: 3.6 m from the line between them (more when they stand apart), 1.9 m high, looking 8° down, a 35° field of view; failing that, the play camera's pitch at 7 m; failing that, for a pair more than 6 m apart, each speaker alone, cut with their bubble. It is never one person for the whole scene, and never over a shoulder, with a slow 8 % push-in and no letterbox bars. In a Listen the overheard pair fill the shot, with the Scout at its edge, turned away, then alone for the last line. It plays the same from the ground view as from the map: both people are set to idle, facing each other, feet planted where the engine has them, and anyone else in the shot keeps walking (view only, put back at the cut out); the low shots stand down the architecture cut and the silhouettes. One line at a time, low on the screen, the speaker's live face in a round inset beside it, held as long as it takes to read. A line may hold before or after it, or lean on one word, where its text says so; these are view only. A click or Space advances, Esc or Skip ends it. On screen stay only the line, its face and a bare Skip; the HUD steps away. What the scene learned and opened comes after the cut, in play, on the person, with New beside any action it opens. It doesn't play in a fight or at Alert 2 or more, if either person is down, gone or inside, while another scene is playing, or in freeze or target mode. The setting is Always, First time for each scene (the default), or Never (bubbles only).
