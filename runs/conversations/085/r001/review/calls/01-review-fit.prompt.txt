# Brief 085, conversation scenes: Motive's review

Fable's scenes are proposals for Sky to read. You're checking fit with the rules, not taste. For each scene:
- Does anyone do, promise or know something the rules don't allow? (§04 approaches, temperament and knowledge; §07 routes, the partners rule, the driver's job.)
- Does a line imply a single right way to win this person over?
- Does a line state a number, deadline or power the rules don't give?
- Do `learned` and `opens` tell the truth about what the player can do next?

Report only real faults, each with the line and a fix in one sentence. Colour that changes no rule (a stamped card, a telephone) is fine. If a scene is clean, say nothing about it.

Built The approach prices are proven in play; the Watch time is a starting value. Paying Inez plus turning the driver costs $110,000 against a $100,000 purse. Helping her plus turning the driver costs $80,000. So finding things out is what pays for a second route.
Turning is private. A conversation is private: asking, paying, helping and listening have no witnesses, so onlookers learn nothing, there is no witness Trail, no change in Standing, no incident and nothing passed along. The approach keeps its own price: a payment is still traceable money, and a Loyal person still reports an approach. Pressure is different: security see a threat at any Alert, and ordinary people from Alert 2 (§05); it is remembered and passed on. A conversation never counts as loitering (§05). Spec

§10 PRESENTATION › conversation scene
conversation scene [S]: when an agent arrives and begins Listen, Ask, Pay, Help, Pressure, Call or Mislead, the camera cuts to a close shot of the two people and the opening lines play (at most 3 beats: the arrival line, the person's first reply, or for Listen the overheard exchange's first lines; never the reply, the result or the read, which come at landing as bubbles, so the scene can't contradict the outcome). The mission clock pauses while it plays (the player can't move) and the act's seconds run after it, as priced; the scene changes nothing the engine records, so the same seed and orders give the same result with it, skipped or never played. A cutaway, not play: hard cut in and out (a 0.12 s dip), back to exactly the view the player left (map or ground, same agent); a profile two-shot of the two people (3.6 m from the line between them, more when apart, 1.9 m high, 8° down, 35° field), else the play camera's pitch at 7 m, else, for a pair more than 6 m apart, each speaker alone cut with the bubble; never one person for the whole scene, never over a shoulder; a slow 8 % push-in, 6 % letterbox bars. Played from the ground view as from the map: both people set to idle, facing, feet planted where the engine has them; the low shots stand down the architecture cut and the silhouettes. One bubble at a time, up to 3 s each; click or Space advances, Esc or Skip ends it. Kept on screen: the clock with its pause mark, the Alert pill, the bubbles, the Skip keycap. It doesn't play in a fight or at Alert ≥ 2, if either person is down, gone or inside, while another scene plays, or in freeze or target mode. Setting: Always · First time with each person (default) · Never (bubbles only).
