REASON: The order acknowledgements are useful and largely accurate. All variants meet the length limits, naming rules and punctuation requirements; keeping every item is correct. A few phrases obscure the action or sound written rather than spoken, and the family gives the Fixer little distinctive warmth.

  • USEFUL ack/pressure/fixer “Let me make their problem harder to ignore.”: This could mean drawing attention to their problem or helping address it. It does not clearly acknowledge using their trouble to compel help.
    Fix: Let me use their trouble to make them help.
  • PLAIN ack/watch/fixer “Let me take time to learn their movements.”: “Take time to learn their movements” sounds like a written task description rather than a spoken acknowledgement.
    Fix: Let me watch where they go.
  • PLAIN ack/end/fixer “We're done; anyone without a car escape will be captured.”: “Without a car escape” is an unnatural construction that makes the consequence harder to understand during the bubble.
    Fix: We're ending this; anyone who hasn't left by car gets captured.
  • VOICE “I'll put pressure on them through their trouble.”: Across the family, literal descriptions of orders dominate. They convey the mechanics but give the Fixer little of her warm, persuasive manner or kindness with steel underneath.
    Fix: Keep the clear actions, but give selected variants a reassuring or persuasive turn. For example: “I'll explain why helping us beats keeping their trouble.”
