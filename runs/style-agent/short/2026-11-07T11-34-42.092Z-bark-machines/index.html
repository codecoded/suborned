  • USEFUL camera-shot/scout “That lens is dead. It's blind.”: Both sentences say the camera cannot see; neither says it was shot out. That distinction explains what just happened.
    Fix: Replace with "That camera's been shot. It can't see a thing."

    Fix: Use "Camera shot out. No picture now."
  • VOICE loop-ends/tech “That loop is finished.”: This is a generic announcement interchangeable with the Scout's variants. It lacks the Tech's practical description of what stopped.
    Fix: Use "The camera's stopped repeating the old picture."
  • VOICE loop-ends/scout “The loop's over now.”: The detached status announcement could come unchanged from the Tech. It gives the Scout no recognisable conversational turn.
    Fix: Use "There goes our loop. The old picture's stopped."
  • VOICE loop-ends/scout “That's the loop done.”: This is another generic completion notice rather than the Scout's quick, conversational report to her cell.
    Fix: Use "Our loop's finished. No more old picture."
  • VOICE patrol-rerouted/tech “It took the false orders. It's leaving its sweep.”: This neutral narration could come unchanged from the Scout. It conveys the event but lacks the Tech's procedural, slightly dry voice.
    Fix: Use "False orders accepted. Off its usual round."
  • USEFUL patrol-stands-down/scout “The patrol's back on its round.”: It reports the resumed sweep but drops the event's useful distinction: the patrol has stopped responding to the alarm.
    Fix: Use "The patrol's done with the alarm. It's back on its round."
  • PLAIN patrol-stands-down/scout “The patrol's left the alarm”: An alarm is a signal, so saying the patrol has 'left' it is awkward shorthand for ending its response.
    Fix: Use "The patrol's stopped answering the alarm. It's sweeping again."
