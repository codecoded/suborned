The titles name who is coming, the shorts give the cause in a few street words, and every short fits its lozenge at its widest (the post short is the tightest at 283 of 296 px). I found three problems. First, the one rewritten line, police.cordon, still leans on the Alert number, which is a rule of the game rather than what happened. It also drops "the district's" and doesn't match its short's tense. Second, the unknown post variant's line uses "was missed", which can be misread. It also says "the police are coming" where its sibling lines say "police come", and it leaves out that police come to the post. Third, split's line says "whoever is left", which doesn't say it means the cell's agents. Everything else I would ship unchanged.

  • PLAIN police.cordon “The Alert reached 3, so the doors locked and police are closing the exits.”: It still states a game rule (Alert 3 sets off a lockdown) and restates the Alert number instead of saying what happened in the street. The rubric rules out anything that happens at an Alert, and the meter already shows the number. It also drops "the district's" from the tells, and its tense ("locked", "are closing") doesn't match the short ("the doors lock and exits close").
    Fix: Say the lockdown plainly and keep the tells' scope: "The district is in lockdown: the doors lock and police close its exits." (13 words, 71 characters)
  • PLAIN police.post.variants.unknown “The guard was missed at his post, so the police are coming.”: "Was missed" can be read as "someone missed him" or as "he didn't do something". It is not plain. The line also drops where police go (the empty post). "The police are coming" breaks the family's "police come" wording.
    Fix: "The guard wasn't at his post, so police come to check on it." This is still true when the cell hasn't watched his routine.
  • NAMES police.split “Popovic is gone, so police close in on whoever is left.”: The tells say police close in on whoever of the cell is left. "Whoever is left" could mean anyone in the street, so the player can't tell that this means their own agents who are still on the block.
    Fix: "Popovic is gone, so police close in on the agents still here." (12 words, 61 characters)
