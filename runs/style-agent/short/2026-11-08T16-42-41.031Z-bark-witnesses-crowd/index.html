REASON: The passer-by rows mostly hold the bar. They're short and human, people speaking for their own reasons, and none of them explains the game. "I'm late as it is." and "Somebody else will see to it." are good new takes. The score is held down by the guard's reaction row. All three of its lines are folksy bystander talk ("Good grief", "that is"), not his clipped officialese, and a passer-by could say any of them unchanged. That puts VOICE at 5. "This can't wait." is a bare line that anyone could say and adds nothing. The new report-begins lines are close, but "For the book" hints that he's logging a report, and "one" reads awkwardly. No line has numbers, names or AI words, and every line fits the bubble.

  • VOICE witness-reacts/guard/radio “Good grief. What's gone on here?”: This is a startled passer-by's exclamation. It isn't the guard's clipped voice about his post and its rules, and the crowd could say it unchanged.
    Fix: Use his voice as he reaches for the radio, e.g. "Hold it there. Nobody touches him." or "Man down by the post. Stand back, please."
  • VOICE witness-reacts/guard/radio “That's a coward's work, that is.”: The folksy "that is" tag belongs to a bystander, not to officialese. Any passer-by could say it.
    Fix: Give him the post's language, e.g. "Not on my door, you don't. Stand clear."
  • VOICE witness-reacts/guard/radio “And on my shift, too.”: It's closer to him, but it's still a grumble any worker could make. It doesn't sound like him taking charge of a scene.
    Fix: Try e.g. "Right. Everyone back from the door." It's clipped, it's an order, and it says nothing about the radio.
  • WORTH witness-reacts/crowd/report “This can't wait.”: It's empty and could follow any event. It tells the player nothing about who this person is or how they take what they've seen.
    Fix: Give a human detail with intent, e.g. "Oh, his poor face. Someone has to know." or "Stay there, love. I'm going for help."
  • VARIANTS witness-reacts/crowd/quiet “I'm keeping out of this.”: It's very close to the avoid row's "Don't look. Keep walking." Both say the same thing about staying out of it, so across the family the two rows blur together.
    Fix: Give quiet a different thought, e.g. "It's none of my business, is it?" or "Best not get involved with that sort."
  • NATURAL report-begins/guard/radio “For the book, desk. Keeping an eye on one out here.”: "For the book" announces that a record is being made, which is the report's rule half-said, and "one out here" is clumsy to say aloud.
    Fix: Try e.g. "Desk, there's somebody hanging about my door."
  • TRUE report-begins/guard/radio “Can't work out what they're doing, desk.”: This holds when someone is loitering, but it's weak when they run, because then he can see exactly what they're doing. Every variant has to be true in both cases.
    Fix: Use a line true of both, e.g. "Desk, door here. Got somebody I don't like the look of." Keep it different from the other variants.
