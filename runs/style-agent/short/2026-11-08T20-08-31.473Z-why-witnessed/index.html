REASON: Most of this family keeps today's wording, and most of it holds up: the seen, carry, dead, mark, missing and cut lines are plain and true to the engine, and at their widest fills they fit the 80-character line and the 296 px news box. The writer was right to drop "Killing confirmed" and "confirmed a killing", because "confirmed" is on the banned list. But the new confirmed wording brings its own problems. "Security" is not a glossary word. The new short is three characters longer than a short that already used most of the box, so it will probably be cut off. The line's "it was a killing" is vague. Other problems: alert.down's short calls a knocked-out living person a "body", so it reads almost like alert.dead's short. alert.carry.variants.vale's title uses the engine's word "escort". The two woken shorts use vague wording ("woke and told", "told when let go") and don't match "came round" in the line. alert.noise's short leaves out that the person was hidden, which is the cause. None of these blocks shipping, but several lines would need changes before I'd ship them, so the score is 6.

  • NAMES alert.confirmed “security calls it a killing”: "Security" is not in the glossary (the guard, the patrol, the police, robots). It is the book's term for the Alert response, not a word anyone in the street uses. "Calls it" also sounds like an opinion, not something found. At 27 characters it is also over the box's budget of about 24 characters (142.9 px). Today's 24-character short already filled 270.9 of 296 px with the widest place, so three more characters will probably be cut off.
    Fix: Name nobody, since the host doesn't say who checked: "the body was a killing" (22 characters). This also keeps it clearly apart from alert.dead's "dead body found".
  • NAMES alert.confirmed “Security finds a killing”: Same glossary problem as the short. It also uses the present tense, while every other title is a past event ("Takedown seen", "Found dead").
    Fix: "Checked: a killing", or "Found to be killed" (no checker named, past event, under 4 words).
  • PLAIN alert.confirmed “Security checked on {about} and found it was a killing.”: "Security" again, and "it" has nothing to refer to (the person? the scene?). The sentence also doesn't say what changed, which is that the person was killed, not knocked out or just missing.
    Fix: "On a closer look, {about} had been killed." This is true for every checker (robot, patrol, officer, guard) and every first report (knocked out, empty post, missing).
  • TRUE alert.down “knocked-out body found”: "Body" reads as dead, but this person is alive and knocked out. It also differs from alert.dead's "dead body found" by only its first word, and these two causes must read clearly apart at a glance. At 291.8 of 296 px it also leaves almost no room.
    Fix: "found knocked out" (17 characters): matches the glossary, says the person is alive, and leaves room in the box.
  • PLAIN alert.carry.variants.vale “Popovic's escort seen”: "Escort" is the engine's word for the Take's escort, not something the player has seen named. It also reads as Popovic's own bodyguard, not the Enforcer leading him away.
    Fix: "Popovic seen led away" (4 words, 21 characters), matching the short and the line.
  • VOICE alert.woken “someone woke and told”: "Told" has no object, so it's vague at a glance. "Woke" doesn't match "came round" in the item's own line. One thing should get one set of words. It also sits at 289.5 of 296 px.
    Fix: "someone came round and told" is too wide. Use "a victim came round" or "someone came round" (18 characters). The card's title already says a report raised the Alert.
  • PLAIN alert.woken.variants.vale “Popovic told when let go”: "Told when let go" is clumsy and unclear about what he told or to whom. It fills 295.3 of 296 px, a 0.7 px margin, so any font or place change will cut it off.
    Fix: "Popovic reported it" or "Popovic let go, reported" (about 19 characters): clearer, with room to spare.
  • USEFUL alert.noise “someone calling out”: The cause is that a hidden victim was heard, so the hiding place failed. "Someone calling out" could be any passer-by and hides what the player must fix next time. The title says it ("Hidden person heard"), but the short is what the lozenge, the meter and the debrief show.
    Fix: "a hidden person heard" (21 characters, fits the box).
