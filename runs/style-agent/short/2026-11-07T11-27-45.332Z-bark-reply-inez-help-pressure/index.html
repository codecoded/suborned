    Fix: Let her weigh the benefit: "Well, that's worth a favour. I'll help."

    Fix: Keep the thanks while showing that interest: "Sorted again. You're worth keeping around, thank you."

    Fix: Use warmer, self-interested gratitude: "Another favour? Thank you. I could get used to this."

    Fix: Let the thanks carry relief, such as: "That's a weight off. Thank you, truly."

    Fix: Connect the repeated care to her own loyalty: "Still looking after me. I'll stand by you, too."
  • VOICE reply/help/waitress/kept/loyal “That's another kindness you've done me. Thank you.”: This is general-purpose gratitude; neither Inez's quick warmth nor her loyalty distinguishes it.
    Fix: Give the thanks a personal assurance: "Kind again. Thank you. I'm still with you."
